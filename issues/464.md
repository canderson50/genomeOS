## Decisions for the owner

- [ ] **People figure note, science flag.** "Populations with the greatest genetic diversity / are
      the least represented in research." The figure's own source (GWAS Catalog, July 2021) has
      African-ancestry participants at 1.1%, but South Asian (0.8%) and Hispanic/Latino (0.08%) lower
      still. "are among the least represented" would be exact; it adds one word. The page carries the
      wording as dictated.
- [ ] **Hero film on Safari without AV1** (Macs before M3) gets the 1080p HEVC, 5,038 KB, over the
      4,000 KB film budget. Options: serve the 720p HEVC (2,226 KB) to desktop Safari, re-encode a
      1080p HEVC at 4 MB or less, or accept the exception.
- [ ] **Atlas button target:** the HbS map it previews (`/app/?entity=hbs-rs334&palette=genome`)
      or plain `/app/`.
- [ ] **Spelling:** marketing pages now use US English (hemoglobin, harmonize, modeled, programs).
      Docs prose keeps UK spellings. Harmonize the Docs too?
- [ ] **AC/AN in Projects' figures:** confirm that "people, not alleles" (R2-16) covers callouts and
      readouts only, not the data contract's field names.
- [ ] **Board names:** the site shows Discussion · Backlog (with Ready folded in) · In progress ·
      In review · Merge. The real board (`docs/board-conventions.md`) has Backlog · Ready ·
      In progress · In review · Blocked · Done · Not planned. Also, the board is titled
      "Genome OS Atlas", but the brand is "genomeOS". Rename the board?
- [ ] **The Merge wall** is smaller (11 blocks, was 23), so the crew can celebrate beside it in a
      248 px column. At 1024 the crew and wall still fill the column edge to edge. Please look at it.
- [ ] **Forced colours** on the orbit and the board keep the illustrations' own colours
      (`forced-color-adjust: none`), not CanvasText strokes. Please confirm.
- [ ] **Headline punctuation:** should Contact's "Or start from a template." keep its period?
- [ ] **Pipeline subtitle:** it reads "Machine learning", not "Bayesian machine learning", because
      the longer one crossed the wires on tablets and phones. A two-line subtitle in the kit would
      allow the longer one.
- [ ] **Board figures from 64rem to about 1,430 px** are drawn 71–100 px tall (spec: about 100), so
      the whole board fits on an iPad in landscape. Move the track breakpoint to about 72rem?
