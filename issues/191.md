## Body

## Scientific objective

The #189 benchmark needs the count distribution of the held-out survey, with draw-aligned
latent, sampling-design, cohort and fitted nugget components. Shared study effects must remain
shared across sites to make joint-site predictions interpretable. This extends the distinction
between latent-frequency and survey-frequency intervals established in #110.

## Reproduction / observed behavior

Inspected `genomeos/surfaces/fit.py` at branch base `8778d21` and the unchanged current predictive
path (commands: `rg -n 'predict_observation|cohort|beta_design' ...`,
`sed -n '330,490p' genomeos/surfaces/fit.py`, and `sed -n '641,810p' ...`).

- `SurfaceFit.predict_observation(lat, lon, an)` accepts no sampling design or cohort identity.
- Its latent draws are reference-design draws. The fitted non-reference design effects are not
  restored to match a non-reference survey.
- It draws a separate normal cohort offset for every `(draw, point)`. Two held-out sites from
  the same new study therefore cannot share one cohort offset. Marginal intervals alone cannot
  reveal the resulting joint-distribution mismatch.
- `FitConfig.nugget=True` adds a fitted observation-level term, but the predictive method does
  not restore a new realization of that term.
- The method returns replicated-count summaries, not the aligned parameters needed to calculate
  integrated count predictive probabilities exactly.

No production artifact or fit was changed as part of this report. This is a source-level
reproducer, not a claim that a new real-data calibration experiment has been run.

## Prior work / non-duplication

#127 is closed as completed. Its grouped-fold/reporting changes are present. Its last substantive
comment describes seen-cohort conditioning on the unmerged local `fix/127-fold-grouping` branch.
That branch was inspected read-only: it conditions seen cohorts, but still assigns independent
offsets to sites of the same new cohort and does not accept sampling-design metadata. Reuse its
rationale where applicable, without merging unrelated stored artifacts or changing legacy
benchmark results silently. The new buffered-cohort benchmark primarily requires genuinely
unseen cohorts; conditional-imputation tracks are separate.

## Engineering contract and acceptance evidence

Introduce a pure, public, typed draw contract for offline observation prediction; do not make
benchmark code import private PyMC helpers. Require actual survey design and cohort IDs, validate
draw alignment and known design levels, and explicitly refuse unsupported missing-reference
semantics rather than assuming the configured anchor was observed. Preserve latent-only output
as a distinct interface.

Analytical synthetic tests should cover design offsets, identical shared new-cohort effects at
multiple sites, distinct new cohorts, fitted/omitted cohort and nugget terms, unsupported designs,
and row-order determinism. A small real fitter integration test checks posterior draw alignment.
Score with #189's exact count scorer. Preserve convergence criteria and report any changed model
or prediction convention as a new research version. Subsequent empirical comparison remains
nonpublication until the qualified-input/external-validation gates pass.

This is approved research-program work under #189 (WP1 current-model integration / WP2
observation-aware inference), not authorization to relabel existing artifacts or publish a new
surface. Design §§4–5, 7.1, 8, 12 apply. Spatial-statistics expert review should assess the
observation target, effect identifiability and joint prediction semantics.
