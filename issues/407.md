## Body

Follow-up from the Part A final review (#404, branch `feat/atlas-mobile-sheets`).

Part A now handles short explorers (at most 52rem wide and under 34rem tall: landscape phones and zoomed desktops) as amended in the mobile sheets design §A.1.5–§A.1.7 under R30. The docked legend and credits no longer cap the sheets. They hide while a sheet is above peek. A sheet whose cap leaves less than 96 px of body covers the top chrome at full. A panel peeks at its handle row alone. At peek the legend strip gives way when it would reach the top chrome. The cases below are still open.

## 1. Credit block under the top chrome at peek on the shortest explorers

At peek the Cesium credits and the data credit stay docked above the sheet. They clear the top chrome only when

`explorerHeight ≥ topChromeBottom + credit block + peek`

That is about 172 px in landscape, 224 px with the warning banner, and 192 px at 320 px wide, where the Cesium credits wrap to three lines.

Measured in Chromium at peek:

- **320×256 (1280×1024 at 400% zoom).** The explorer is 170 px. The credits span y 48–99 px against a top-slot bottom of 70 px. The top slot (z-index 4) paints over part of the attribution.
- **740×300 with the warning banner.** The explorer is 214 px. The credits span y 112–143 px against a top-slot bottom of 122 px.

The sheets themselves are fine in both cases. Full shows at least a 104 px body.

The fix needs a ruling. Options:

- Record a minimum supported explorer height in the spec.
- Move the credit block into the peek row on such explorers.
- Collapse the warning banner to one line.

The test `zoomed desktop › the controls sheet opens…` skips the peek clause at 320×256 and cites this issue.

## 2. A focused InfoTip can cover its own trigger on viewports too short for its text

`InfoTip` places its tooltip below the trigger, otherwise above it, otherwise at `top: 12px`. The last case overlaps the trigger. This was seen at 320×256 for "About map selection" in the controls sheet's peek row. It predates Part A and applies on any viewport too short for the text. A cap with scrolling on the larger side would fix it.

## 3. `/docs/` is 358 px wide at 320 px

The Starlight header measures 358 px at 320 and 344 px widths, so `/docs/` scrolls horizontally there. The other `SiteLayout` routes now fit, because the `body { min-width: 20rem }` floor was dropped. The new narrow-phone tests leave `/docs/` out for this reason. This predates Part A.
