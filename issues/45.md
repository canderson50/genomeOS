### bschilder, 2026-09-16T01:29:55Z (https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/45#issuecomment-5690651439)

Canonical 2026-09-16 acceptance run completed and **fails this gate** after correcting the population reference year in #300 / PR #301.

Frozen run:

- 994 Piel-comparable HbS surveys, 385 cohorts, 11,359,130 alleles
- beta-binomial sparse GP; 150 inducing cells; 4 × 1,200 retained draws after 1,200 warmup; seed 42
- convergence: max R-hat 1.0059, min ESS 767.6, zero divergences
- 500 coherent posterior rows over 77,844 populated H3 res-4 cells
- WorldPop 2020 used only for within-country weights; each represented country rescaled to the Piel 2010 national population before applying Piel's birth rate

Canonical supported-only result:

| Metric | Countries | Point inside Piel IQR | Interval overlap | Model global median (IQR) | Piel global median (IQR) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| HbSS | 155/191 | 29.3% | 46.6% | 253,080 (231,469–275,293) | 312,000 (294,000–330,000) |
| HbAS | 155/191 | 28.8% | 40.3% | 6,471,133 (6,229,413–6,707,077) | 5,476,000 (5,291,000–5,679,000) |

The fit learns broad geography (country-rank Spearman 0.927 for HbSS, 0.826 for HbAS), and major endemic-country HbAS ratios are close after year alignment: Nigeria 1.08×, DRC 0.98×, Tanzania 0.96×, Cameroon 1.03×, Ghana 1.08×, Kenya 1.00×.

The remaining error has opposite components. A ~1% global intercept leaves a diffuse non-endemic frequency floor, producing excessive HbAS (for example China 185,244 vs Piel 43), while flattened endemic peaks depress HbSS (India 0.65×, Tanzania 0.61×, Sudan 0.19×). A global multiplier cannot fix both.

Recommended next bounded comparison: current stationary GP versus one explicitly low-background/local-departure model, using the same observations, seed, posterior budget, denominator, national gate, and held-out survey-count score. A hurdle model (presence × conditional positive frequency) or a nonstationary mean is the leading formulation. No malaria/MAP environmental raster gets priority without a named mechanism and evidence that it adds information beyond simpler geography/climate baselines.
