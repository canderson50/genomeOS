## Body

## Scientific objective

Evaluate exact posterior-predictive count intervals at the million-scale denominators already admitted by the global AF benchmark without spending most of the run summing irrelevant support far from the predictive mass. The current exact quantile search initializes every level with `[-1, AN]`; its first midpoint is therefore near `AN / 2` even for rare alleles, forcing exact beta-binomial CDF work over hundreds of thousands or millions of counts.

This issue advances #189 and follows #314. It is source-neutral: the performance contract applies to every admitted count dataset and does not give the current HbS development source or MAP-published datasets special priority.

## Measurable output and acceptance evidence

- `CountPredictive.quantiles` returns the same exact left-continuous quantiles as the existing full-support binary search for binomial and beta-binomial mixtures.
- Initial search brackets use analytic posterior-predictive moments and a conservative one-sided probability bound, or another derivation with an equally explicit correctness argument; every bracket is verified by the exact CDF before binary search.
- A deterministic test with million-scale `AN` proves that the first non-boundary CDF probes avoid the `AN / 2` region for a rare-allele predictive distribution.
- Boundary means, `p=1`, small denominators, high overdispersion, and mixtures whose moment bracket reaches the support edge retain exact behavior.
- No support-sized allocation is introduced. CuPy continues to batch observations, draws, and bounded support chunks.
- Focused tests, full pytest, smoke, Ruff, contract, module-size, and privacy gates pass.

## Engineering component and public interface

Change the offline quantile-search implementation behind `genomeos.validation.predictive.CountPredictive.quantiles`. Keep its public signature, deterministic behavior, exact CDF semantics, and `cdf_backend` contract unchanged. Moment calculation belongs in the predictive distribution layer; the SciPy and CuPy CDF backends remain numerical evaluators rather than policy owners.

## Assumptions, refusal conditions, and downstream consumers

- Exact final quantiles remain required. A normal approximation, posterior sample quantile, or unverified heuristic bracket is ineligible as the returned answer.
- Invalid parameters and arithmetic outside the validated numerical domain still fail loudly.
- Runtime may still scale with the support actually needed for an exact tail sum; the objective is to stop querying obviously irrelevant halves of the full support.
- `predictive_diagnostics`, the five-fold current-GP benchmark, B0/B0H comparisons, and later model-selection reports are downstream consumers.

## Expert review

A numerical/statistical reviewer should check the mixture-variance derivation, the discrete form of the one-sided bound, bracket verification, and equivalence evidence against exhaustive small-support quantiles.
