## Body

## TL;DR

Our variant lookup table records who worked out each entry, but has nowhere to record who checked it afterwards. Right now that name is being kept in a free-text notes field. That matters because the whole point of having a checker is that it is a different person or system from whoever did the original work — and if the name lives in free text, nothing can actually verify that it is different. This adds a proper column for it.

---

## What

`VARIANT_NORMALIZATION_SCHEMA` has `reviewed_by` (who resolved the row) and `verification_status` (`verified` / `pending`), but **no `verified_by`**. When #270 performed the first independent verification under the #242 policy, the verifier had to record its identity in the `notes` free-text column.

That is a stopgap and should not stand. The whole force of the extractor-cannot-be-its-own-verifier rule is that the two identities are *comparable* — you must be able to assert `verified_by != reviewed_by` mechanically. In free text you cannot: nothing can check it, nothing can parse it, and a row could claim verification by the same party that resolved it with no gate noticing.

## Why it matters more now than it did yesterday

#242 was decided today: an agent may verify, provided the verification rests on links a human can follow. That decision makes agent verification routine rather than exceptional, which makes the identity of the verifier load-bearing rather than incidental. It is the field that distinguishes a real independent check from a rubber stamp.

## Suggested change

Add `verified_by` to `VARIANT_NORMALIZATION_SCHEMA`, formatted as `reviewed_by` already is (`human:<slug>`, `agent:<provider>:<model>`, `import:<name>@<40-hex>`):

- Required when `verification_status == "verified"`; blank when `pending`.
- **Must differ from `reviewed_by`** — enforced in `validate_rows`, alongside the other cross-field invariants. This is the check the column exists for.
- Identity rows are exempt from verification entirely (see the exemption in `normalized_identity`), so they carry neither.
- Migrate #270's three verified rows out of `notes` into the new column.

Consider at the same time whether a `verified_at` is wanted. Probably yes, for the same reason `resolved_at` exists: a verification against a live resource is only meaningful relative to when it happened, and dbSNP builds move.

Needs `python scripts/freeze_contract.py` and a committed `contract/` diff.

## Acceptance evidence

- A `verified` row without `verified_by` is refused.
- A row where `verified_by == reviewed_by` is refused, with a message naming the rule.
- A `pending` row carrying a `verified_by` is refused.
- #270's rows carry their verifier in the column, and `notes` no longer does.
- Frozen contract regenerated and committed.
