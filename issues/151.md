## Body

## Scientific objective

Demonstrate that independently curated HBB rs334 publication measurements make a lossless,
non-duplicating round trip through the literature evidence ledgers, P0 resolution, and P1 before
they are used by HbS parity. This advances #45; it does not claim that parity is complete.

## Measurable output and acceptance evidence

- Publish a versioned PubMed/search manifest with every candidate and an explicit screening
  decision.
- Curate a representative, predeclared set of original rs334 population measurements with all 19
  field-evidence decisions, exact allele orientation, called AC/AN, source location, cohort
  identity, ascertainment, dates, independent verification, and checked reuse terms.
- Resolve every population through a reviewed `literature` P0 alias; copy P0 coordinates and
  uncertainty radii exactly. No publication or country-centroid fallback.
- Compare promoted rows against the corresponding MAP/Piel survey records using source/study and
  population identities. Report exact matches, explained transformations, duplicates, and
  unresolved mismatches; never count one recruited cohort twice because it appears in both paths.
- Run the full P1 build and feed the reconciled input to #45. #45 closes only after its existing
  national AS/SS point-estimate, interval-overlap, and global-total thresholds pass.

## Engineering interface

Use `LITERATURE_EVIDENCE_SCHEMA`, `LITERATURE_FIELD_EVIDENCE_SCHEMA`,
`publications.load(...)`, and `OBSERVATIONS_SCHEMA.source_record_id` from #149. Add corpus fixtures
and a compact round-trip/reconciliation report; do not add a new generic parser or a promotion
override.

## Assumptions and refusal conditions

- rs334 counted-allele/strand/build ambiguity refuses promotion.
- Rounded frequencies and interval counts remain evidence but do not enter exact-count P1.
- Missing cohort or sample identity refuses rows whose independence cannot be established.
- MAP and publication copies of the same cohort are reconciliation matches, not independent
  observations.
- Missing P0 geography/radius, ascertainment, independent verification, or reuse checks refuses.

## Expert review

Population-genetics review should check rs334 orientation, genotype-to-allele calculations, study
reuse/cohort identity, and the reconciliation against the Piel/MAP corpus.

Depends on #149. Feeds #45.

### manpreetbola, 2026-10-09T00:04:25Z (https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/151#issuecomment-6071481091)

**Progress update, and a refusal.** Following my scoping comment I built the reconciliation half of this issue outside the repository. Two results and one blocker, all checkable.

**1. Finding — the MAP side reconciles cleanly.** I fetched the live layer with this repository's own tool (`scripts/fetch_map_hbs.py`: 402,028 bytes, 1,287 data rows) and reconciled it against the committed artifact (`hbs-rs334.observations.json`, 1,071 observations, `map-2026-08`).

The committed `an` and `ac` are derived, not columns. The rule, pinned to the artifact:

```
an = 2 * (AA + AS + SS)      blank hbaa recovered as sample_size - hbas - hbss
ac = AS + 2 * SS             blank hbss read as zero
```

That reproduces the committed payload on **1,071 of 1,071 records with zero mismatches**. The tempting `an = 2 * sample_size` is **wrong for 207 of those 1,071** — that error is now impossible to repeat silently.

Layer coverage: **all 1,071 artifact ids are present in the live layer**, which has **216 more**. Of those 216: 150 derive under the rule, 52 are all-wild-type (`ac` 0, `an` = 2 × `sample_size`), and 66 carry **no counts at all** — `hbaa` and `sample_size` both blank, location and citation only. I name every id in each class in the report.

Cohort identity holds: 419 distinct `study_id` values, 143 contributing more than one observation (max 33), and **cohorts are distinct within every study**. So the 143 are repeated measurements on distinct cohorts, not one recruited cohort entered twice.

One refinement I want to flag rather than bury: a blank `hbas` reads as **zero**, but only where `hbaa` is present or recoverable from `sample_size`. Treating a blank as zero asserts the paper reported no carriers, which is a real claim. The committed artifact contains no blank-`hbas` row, so this cannot be checked against it — it is a refinement, it is applied only under that condition, and every row it recovers is named. A row with `hbaa` **and** `sample_size` both blank is refused, not zero-filled.

**2. Contradiction — the publication path and the MAP path are disjoint for Salih 2010.** Salih 2010 (`pmid:20128890`) is `included` in the corpus on two search decisions, with two measurement rows: Hausa and Massalit. Its Table 1 N row reconciles exactly against those rows — Hausa AA 118 + AS 91 + SS 15 = 224 → `an` 448, `ac` 121; Massalit AA 163 + AS 68 + SS 15 = 246 → `an` 492, `ac` 98.

But **neither population appears anywhere in the 1,071-record MAP artifact.** `Salih`, `20128890` and `balancing selection` match zero records; the 62 Sudan rows resolve to ten other studies, and the single label hit is refused as a homonym (id 12, Adamson 1951, Nigeria). So there is currently **no double-counted cohort between the two paths** — which is the outcome the issue asks for, not a defect.

**3. The approach I reproduced.** `ac_lower`/`ac_upper` raw values are keyed by the ALT allele first and hold **genotypes, not allele counts**: `{"AA":15,"TA":91,"TT":118}` for Hausa. Read against counted allele A (ALT, HbS), those are ALT/ALT = HbS/HbS = SS, ALT/REF = AS, REF/REF = AA — so 15/91/118 are the paper's SS/AS/AA exactly, and their sum (224) is the paper's N. I flag this only because the keying reads as an inconsistency in the opposite direction and cost me a false alarm.

**4. What I did not do, and will not.** I did not supply a `verified_by`. Both rows carry `verification_status: pending` with `verified_by` and `verified_at` empty, and this issue requires independent verification (`verified_by != extracted_by`). The extraction was automated, so the second reviewer has to be a person. Writing a name there would fabricate the one thing this foundation exists to prevent, so the worksheet carries `verified_by: null` and `promotable: false` on purpose.

**5. What still blocks promotion.**

- **P0 geography is unresolved and not resolvable from the paper.** It gives village names and relative positions only — Koka is about 40 km east of Um-Salala, Um-Salala is on the eastern bank of the River Rahad about 400 km south-east of Khartoum. No coordinates, no radius, no map with a scale. The empty `lat`/`lon` on both rows **is** the blocker; the issue refuses a country-centroid fallback, so I have left them empty.
- **Seven fields proposed but not human-reviewed**: `sample_id`, `cohort_id`, `assay`, `sampling_design`, `disease_ascertainment_excluded`, `date_lower`, `date_upper`. I left `disease_ascertainment_excluded` **explicitly unresolved** — the frequency sample is a population sample (470 individuals, non-first-degree relatives), but the same surveys collected clinical and parasitological malaria data, so whether disease ascertainment is excluded is a curatorial call and not mine.
- `date_lower`/`date_upper` carry the paper's own contradiction: 1994–2006 in Study design, 1994–2005 in Results and Figure 1, and the actual sampling date is never stated.
- `reported_frequency` stays `not_reported`: the paper publishes HbS 0.25 (Hausa) and 0.20 (Massalit), but for all 546 screened individuals, not for the 470-independent subset the corpus counts. Attaching those figures to the 224/246 denominators would be a mismatch.
- **29 of 44 search candidates remain `pending`** on this corpus (2 included, 13 excluded). The issue asks for an explicit screening decision on every candidate.
