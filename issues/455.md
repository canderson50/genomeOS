## Body

## Symptom

`tests/atlas-mobile.spec.ts` › "the Stadia credit lightbox closes from its own close button" fails intermittently in CI on the mobile-chromium project, at 360×780 and 390×844. It usually passes on retry, so the run is reported as flaky rather than failed.

```
Error: Timeout 5000ms exceeded while waiting on the predicate
  > 1978 |         .toBe(true);
```

The poll checks that `.cesium-credit-lightbox-close` is the topmost element at its own centre (`document.elementFromPoint`). For more than 5 s, something else sits on top of the close button.

## Where it shows up

Seen on main (run 38494996669, a04899f), and on PR branches based on 5cd536c both before and after #447. So it predates the perf PRs.

The first branches showing it are based on the GA4 merge (#429, 5cd536c). That merge added a cookie control and a consent banner, so one hypothesis is a GA4 element overlapping the lightbox's close button at narrow phone widths. This needs confirming.

## Next

Reproduce locally with `--repeat-each` at those viewports. Find what covers the close button (`elementFromPoint` at failure), and fix the product cause, as #434 did for #430, rather than adding retries or longer waits.
