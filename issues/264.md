## Body

## Problem and scientific consequence

Hollenbach et al. (2012), *KIR gene content variation in the HGDP-CEPH populations*, DOI [10.1007/s00251-012-0629-x](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00251-012-0629-x), has conflicting HGDP geographic assignments in Table 1. They appear in the actual publisher PDF (page 4, printed 722) and the official Europe PMC XML, so this is not just an HTML or search-extraction problem.

Before using this table as geographic evidence in genomeOS, resolve its source/release and population mapping. Otherwise published-looking metadata could put inferred population frequencies at an unsupported location, violating the no-invented-geography and explicit-provenance invariants (Atlas §§6,12; #189 WP0). This is a source-qualification issue, not evidence that a current genomeOS map or the paper's numerical conclusions are wrong.

## Located evidence

Comparison with the [CEPH access document dated 2021-03-11, PDF page 4](https://cephb.fr/data/HGDP-CEPH-access-conditions-2021-03-11-web.pdf):

| Article Table 1 label | Article latitude; longitude | Provider label | Provider coordinates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Biaka Pygmies | `30–31`; `66–67 E` (latitude hemisphere absent) | Biaka Pygmy relatives | `4N, 17E` |
| Bedouin | `4 N`; `17 E` | Bedouin relatives | `31N, 35E` |
| Barusho | `31 N`; `35 E` | Burusho — spelling correspondence remains unreviewed | `36-37N, 73-75E` |
| Brahui | `36–37 N`; `73–75 E` | Brahui | `30-31N, 66-67E` |
| Han | `36–39 N`; `108–120 E` | Han | `26-39N, 108-120E` |

The article's Bedouin point repeats the provider Biaka point; Barusho repeats its Bedouin point; Brahui repeats its Burusho range. These require source resolution, not a silent row swap. Similar names here do not certify canonical aliases or identical sample subsets.

The older [Rosenberg 2011 author PDF, pages 2–3, Figure 1 caption and Table 1](https://web.stanford.edu/group/rosenberglab/papers/Rosenberg 2011-HumBiol.pdf) also places Biaka at 4,17, Bedouin at 31,35, and the Brahui/Burusho/Han analytical points within the corresponding provider ranges. Its caption explicitly defines derived range centroids; these are not recruitment locations or uncertainty radii.

Controls/cautions: Yoruba and Balochi ranges agree across the article/provider tables. Pathan's article cells instead contain latitude `32–35 N 69` and longitude `72 E`. Mongola agrees between the 2011 and 2012 papers at 45N,111E but differs from the 2021 provider range 48–49N,118–120E. Replacing all older coordinates with the newest table would therefore also require qualification.

Their visible Biaka group is an AFND Bagandu record at 5N,15E, not the suspect table coordinates; its identity with this HGDP sample is not established. This bounded check does not show current-map contamination or certify the existing AFND geography.
- Checked publisher/PDF/XML terms: the publisher names CC BY 2.0. No genotype table, source PDF or derived scientific artifact is uploaded by this issue.
- Searched open and closed issues and bounded correction/erratum queries; no duplicate or correction was located. This is not proof that no correction exists.
