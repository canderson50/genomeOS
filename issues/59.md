## Body

Hatched overlay distinguishing `unknown` (no data in range) from `prior_dominated` (data in range, posterior mostly prior). Hiding it requires an explicit click. This is the single most important honesty feature in the product — and for the 'where is the world's genomic data missing' use case, it *is* the product.

### bschilder, 2026-08-27T08:44:38Z (https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/59#issuecomment-5436594355)

Evidence for making this on-by-default rather than optional, from the held-out validation run in
#132 (numbers on #109).

On the MAP HbS corpus, 95% predictive interval coverage:

| Fold strategy | What it tests | 95% coverage (nominal 0.95) |
|---|---|---|
| grouped | interpolation, near neighbours in training | **0.96** |
| spatial | extrapolation, whole regions held out | **0.74** (one fold 0.478) |

MAE tells the same story more quietly: the surface beats a global constant by 29% when
interpolating and by **3%** when extrapolating.

So the surface is not uniformly trustworthy, and the failure is specifically in the places the
support mask is designed to flag. A reader of a rendered map cannot see MAE; they see a credible
interval, and in unsampled regions that interval covers 74% of the time while claiming 95%.

That makes the mask a correctness feature rather than a presentation preference, which is an
argument for it not being switchable off.
