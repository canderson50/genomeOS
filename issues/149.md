## Body

## Decision

Yes: publications are a first-class P1 source **after** their measurements pass a separate,
auditable evidence ledger and deterministic promotion gate. This issue now tracks the reusable
foundation being merged, not the claim that an LLM or generic parser can turn papers directly into
observations.

## Foundation delivered by this issue

- Frozen `literature_searches`, `literature_evidence`, and `literature_field_evidence` contracts.
  Every evidence record has exactly 19 per-field decisions distinguishing `reported`, allowlisted
  `derived`, `not_reported`, `ambiguous`, and `not_reviewed`.
- A detailed human/agent data dictionary with exact headers, valid/unresolved/derived fixtures, and
  prohibited examples (`docs/literature-evidence-curation.md`).
- `publications.load(...)`, which recomputes variant normalization, exact-count, independent-
  verification, checked-reuse, population, and duplicate gates. It has no caller eligibility,
  force, radius, ascertainment, or fallback option.
- Exact P0 integration: a verbatim publication label resolves through one `literature` alias; P1
  receives the registry row's latitude, longitude, and uncertainty radius unchanged.
- Required globally unique `source_record_id` on every allele-count P1 row, including existing
  gnomAD, MAP, and AFND adapters, so compact observations join back to stable evidence records.
- Offline build composition and a pure PubMed ESearch manifest builder. Network I/O and discovery
  remain outside science modules; every fetched candidate starts `pending`.
- Agent safeguards: automated/LLM output is always `automated_proposal`/`pending`, cannot verify
  itself, cannot turn uncertainty into plausible metadata, and treats refusal as a valid output.
- Reuse policy: no named licence is not itself a restriction after every relevant surface has been
  checked and logged (`no_restriction_found`). Unperformed checks remain `not_checked`; any explicit
  restriction wins.

## What this resolves

The reusable publication foundation is now complete: discovery manifests, record- and field-level evidence ledgers, stable identities, deterministic validation, exact P0 geography resolution, refusal reporting, and promotion into P1 only for independently verified exact counts. This also resolves the original concern that an LLM could fill missing scientific fields with plausible values: missing coordinates, uncertainty radii, cohort/ascertainment facts, denominators, locators, or verification evidence remain unresolved and cannot be promoted.

The reuse rule is also settled for this path: absence of a named licence is not itself a blocker after every contributing source surface has been checked and recorded with no explicit restriction. An unchecked source still blocks promotion, and any explicit restriction wins.

## How to use the foundation

1. Record every reproducible literature/database search in `literature_searches`; every returned candidate begins `pending`.
2. Transcribe one independently sampled population measurement per `literature_evidence` row.
3. Supply exactly 19 `literature_field_evidence` decisions per record, anchored to the source location actually checked.
4. Use only the documented allowlisted derivations. The validator recomputes them; an agent-provided result is not trusted.
5. Resolve the verbatim population label through an exact `literature` P0 alias. Coordinates and `uncertainty_radius_km` come only from that registry row.
6. Have a different reviewer verify the original source. Automated proposals cannot verify themselves.
7. Treat refusal as useful output. Frequency-only, interval-count, ambiguous, or scientifically incomplete rows remain in the evidence corpus but do not become P1 measurements.

The copyable headers, field meanings, correct examples, and prohibited shortcuts are in `docs/literature-evidence-curation.md`.

## Recommended next steps

1. **HBB first:** complete #151, including rs334 publication de-duplication against MAP/Piel. This is the shortest path from the new adapter to the still-open HbS parity gate in #45.
2. **G6PD second:** complete #150 with explicit X-linked denominator handling and strict separation of genotype, enzyme phenotype, and deficiency prevalence. This feeds #46.

4. **Scale discovery, not trust:** expand PubMed queries, non-English literature, national frequency reports, and locus-specific databases through versioned manifests.
