## Body

## TL;DR

Exact GPU scoring now gives correct answers for the spatial-activity campaign, but it still takes longer than fitting the model. Three registered synthetic tasks spent most of their 12–24 minute wall time in predictive scoring after sampling had finished. Increase the amount of exact CDF work performed per GPU batch while preserving every numerical and memory-safety guarantee.

## Scientific objective and claim

Make the registered 720-task synthetic activity campaign operational without changing its count law, posterior draws, quantiles, diagnostics, or decision gates. This is source-neutral numerical infrastructure; it does not change any biological model or permit a real-data fit.

## Measurable output and acceptance evidence

- Record an immutable before/after profile on the same A100, source revision, pinned environment, 500-draw activity mixture, 232 held-out rows, and the registered denominator range.
- Reduce complete `predictive_diagnostics` wall time materially, including all seven count quantiles, interval coverage, PIT, and log score.
- Preserve exact integer quantile endpoints, coverage flags, interval widths, seeded samples, and existing CPU/CuPy probability tolerances.
- Keep temporary device memory explicitly bounded and adaptive to current free memory. The implementation must reduce its plan or refuse when capacity is insufficient.
- Re-run an exact registered task artifact through the public campaign adapter and require byte-equivalent scientific diagnostics apart from allowed floating tolerances and provenance fields.
- Pass focused tests, full pytest, smoke, Ruff, contract, module-size, privacy, and actual-device CUDA tests.

## Current evidence

The existing planner caps batches at 8 query rows × 256 posterior draws × 2,048 support values with at most 512 MiB budgeted. On an A100 using JAX's default allocation, exact scoring remains GPU-active at roughly 82–91% utilization but dominates end-to-end task time. The earlier hardware profile measured about 176 MiB of additional device use for the current plan, suggesting room for a larger adaptive row batch on high-memory devices; that must be demonstrated rather than assumed.

## Engineering component and public interface

The likely boundary is `genomeos.validation.predictive_cupy._cdf_chunk_plan` and the shared `CuPyCDF` / `ActivityCuPyCDF` exact-tail batching path. Keep `CountPredictive`, `ActivityCountPredictive`, `cdf_backend="cupy"`, returned arrays, and all failure semantics unchanged. Prefer larger vectorized operations chosen from measured free memory over more Python loops.

## Assumptions, refusals, and downstream consumers

- Exact finite-support float64 semantics remain fixed. No approximation, reduced draw count, silent CPU fallback, or weakened parity tolerance is eligible.
- Do not tune solely for an 80 GiB A100; test the downshift/refusal path for constrained memory.
- Do not launch the 720-task campaign until the optimization is measured on the registered activity workflow and merged.
- The spatial-activity campaign, count-predictive diagnostics, and other CuPy-backed model comparisons are downstream consumers.

## Expert review

A numerical/GPU reviewer should check peak-array accounting, synchronization in the timing harness, parity at row/draw/support boundaries, and whether larger row batches actually reduce dispatch overhead for the registered activity-mixture query shape.
