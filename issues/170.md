## Body

## Product claim

Measured observations must remain visually distinct from—and never be clipped by—the inferred surface. The current `hemisphere` option is a full circular sphere sprite; it should read as a surface-mounted stud whose base follows the local globe/surface orientation.

## Measurable output and acceptance evidence

- Circles, pins, and studs render above the inferred surface with statistical elevation both off and on.
- The globe still occludes markers on the far hemisphere; fixing surface overlap must not reintroduce through-Earth markers.
- The `Hemispheres` presentation is renamed `Studs` while the internal/shareable `hemisphere` value remains backward compatible.
- Stud artwork has a curved dome and elliptical base rather than a full circular sphere silhouette.
- Focused scene tests assert marker surface clearance/eye offset, local-normal alignment, label compatibility, and opacity behavior.
- Desktop visual evidence covers a close oblique view where the old clipping was visible.

## Engineering interface

Fix the observation rendering boundary in `website/src/atlas/scene/observation-layer.ts` and its presentation label in `ObservationControls`. Preserve the existing observation artifact and URL-state contracts.

## Assumptions, refusals, and consumers

Observation coordinates, sizes, colors, radii, and provenance do not change. Do not disable globe depth testing globally or expose far-side observations. Consumers are Atlas users comparing measured observations with modeled surfaces.
