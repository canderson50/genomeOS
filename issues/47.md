## Body

CFTR F508del, SMN1, HEXA, β-thalassaemia — conditions where population screening has measured carrier frequency directly. Our ascertainment-corrected estimates from healthy-panel data must reproduce screening-derived truth.

**This is the only test that validates §7.1.** Golden tests 1 and 2 pass whether or not `β_design` is right, because the MAP surveys were already well ascertained.

## 1. The target should be F508del-specific counts, not panel carrier totals

The curated variant is F508del alone (`biallelic_allele_count`), but most screening programmes report *CF* carrier frequency across a mutation panel. That total depends on the panel. In a North American referral population ([*Genet Med*, doi:10.1038/gim.2013.188](https://www.nature.com/articles/gim2013188)), 1,601,308 people tested on a 32-mutation panel and 109,830 on a 69-mutation panel, and F508del's share of detected carriers fell from 68.7% to 60.5% as the panel grew. Every panel includes F508del, so F508del-specific counts are comparable across programmes and panel totals are not. Comparing a single-variant surface against a panel total would build the panel into the error.

## 2. The truth sets have their own ascertainment

None of these are `population_random`, and the differences matter:

- **Reproductive carrier screening is self-selected.** In Victoria ([Archibald et al. 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/gim2017134)), 12,000 individuals were tested through GP, obstetric, fertility and genetics settings; 96.92% were female, and about 88% of the 610 carriers found across CF, fragile X and SMA had no family history, so roughly 12% did. Family-history and cascade tests enrich carriers and need excluding or modelling.
- **The same programme can differ sharply by referral route.** In northeastern Italy ([Castellani et al. 2009](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/185072)), the eastern region ran 87,025 tests and found 3,650 carriers (4.2%), while the western region ran 2,559 and found 314 (12.3%), 2.9× higher. I have not established why. Whatever the cause, each truth row needs its testing route documented before it can be trusted.
- **IRT-first newborn screening is not a carrier census.** Only infants with raised IRT are genotyped, so the carriers found are ascertained. Unselected newborn genotyping is different: a north Indian cord-blood study ([Kapoor et al., *J Cyst Fibros*](https://www.cysticfibrosisjournal.com/article/S1569-1993(05)00131-1/fulltext)) found 4 F508del carriers in 955 newborns, 1 in 238.

## 4. Scope: can this test validate §7.1 for these conditions?

§8 calls this "the **only** test that validates §7.1". But all four named conditions are autosomal recessive, and §7.1a's own arithmetic says recessive depletion in a healthy-reference panel is O(p): "~2% relative error on carrier frequency" at p = 0.01, "roughly an order of magnitude below the posterior width".

At F508del's non-Finnish European allele frequency (1.238%, gnomAD via [ClinVar](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/RCV000007523/)), a reference population of 100 genomes expects about 2.5 copies, a Poisson relative SE near 64%. Resolving a 2% relative effect at all needs about 2,500 observed copies, roughly 200,000 alleles (100,000 genomes), from counting error alone and before spatial uncertainty.

So for these four, the test can show that panel-based surfaces agree with regional screening truth, and that the near-zero recessive prior does no harm. It cannot tell a correct `β_design` from none. @bschilder, is that the intended scope? If so, §8's sentence probably wants rewording. If the O(1) pediatric-dominant arm also needs validating, carrier screening can't supply that, and simulation (#442) may be the only route.

## 5. The acceptance criterion isn't defined yet

§8 says "within overlapping intervals", without an interval type, a minimum number of regions, or a pass fraction. With posteriors this wide, overlap is close to automatic. It is the #92 problem again, and screening truth usually comes as 95% binomial CIs. I'd suggest fixing the interval pairing, the region count and the pass threshold before any truth data is extracted, and adding a negative control: a deliberately mis-specified fit that the test must fail.
