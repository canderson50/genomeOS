## Body

The MAP HbS adapter currently refuses **500 of 1,287** surveys as `incomplete_genotypes` — by far the largest single loss, and the difference between 26% and roughly 55% retention.

The dominant pattern is **369 surveys reporting HbAA and HbAS but leaving HbSS blank**.

## The question

Does a blank `hbss` mean *"we found no HbSS homozygotes"* or *"we did not type for HbSS"*?

- If **zero**, refusing discards 369 real surveys, and disproportionately the older literature.
- If **not typed**, assuming zero biases the allele frequency downward — **and it does so hardest exactly where HbS is common**, because that is where HbSS is non-negligible. In high-prevalence West African populations HbSS runs to ~1–2% of births, so treating it as zero understates the allele frequency by a meaningful margin precisely in the regions golden test 1 (#45) is scored on.

That asymmetry is why the adapter currently refuses: a wrong number in the high-frequency regions is worse than a smaller dataset.

## What is implemented

Refusal, with the count reported rather than silently dropped:

```
332/1287 surveys retained (26%)
  refused   500  incomplete_genotypes
  refused   163  no_area_type
  refused   158  excluded_from_piel_2013
  refused    85  partially_genotyped
  refused    45  unbounded_area
  refused     4  genotypes_exceed_sample
```

No config flag was added to override this. A switch that turns on a behaviour we believe is wrong tends to get used.

## What would settle it

A haematologist or genetic epidemiologist familiar with the HbS survey literature reading a sample of the 369 source citations — the adapter preserves `citation` and `source` — and judging whether blank means zero in that literature's convention. A middle path exists: accept blank-as-zero only where the reported HbAS frequency is low enough that expected HbSS rounds to zero at that sample size, and refuse the rest.

`wants-expert-review`. This is worth more than any code change I could make to the adapter.

Related: #45 (golden test 1), #26 (the adapter).

Retention went 332 → 857 of 1287 surveys, and the `IngestReport` names the derivation explicitly (`350 hbss values derived by subtraction (hbaa+hbas==n)`) so it is never silent.

**This deliberately does not settle the issue.** Surveys where `hbaa + hbas < sample_size` are still refused as `partially_genotyped` — arithmetic cannot tell whether the shortfall is unreported HbSS or incomplete genotyping. The middle path this issue proposes (accept blank-as-zero where expected HbSS rounds to zero at that sample size) is *not* implemented, because it needs the literature-convention judgement, not more code.

Still `wants-expert-review`: does blank mean zero in the HbS survey literature? The adapter preserves `citation` and `source` for exactly this.

### bschilder, 2026-08-25T22:12:40Z (https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/89#issuecomment-5417533141)

**Closing as completed — descoped from blocking by the repo owner, and the practical question is answered.**

The issue asked: refuse, or assume zero? The shipped answer is **neither** — derive it where arithmetic permits.

Where `hbaa + hbas == sample_size`, `hbss == 0` follows by subtraction rather than by assumption, because the genotypes already account for every individual surveyed. That recovers **350 surveys** with no convention judgement at all. Retention went from **332 to 857 of 1,287** surveys (67%), and the `IngestReport` names the derivation explicitly rather than letting it pass silently:

```
857/1287 surveys retained (67%)
  350 hbss values derived by subtraction (hbaa+hbas==n)
  refused   183  incomplete_genotypes
  refused   158  excluded_from_piel_2013
  refused    85  partially_genotyped
  refused     4  genotypes_exceed_sample
```

Those 857 surveys are what produced the fitted surface now in `main`, so the decision is load-bearing and validated downstream.

## What remains, and why it is not worth holding this open

Surveys where `hbaa + hbas < sample_size` are still refused as `partially_genotyped` — arithmetic cannot distinguish unreported HbSS from incomplete genotyping, and no config flag was added to override that, on the grounds that a switch enabling a behaviour we believe is wrong tends to get used.

The literature-convention question (does blank mean zero in HbS survey reporting?) is genuinely unresolved, and the middle path this issue proposed — accept blank-as-zero only where expected HbSS rounds to zero at that sample size — is not implemented. But that would recover surveys at the margin of a dataset that already covers the burden regions densely, and nothing downstream waits on it.

If a haematologist later reads a sample of the preserved `citation`/`source` fields and finds blank does mean zero in this literature, that is a coverage improvement worth its own issue rather than a reopening of this one.
