## Trigger and observed evidence

The frozen B0H v2 calibration at source revision
`d98794b18b64e230cbdbae8b23d37488a42de804` completed its sole runner invocation and
exactly-once terminal collection. The retained snapshot passed file/hash/database/inventory
verification, but the frozen reducer refused the scientific claim:

- 1,935 fit-bearing cases accepted;
- two planned structural cases returned the expected refusal;
- one fit-bearing case failed: `study_id=1`, `track_id=1`, `case_id=8`,
  `replicate_id=11`, attempt 0;
- zero execution failures and zero ownership losses;
- attempt 1 was correctly not admitted because the attempt-0 outcome was `failed`, not
  `convergence_failed`;
- reducer outcome: `unconditional_claim_eligible=false`, reason
  `unresolved_case_or_required_diagnostic_outcomes`.

The retained typed attempt error is:

```text
category: value
exception: builtins.ValueError
message: interior mean and concentration produce beta-binomial shape parameters
         outside the supported stable numeric domain
```

`PopulationHeterogeneityFit.__post_init__` converts each sampled `rho` to
`concentration=(1-rho)/rho` and constructs `CountPredictive`, whose stable-domain check refuses
concentrations above `1/sqrt(float64 eps)` or unusable alpha/beta terms. The B0H sampler itself
admits every `rho` strictly between zero and one, so the model's posterior support is currently
wider than the predictive artifact contract. One valid sampler draw near zero was enough to make
the entire fit result unavailable.

The failed calibration remains immutable. Its B0/B0H comparison and downstream B1G launch were
correctly barred.

## Scientific objective and product claim

Make the B0H model's admitted posterior support and the exact finite-count predictive contract
coherent, without clipping or dropping posterior draws, silently substituting a binomial law, or
weakening the existing numerical and calibration gates.

The claim is limited: every retained B0H posterior draw is either evaluated as the declared
beta-binomial law to a validated numerical error bound or the fit is refused by a scientifically
justified support rule fixed before sampling. This issue does not establish predictive superiority
or authorize publication.

## Measurable output and acceptance evidence

1. Add a minimal deterministic reproducer for the retained failing case or an equivalent frozen
   synthetic fixture, and record the offending mean/rho/concentration range without committing the
   408 MB private campaign snapshot.
2. Establish an independent high-precision oracle across the exact failure neighborhood and the
   declared count domain. Test log mass, CDF/tails, quantiles, coverage, randomized PIT,
   normalization, allele-complement symmetry, and the binomial-limit behavior.
3. Choose and justify one coherent support contract. A numerical implementation must evaluate the
   same beta-binomial law and pass the oracle. A model-support restriction must be part of the
   sampled model and admission record rather than post-hoc filtering. Any other refusal must remain
   explicit and make campaign ineligibility predictable before spending a full run.
4. Preserve exact failures at and beyond the validated boundary. Do not relabel `ValueError` as a
   convergence failure merely to unlock the doubled-budget retry.
5. Recheck the 11 failed laws and runtime regressions retained by closed PR #274. That recurrence
   remains rejected unless all prior failures are resolved under unchanged or stronger tolerances.
6. Run focused tests, full CI gates, and actual-device CPU/GPU parity if the GPU scoring path changes.
7. Only after the repair is reviewed, run a new immutable B0H calibration under a new version. It
   must produce all 1,936 summary cases plus the two expected structural refusals and an eligible
   frozen reduction before the unchanged B0/B0H comparison is admitted.

## Engineering component and public interface

The affected contract crosses `PopulationHeterogeneityFit` in
`genomeos/surfaces/heterogeneity_types.py`, the public fitter in
`genomeos/surfaces/reference_heterogeneity.py`, and `CountPredictive` in
`genomeos/validation/predictive.py`. Keep science modules pure and deterministic. If accepted
numeric support changes, bind it to immutable campaign/runtime evidence and test both CPU and CuPy
paths where applicable.

## Assumptions, refusals, and downstream consumers

- The exact beta-binomial finite-count law remains the scientific target.
- No posterior draw may be clipped, discarded, or silently approximated.
- A prior approaching `rho=0` makes the binomial limit scientifically relevant, but mathematical
  proximity alone does not authorize substituting the limit for the declared finite-concentration
  law.
- The frozen v2 failure is retained evidence and must not be rewritten or resumed.
- If no accurate and operational implementation supports this region, preserve explicit refusal
  and revise the preregistered model/support contract before a new campaign.

Downstream consumers are #211, the blocked B0/B0H comparison, #331 B1G, and subsequent WP4 model
comparisons. A numerical/statistical expert should review the support choice, oracle, and error
envelope.
