## Body

## TL;DR

The global modeling plan requires development seeds 42, 43, and 44, but the frozen B0 and B2 HbS baselines and the registered primary B1G comparison use seed 42. The comparison validator correctly refuses a candidate and baseline with different seed identities. Define and implement matched seed-43/44 sensitivity comparisons before launching those fits so seed choice cannot be used to select a favorable result.

## Scientific objective

Measure whether B1G's development conclusion is stable to the declared stochastic seeds without treating repeated computation on the same held-out observations as independent biological replication.

Seed 42 remains the preregistered primary comparison under #331. Seeds 43 and 44 are sensitivity evidence only: they cannot rescue a failed primary result, replace a failed fold, or establish promotion.

## Measurable output and acceptance evidence

- Run B0, B2-current, and B1G with matched configuration identity at each of seeds 42, 43, and 44. Inputs, outer splits, buffers, candidate grid, priors, convergence gates, retry policy, scoring, and held-out rows remain unchanged.
- Every per-seed comparison uses the existing exact B1G-versus-B0/B2 validator and must retain all five outer folds, failures, retries, refusals, and matched-row exclusions.
- Preserve three separate per-seed reports. Do not concatenate or bootstrap the same held-out observations as if the seeds created new studies.
- Add one deterministic robustness summary that requires the seed set to be exactly `{42, 43, 44}`, validates equality of every non-seed identity field, and reports the per-seed values plus their minimum, median, and maximum.
- State whether the sign of the paired log-score difference and the direction of relative macro-MAE change agree across all completed seeds. Do not invent an additional promotion threshold from the observed results.
- An incomplete seed remains explicit and makes the sensitivity summary incomplete; no replacement seed or selective rerun is allowed.

## Engineering component and public interface

Add a pure research-side reducer over three already validated comparison reports. It performs no fitting, file access, network access, serving inference, or automatic promotion decision. Run orchestration remains an offline adapter and writes immutable per-seed artifacts.

Before launching seed 43 or 44, confirm that the B0 and B2 runners can reproduce the frozen seed-42 non-seed identity and create matching baseline artifacts for the new seed. If they cannot, stop and preserve the refusal instead of bypassing `configuration.seed` validation.

## Assumptions, refusals, and downstream consumers

- Repeated sampler seeds quantify computational sensitivity; they are not independent populations, loci, regions, or external confirmation.
- Seed 42 remains primary because it was frozen before the B1G result. Seeds 43/44 are not used for model selection or gate revision.
- The adequately powered stratum rule in #371, reviewed dependency evidence, independent variants, and sealed external confirmation remain unresolved and cannot be supplied by this sensitivity analysis.
- No publication or production promotion follows from this work.

This advances #189 and #331 and closes neither. A spatial statistician should review the treatment of repeated seeds and confirm that per-seed reporting avoids pseudoreplication.
