## Summary

Two requirements attach to the AVI value we publish for rs334, one from the Atlas page and one from the Additional Terms. Neither is recorded anywhere in the repository. Both are cheap to satisfy and both are decisions for the maintainer, not for me, so this is a scoping issue rather than a PR.

## 1. The citation the Atlas page asks for

The live Atlas page says, verbatim:

> If you use an AlphaGenome output in your work, please cite the following paper: Avsec et al., 2026.
> If you use AlphaGenome Atlas website or scores, please cite: Cheng, et al., 2026.

The Atlas paper is Cheng et al., "AlphaGenome Atlas: in silico mutagenesis of the entire human genome improves prioritization and interpretation of non-coding variants", medRxiv 2026.09.16.26363192, doi 10.64898/2026.09.16.26363192.

Our committed record cites the model paper only. Both the payload and the audit carry the string `AlphaGenome (Avsec et al. 2026); Atlas AVI, accessed 2026-09-09`, and `Cheng` does not appear anywhere in the tree (checked with `git grep` across `main`).

This looks like timing rather than an oversight: `docs/audits/alphagenome-avi-licensing.md` states every statement was read from the primary source on 2026-09-10, and the Atlas preprint is dated 2026-09-16.

## 2. The notice the Additional Terms ask for

AlphaGenome Services Additional Terms of Service, last modified 2026-09-08, section "Prohibitions and Confidentiality", item 5, verbatim:

> Except for Permissive Use Downloadable Artifacts for Commercial and Non-Commercial Use, without providing conspicuous notice that any Output or Derivatives you publish or distribute are provided under and subject to AlphaGenome Output Terms of Use and of any modifications you make to Output.

The terms suggest the shape of that notice: a copy of the Output Terms plus a file containing the line "By using this information, you agree to AlphaGenome Output Terms of Use found at https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/output-terms".

What we publish today: the cached payload at `website/public/data/atlas/external/alphagenome/chr11-5227002-t-a.json`, rendered by `website/src/components/atlas/AlphagenomeEvidence.tsx` as a tab labelled Predicted impact, and recorded in the catalog with `commercial_use.terms_url` pointing at the Additional Terms. There is no reference to the Output Terms of Use anywhere: not in the panel, not in the payload, not in the catalog entry, not in the audit.

## Why this is a decision and not a patch

Item 5 carries the same carve-out as the commercial rule: it does not apply to Permissive Use Downloadable Artifacts. The committed payload states the value was read through the Atlas interface (`method: atlas_lookup`, accessed 2026-09-09), which is exactly the distinction left open in #246: the carve-out is worded against artifacts made available for download in the Permissive Use section, not against an AVI value however it was obtained. So either

- the notice and the citation are added to what we publish, which is small and harmless, or
- it is recorded that the value is a Permissive Use Downloadable Artifact and neither applies.

Either way the reasoning should be written down next to the existing audit, because the panel is public and the next person to publish an Atlas value from this repo will inherit whatever we decide here.

## What I am not proposing

No change to the panel's shape, no change to the payload's immutability convention, no edit to any committed payload. If a change to published data is wanted, it belongs in a new payload version with the citation in `model_version` or an equivalent field, not in an in-place edit.

## Relationship to other issues

Advances #246 (acquisition path and refresh cadence): if the answer is that we should source AVI from the permissive bulk download, both requirements resolve without a special case.
