## Body

Give every population label a coordinate, a provenance, a `location_type`, and an uncertainty radius.

The spec's central finding is that the binding constraint on this project is **coordinates, not variants** — gnomAD v4 has ~800k people and no usable geography; HGDP has coordinates and 929 people. Nothing can be mapped until this exists, so P0 blocks everything.

This registry is also independently publishable: nobody has released a curated cross-source population→coordinate table with uncertainty radii.

#149 now adds the publication-side interface into P0 without weakening this registry contract.
Publication evidence stores only the source's verbatim `population_label`; promotion resolves it
through exactly one alias with `source=literature`, requires the referenced P0 row, copies `lat`,
`lon`, and `uncertainty_radius_km` verbatim, and asserts equality after P1 validation.

The publications adapter cannot create a registry row, accept source coordinates/radii, choose a
country centroid, or use a fallback radius. Missing ordinary aliases fail the build; unresolved
regional labels are counted refusals. The schema-tested Sami fixture demonstrates the complete P0
join, while the 426-row LCT audit deliberately imports none of its upstream coordinate columns.
This advances #7 but does not close the broader registry work.
