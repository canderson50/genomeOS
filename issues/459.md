## Body

## Problem

When a visitor switches dataset in the Atlas, the main thread stalls for about 0.6 s right after the new surface is committed. The stall exists on main from before #453 (the reveal wave).

Since #458 the wave holds at the view centre while the stall lasts, so the globe briefly looks empty instead of frozen. The time is still lost.

## Found in

- Measured while tuning #458 (the reveal wave): headed Chrome on ANGLE Metal, Apple M3 Pro.
- The cold load does not show it. A dataset switch does.

## What to do

- Profile a dataset switch, with the `atlas:*` User Timing marks and a Performance trace, to find the long task after the commit.
- Then fix it at its cause. #450's cold-load stall came from re-uploading unchanged buffers, which may be a lead.

## Done when

- A dataset switch on desktop has no task longer than 100 ms after the commit.
- The reveal wave starts within one frame of the commit.
