## Problem

The Natural Earth country borders ship as `website/public/data/atlas/ne-50m-admin-0.geojson`: 3.08 MB raw, 1.03 MB gzip. It's the second-largest download on a first visit, after the Cesium bundle.

## Change

Serve a compact derived encoding instead, either TopoJSON with quantization or a small quantized binary. Choose by measured gzip size and decode cost. Requirements:
- **Bounded error:** the positional error is bounded, and the bound is stated and tested.
- **Deterministic generation:** a script builds the file from the pinned upstream revision and verifies the source digest.
- **Provenance:** the catalog's `context_sources` entry and its provenance are updated, and the exporter writes the same entry.
- **Same output:** the rendered borders and heights are unchanged within the stated tolerance.
- **Credit:** "Natural Earth (public domain)" is unchanged.

## Acceptance

- The compact file is several times smaller (gzip) than the GeoJSON, with a measured decode time.
- Generator tests, and overlay and worker tests on the new format, pass.
- `freeze_contract` stays current.
- The PR notes the one-line catalog interaction with #437.
