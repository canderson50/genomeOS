## Body

## Problem

On a phone at Slow 4G, the first observations appear at about 11.3 s. About 1.55 MB has to arrive before that, and 1.1 MB of it is the Cesium chunk (`_astro/cesium.*.js`, gzip).

The scene builds the full `Viewer` (`atlas-scene.ts`, `new Viewer(...)`). `Viewer` imports the whole widget layer even though the Atlas hides every widget: Animation, Timeline, BaseLayerPicker, Geocoder, HomeButton, SceneModePicker, NavigationHelpButton, FullscreenButton, VRButton, InfoBox, SelectionIndicator and knockout.

## Change

Build the scene on `CesiumWidget`, the engine-only widget, and replace any Viewer-only API. Import from the engine so the widget layer and its CSS leave the bundle. Behaviour, credits and visuals must not change.

## Acceptance

- The Cesium chunk's gzip size is measured before and after.
- The §B.8 bundle checks are updated to pin the new size.
- Atlas unit tests and e2e specs pass.
- Desktop and phone captures are identical in layout and credits.
- The cold-load harness shows observations-visible and surface-visible before and after.
