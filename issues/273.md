- Matplotlib also reports `[Errno 13] Permission denied: '/private'` for `MPLCONFIGDIR`.

pytest-owned temporary space for that test run. They must not inherit a host-specific or poisoned
cache path, and the renderer/demo production code and scientific behavior must remain unchanged.
