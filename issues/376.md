## Body

## TL;DR

The frozen global-modeling plan allows one recorded retry with twice the sampling and tuning budget when a model fit fails the convergence checks. B1G currently marks the fit failed immediately. Add the prespecified retry as part of the original benchmark identity, retain both attempts, and still fail after one unsuccessful retry.

## Trigger

`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-09-global-af-modeling.md` says: “Keep convergence thresholds; allow one logged retry with doubled draws/tuning, then mark failure.” `fit_b1g` raises a typed `B1GConvergenceError`, but `_fit_and_score` records that first failure without retrying. The reference B0H path already implements the intended two-attempt pattern in `genomeos/validation/reference_b0h_fold.py`.

This must coexist with #333’s rule that checkpoint resume cannot selectively retry a terminal fold. The retry belongs inside the originally frozen fold execution and must be planned before outcomes are observed; a completed terminal shard remains immutable.

## Scientific objective

Apply the prespecified convergence policy consistently to B1G inner and outer fits without changing convergence thresholds, candidate selection, or held-out evidence in response to results.

## Measurable output and acceptance evidence

- every requested B1G fit freezes exactly two attempt slots: `initial` and `retry`;
- an accepted initial fit leaves the retry explicitly `not_attempted`;
- only a typed `B1GConvergenceError` admits the retry;
- the retry doubles `draws` and `tune`, changes only the deterministic fit seed and sampling budget, and keeps the same training data, basis configuration, priors, likelihood, chains, target acceptance, and convergence thresholds;
- structural, runtime, prediction, scoring, and interruption failures never trigger the scientific retry;
- both attempts retain their config, seed, status, reason, and sampler diagnostics;
- a failed retry makes the inner candidate or outer fold fail exactly as today, with no third attempt;
- checkpoint resume preserves terminal evidence and never creates a retry after the shard exists;
- synthetic tests cover accepted initial, accepted retry, failed retry, non-retryable failure, deterministic seeds, and immutable checkpoint round trips.

## Engineering component and public interface

Add a pure B1G fit-attempt boundary analogous to `run_b0h_fold`, then use it from nested candidate fitting and outer-fold fitting. Extend the immutable inner/outer checkpoint records and evidence tables to retain both attempts. Version the shard schema because the serialized evidence changes.

## Assumptions and refusals

- Do not weaken R-hat, ESS, or divergence thresholds.
- Do not raise `target_accept`, alter priors, change basis candidates, or inspect scores to decide whether to retry.
- Do not retry a prediction or scoring error.
- Do not retry a terminal checkpoint shard or an already observed campaign result.
- Existing artifacts remain evidence for their recorded protocol and are never rewritten.

## Expert review

A spatial statistician should confirm that one doubled-budget retry is the intended interpretation of WP2 for both inner candidate fits and untouched outer fits, and that a distinct deterministic retry seed does not change the benchmark estimand.
