## Body

## TL;DR

We found a Chinese genomic dataset that publishes allele frequencies broken down by region **with
the sample sizes behind them**, downloadable with no login and no registration. Sample sizes are the
part that matters: a frequency without a denominator cannot enter our observations store at all.

There is one trap. The four regions are wildly unequal — one has about 4,000 people behind it and
another has about 50 — so three of the four cannot support a usable estimate and must be masked
rather than mapped.

---

## What it is

The Westlake BioBank for Chinese: 4,480 whole genomes plus about 5,900 array-genotyped individuals,
published in Nature Communications in 2022.

## Why it is unusual

Nearly every national resource we assessed in #3 section H publishes one pooled national number.
This one ships per-region frequencies **and allele numbers** directly in the VCF INFO column, so
counts are recoverable. Verified by fetching a chromosome:

```
AC=2;AF=0.000223214;AN=8960;NS=4480;North_AF=0.0;North_AN=448;Central_AF=0.0;Central_AN=100;
South_AF=0.000247831;South_AN=8070;Lingnan_AF=0.0;Lingnan_AN=126
```

Download, no authentication:
```bash
curl -O https://wbbc.westlake.edu.cn/data/WBBC.chr22.GRCh38.vcf.gz   # HTTP 200, ~30 MB
```

## The trap, stated plainly

From the row above, the per-region denominators are:

| Region | Allele number | Roughly |
|---|---:|---:|
| South | 8,070 | ~4,035 people |
| North | 448 | ~224 people |
| Lingnan | 126 | ~63 people |
| Central | 100 | ~50 people |

The cohort is overwhelmingly southern, which follows from Westlake being in Hangzhou. A frequency
estimated from fifty people is not wrong so much as uninformative, and drawing it as a regional
value on a map would imply confidence we do not have.

**So: ingest all four regions with their true denominators, and let the existing masking rules do
their job.** This is precisely the case the `unknown` and `prior_dominated` states exist for. Do not
drop the small regions silently and do not pool them into a national number — both destroy
information the source actually gave us.

## What this needs

1. A source adapter producing georeferenced counts, not frequencies, from the four regional fields.
2. Reviewed coordinates and an uncertainty radius for each of North, Central, South and Lingnan.
   These are large multi-province blocks, and the radius must say so.
3. Ascertainment fields. The recruitment design needs reading from the paper; do not default them.
4. A note in the adapter that the array and WGS tiers are different populations.

## Blocked on one thing

**#326 — the sample-overlap audit.** If this cohort shares people with any other Chinese resource we
ingest, a merged surface double-counts them. Worth ingesting alone regardless, but do not merge with
another Chinese source until that is answered.

## Terms

Citation request only; no redistribution ban found on the summary tier. Raw individual genotypes and
FASTQ are separately gated behind China's Human Genetic Resources Administration and an IRB, and we
neither need nor want those.

Minor correction to the assessment in #3: the Han-only admixture subset spans 27 administrative
divisions, not 29.

### Full chr22 feasibility audit: useful, with one explicit derivation gate

I retrieved the public GRCh38 chr22 VCF, TBI and official download documentation into the ignored private research area and audited every record with a C-streamed extraction plus chunked vectorized reduction.

Source identities:

- VCF: 29,921,805 bytes, SHA-256 `1c67735d7394943edc2ba8477e06370b67e182c44c11dcf633cf3964902d6874`
- TBI: 30,508 bytes, SHA-256 `15b6ccdd668c7821aff35e7899bcbec38bb850681be608f25210adc8f5c66f56`
- official GRCh38 download page: SHA-256 `500097a75165b5755d1773f1ab7b3971c837c63d6fa5ad2a8e098cc7876492bd`

The file contains **1,091,904** records. Every one is PASS, biallelic, uniquely keyed and position-sorted; 986,992 are SNVs and 104,912 are indels. There are no missing regional AF/AN pairs and no invalid frequency/count domains.

The source's own global fields provide a strong rounding control: nearest-integer `AF × AN` recovers the literally reported `AC` on all 1,091,904 records. The maximum product residual is 0.00448, leaving a minimum 0.49552 margin from the wrong half-integer boundary.

Regional count reconstruction is similarly unambiguous on every record:

| Region | Fixed AN | Maximum `abs(AF × AN − nearest integer)` | Minimum half-integer margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| North | 448 | 0.000192 | 0.499808 |
| Central | 100 | 7.1e-15 | 0.500000 |
| South | 8,070 | 0.00425 | 0.495750 |
| Lingnan | 126 | 0.000062 | 0.499938 |

This confirms the severe imbalance and also shows that integer regional AC candidates are uniquely recoverable under the file's demonstrated rounding convention. However, regional AC is **derived**, not reported. The adapter must not relabel it as reported or silently round it: genomeOS needs an explicit reviewed executable derivation rule, with the global AC/AF/AN round-trip as a mandatory source-level control.

Two additional source quirks matter:

- The VCF header declares no INFO fields even though every row carries them; the official HTML documents their meanings. A source adapter must inject or independently validate a closed exact schema rather than trust generic VCF typing.
- `NS=4480` and `AN=8960` on every chr22 row, exactly matching the paper's retained WGS cohort. This strongly indicates the frequency VCF is WGS-only rather than the 5,841-person array tier, but that remains an inference from the file/paper agreement and should be recorded as such.

Private reproducible evidence is at `data/research/wbbc-chr22-regional-count-probe-20260916/`; audit JSON SHA-256 is `89f64b0599a3621f8a39244ef80401bb1eda49199726c686e47ea7bc433f5361`.

Decision: **continue #325**. WBBC supplies unusually strong source-neutral regional evidence with denominators. Promotion still requires reviewed regional aliases/coordinates/radii, ascertainment fields, the derivation decision above, and the #326 no-merge overlap rule. No row has entered P1 and no model or surface changed.

### Ascertainment qualification: mixed clinical/population cohort, so preserve the bias

The public participant supplement resolves the mandatory ascertainment fields without exposing any participant row. I aggregated its 4,480 retained WGS records locally:

- phenotype: 2,546 `Normal`, 1,934 `Parkinson's disease`;
- sex: 2,444 female, 2,036 male;
- age: 16–89 years, median 58.

The paper independently states that Xiangya Hospital contributed 1,973 Parkinson's disease cases and 1,362 healthy controls to the broader WBBC collection, and that Hunan and Jiangxi account for 87% of all WGS samples. The regional definitions printed by the paper are also now captured exactly: North (13 named divisions), Central (Anhui/Jiangsu), South (9 named divisions) and Lingnan (Guangxi/Guangdong/Hainan). Source: [WBBC primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-30526-x).

This means the pooled frequency release cannot honestly be labelled `population_random`, `clinical_case`, or `clinical_control`. Under the existing P1 vocabulary, the supported values are:

```text
sampling_design = convenience
disease_ascertainment_excluded = false
cohort_id = wbbc:wgs-frequency-release-v1  # proposed stable ID
```

The disease enrichment is scientifically material: the adapter must preserve it so the model can estimate or stratify ascertainment effects rather than treating these as general-population frequencies.

I added a private, aggregate-only reproducibility script and report under `data/research/wbbc-chr22-regional-count-probe-20260916/`. The report contains no participant identifiers or participant rows; its SHA-256 is `77e2a62c7d033021e1d0265207bb7e1b32ed8596347173e9e967bbc47ebdbb8f`. The participant-linked workbook remains ignored, private and unuploaded.

Decision remains **continue, but not yet P1-eligible**. The remaining hard gates are reviewed regional aliases/coordinates/radii, a reviewed executable AF×AN→AC derivation rule, reuse/redistribution review, and the #326 no-merge overlap rule.
