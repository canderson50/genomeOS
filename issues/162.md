## Body

## Scientific objective / product claim\n\nPredict the posterior on a triangle mesh rather than only at H3 cell centers, so the scientific artifact—not just the renderer—can support smoother and more geometrically flexible contours when statistical elevation is enabled.\n\nThe current Issue #55 hotfix triangulates H3 cell-centered posterior values only for presentation. It improves rendering but does not claim or invent additional scientific resolution.\n\n## Measurable output and acceptance evidence\n\n- Define and freeze a versioned triangle-mesh surface contract with stable vertex and face identities.\n- Publish posterior mean, posterior uncertainty, credible interval, and evidence-support state at the resolution consumed by each triangle.\n- Demonstrate an offline deterministic fit for at least the HbS and G6PD validation artifacts.\n- Show that masked or unsupported regions remain masked after triangulation and that coastlines/islands do not acquire unsupported values.\n- Compare the direct mesh posterior with the current H3-centered artifact and document which apparent detail is supported by data.\n- Add a review figure showing measured observations, the direct mesh posterior, uncertainty, and unsupported areas as separate visual layers.\n\n## Engineering component and public interface\n\n- P2 offline surface inference emits immutable mesh artifacts keyed by variant, model version, and data version.\n- The serving adapter exposes the mesh without fitting or interpolation on the read path.\n- The P5 Cesium renderer consumes direct vertex/face values through the existing surface-layer boundary.\n- Existing H3 artifacts remain supported until a reviewed migration and compatibility plan exists.\n\n## Assumptions, refusal conditions, and downstream consumers\n\n- Mesh density is not evidence density. Do not publish finer triangles unless support and validation justify the scientific resolution.\n- The browser must not create posterior values, uncertainty, observation radii, or support states.\n- Unknown and prior-dominated regions remain nonnumeric and excluded from aggregation.\n- Observations and inferred surfaces remain separate layers and contracts.\n- No method, mesh resolution, coordinate source, or success threshold may be silently substituted.\n- Downstream consumers are the Cesium elevation renderer, future tiled delivery, regional aggregation, and burden modeling.\n\nAdvances #55.
