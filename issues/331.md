## Trigger and dependency

This is the predeclared post-B0H spatial candidate implied by #103 and the hard-local result in
#307. It must remain in `Backlog` until the B0H calibration and, if admitted, paired B0/B0H
comparison in #211 are terminal. Do not change this candidate, its splits, or its gates in response
to partial or final B0H outcomes. B0H remains a nonspatial count model; it neither validates nor
invalidates this spatial mechanism by itself. The checkpointed current-B2 benchmark must also reach
a terminal, complete result; B1G cannot be promoted against B0 alone while B2 is incomplete.

## Scientific objective and claim

Test whether a low global background plus geographically compact, positive local residuals can
preserve HbS endemic peaks without spreading an approximately 1% allele-frequency floor across
supported non-endemic regions.

The candidate is **B1G**, a target-family development model rather than a new global default:

```text
logit(p(s)) = alpha + A * sum_j pi_j * phi(d_edge(s, c_j) / r)
phi(u) = (1 - u)^4 * (4*u + 1) for 0 <= u < 1, else 0
```

`phi` is the fixed C2 compact-support Wendland basis. The intercept `alpha` represents the
background; the nonnegative basis mixture can raise local frequency but cannot manufacture negative
troughs to compensate for broad positive smearing. Reuse the current intercept prior
`Normal(-3.5, 1.5)`, set `A ~ HalfNormal(2)`, and set
`pi ~ Dirichlet(0.5, ..., 0.5)`. Retain the current design/cohort terms, beta-binomial concentration
prior, and observation prediction semantics unchanged. Zero counts are never labelled structural
zeros.

This tests one mechanism only. It does not claim that HbS is literally generated by radial kernels,
that the result generalizes to other variants, or that basis coefficients are migration, selection,
or ancestry parameters.

## Measurable output and acceptance evidence

Use the unchanged 994-row HbS development artifact, dependency-aware outer folds, 300 km buffer,
2010-aligned burden denominator, support semantics, and count diagnostics already used by the B0/B2
and B1 comparisons. Piel national burden remains a secondary integration diagnostic and may not tune
the model.

The candidate grid is fixed now:

- radii `r = (500, 1000, 2000)` km;
- basis counts `m = (8, 16, 32)`, producing nine candidates with no additional prior grid;
- one candidate centre per unique positive-count training footprint; choose the first by the smallest
  `source_record_id`, then use farthest-first edge-to-edge geodesic placement, breaking every distance
  tie by `source_record_id`; a candidate is infeasible when fewer than `m` unique positive footprints
  exist;
- select inside each outer-training set by mean cohort-macro integrated log score over complete inner
  folds; exact score ties choose fewer bases, then the smaller radius;
- reuse source revision `d98794b18b64e230cbdbae8b23d37488a42de804` and the exact B1 input
  hashes: observations `820d725fae9859a6cebca98296676e8c525b103f7033aa5237b9aaa00f79b331`,
  assignments `c922b240624c761f0752821d9e6986bb1479bbc97a0d9931da7db3a17ee20b20`, and
  dependencies `fa7b4c093dc2f83a2ea3c7f0810b9130e65feac7c94dc80fb8475d03c2d3cf22`;
- compare against both the matching B0 and the completed checkpointed current-B2 result; failure to
  beat the stronger eligible baseline is rejection.

Primary admission requires all planned outer folds to complete and the global plan's existing
promotion defaults: at least 5% lower balanced macro MAE, positive paired dependency-aware integrated
log-score improvement with a 95% interval excluding zero, predictive coverage within three percentage
points at 50/80/95%, and no more than 5% relative MAE degradation in adequately powered prespecified
strata. Report zero-count and positive-count strata separately. A result available on fewer held-out
rows than the baseline is compared only on matched rows and reports the excluded fraction; refusal
cannot create an apparent win.

Secondary mechanism diagnostics, never selection targets:

- supported-cell background in the already-consulted China, Indonesia, Afghanistan, and Nordic

- West-African peak height and fixed peak/background contrasts;
- large-zero posterior-predictive probabilities;

The first bounded artifact is a basis preflight using the frozen folds. Stop before a full GPU

## Engineering component and public interface

Implement pure research-side functions behind the existing benchmark contracts:

- construct deterministic training-only centres and radii;
- build chunked query-by-basis geodesic matrices;
- fit count likelihood parameters from training rows only;
- emit `CountPredictive` draws and ordinary benchmark reports;
- serialize immutable fit/config/source/split hashes and convergence diagnostics.

Basis evaluation must be vectorized in bounded chunks. No Python population × centre or
observation × draw nested loop is admissible in the measured path. Record chunk sizes, peak memory,
and end-to-end timing. Science modules remain free of file, network, HTTP, and environment access.
The serving path remains unchanged and consumes only precomputed admitted artifacts.

## Assumptions, refusals, and downstream consumers

- Centres are constructed separately inside each training fold; held-out coordinates or counts may
  not affect their presence, order, radius, basis count, or prior.
- A positive count does not prove an endemic centre. Centre construction is a model proposal whose
  predictive value must survive held-out evaluation.
- Refuse missing variants, leaked dependencies, invalid counts, nonfinite basis values, singular or
  non-converged fits, incomplete folds, or mismatched prediction coverage.
- Do not add MAP malaria, EVI, friction, Satellite Embeddings, or any other environmental feature.
  Each is a separate WP3 hypothesis and the publisher/topic supplies no evidence for this model.
- Do not relax support masks, widen radii post hoc, substitute burden parity for held-out count
  performance, or turn an unavailable prediction into zero.
- No publication or production promotion follows from HbS development evidence alone. A successful
  result becomes a candidate for independent variants and external confirmation under #189.

Downstream consumers are the offline #103 comparison, then #45 only after the count gate. This
advances #103 and #189 and is conditional on #211; it closes none of them.

`wants-expert-review`: a spatial statistician should review identifiability of the background and
positive bases, the compact-support kernel/radius family, and whether the uncertainty propagation is
adequate before any GPU fit.
