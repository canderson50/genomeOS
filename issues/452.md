## Body

## Problem

On desktop the explorer's controls panel (about 25rem wide) floats over the left edge of a full-width globe canvas. The camera centres the globe in the canvas, so the panel covers part of the globe's left side, including West Africa in the default HbS view.

## Proposal

Draw the globe centred in the open area to the right of the panel, by shifting Cesium's projection centre (the camera frustum's `xOffset`) instead of moving the camera.

- **The camera does not move.** The URL view (lat, lon, height, heading, pitch), fly-to, keyboard controls and picking keep their meaning. Only where the globe is drawn on screen changes.
- **Amount:** half the width the panel covers, so the globe is centred in the remaining area. It is recomputed when the window or the panel changes size.
- **No offset when the panel is hidden, and none on the phone layout,** where the panel is a bottom sheet.
- **Only the persistent controls panel counts.** The transient inspector on the right does not move the globe.
- **Rejected alternatives:**
  - starting the canvas to the right of the panel, which loses the full-bleed starfield behind the translucent panel and moves the credits and the legend;
  - rotating the camera, which moves the map's content, not the globe.

## Acceptance

- On desktop with the panel visible, the globe's on-screen centre sits about half the panel's covered width to the right of the canvas centre. With the panel hidden, it is back at the canvas centre.
- Phone layouts are unchanged.
- Hover and click select the cell under the pointer with the offset applied, pinned by an e2e test that clicks a known cell.
- Unit tests cover the offset calculation (panel visible, hidden and phone) and the resize updates.
- The cold-load budgets and the long-frame budget still pass.
