## Body

Normalise every source into one schema-validated observations table: allele counts with coordinates and **mandatory ascertainment metadata**.

Counts (`ac`, `an`) rather than frequencies, because a zero count out of 200 alleles is weak evidence, not evidence of absence. The ascertainment fields (`sampling_design`, `disease_ascertainment_excluded`, `cohort_id`) are what make the §7.1 correction estimable at all — and they cannot be retrofitted without re-ingesting every source.

### bschilder, 2026-09-05T23:04:25Z (https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/8#issuecomment-5555388463)

#149 now adds a general publication-evidence path into this P1 contract. Every allele-count
observation has a required unique `source_record_id`; existing gnomAD, MAP, and AFND adapters now
derive it from source-native immutable keys. Separate frozen `literature_evidence` and
`literature_field_evidence` ledgers retain verbose provenance, while P1 keeps only the stable key.

`publications.load(...)` recomputes variant, exact-count, verification, reuse, population, and
duplicate gates; resolves geography only through P0; and reports refusals. The offline build accepts
the two evidence ledgers as an all-or-neither pair and persists retained evidence beside the
chromosome-partitioned observations. There is no force/eligibility/radius/ascertainment fallback.
This advances #8 but does not close the broader observations-store objective.
