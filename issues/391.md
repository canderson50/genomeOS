## Body

## TL;DR

The spatial-activity model still produces a divergent sampler transition when the simulated truth
has no spatial activity signal, even after increasing the sampler acceptance target from 0.90 to
0.95 and using the single registered doubled-budget retry. The ordinary model and the strong-signal
activity model both pass. Reparameterize or otherwise repair the candidate's geometry near the null
boundary before launching the 720-task simulation campaign.

## Trigger and retained evidence

The three-task simulation-only probe uses one frozen outer split and compares:

- ordinary model under the null scenario;
- spatial-activity model under the same null scenario;
- spatial-activity model under the localized strong-signal scenario.

At target acceptance 0.90, the ordinary/null and activity/null tasks both failed after their single
registered retry because divergences remained. A development-only target-acceptance 0.95 probe then
showed:

- ordinary/null: completed on attempt 1 with zero divergences;
- activity/strong: completed on attempt 1 with zero divergences;
- activity/null: one divergence on attempt 1 and one divergence again on the doubled-budget retry.

For the failed retry, maximum rank R-hat was 1.0058, minimum bulk ESS was 743, and minimum tail ESS
was 1,275. Divergence was the only failed gate. Repeatedly increasing `target_accept` would therefore
chase a rare transition without addressing the candidate-specific null geometry.

## Scientific objective and claim

Make the spatial-activity candidate sample reliably when the true activity field carries no spatial
signal. The repaired candidate must preserve the analytically marginalized count likelihood and the
interpretation rule that an observed zero remains compatible with both inactive and ordinary
sampling-zero paths.

## Measurable output and acceptance evidence

- Diagnose the divergent transition with retained sampler state and energy/geometry evidence on the
  frozen null task; do not infer its cause from aggregate diagnostics alone.
- Specify the reparameterization before inspecting a replacement campaign's outcomes.
- Run the same three frozen probe tasks with the same single-retry rule. All three must converge with
  zero divergences and meet the existing R-hat/ESS thresholds.
- Re-run null false-support and component-dependence checks after the change. A sampler repair must
  not create apparently identifiable activity structure under the null.
- Preserve ordinary-arm behavior and the strong-signal marginal predictive target within declared
  numerical tolerances.

## Engineering component and public interface

The likely boundary is `genomeos.surfaces.spatial_activity_model._spatial_field` and the candidate
activity-field parameterization. Keep the likelihood, fitting, convergence, and assessment modules
separate. Any new configuration value must be a scientific parameter with a frozen manifest value,
not a runtime escape hatch.

## Assumptions, refusals, and downstream consumers

- Do not relax the zero-divergence gate or discard divergent draws.
- Do not add repeated retries or tune target acceptance again as a substitute for a model change.
- Do not launch the full 720-task campaign until this issue and the bounded CDF/GPU bug in #390 both
  pass their actual-device probes.
- This remains simulation-only work. It does not permit a real HbS fit or publication.

A spatial-statistics reviewer should examine whether activity amplitude/intercept and conditional
frequency become weakly identified at the null, and whether the proposed parameterization retains
the intended prior over the marginal count law.
