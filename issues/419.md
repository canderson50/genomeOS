## Body

On a 390 px phone, the home hero's floating labels ("Open source", "Built together", "For the public good") sit on top of the "Join the global community" and "Explore the vision" buttons, so part of each button is covered.

**Evidence** (main at a6188a2, 390x844, measured in Chromium): labels at y 741–786 and 802–847 px, buttons at y 719–778 and 793–851 px. Found while reviewing the header logo branch, which leaves the phone hero layout unchanged: the branch and main give identical positions.

**Done when** the labels and the buttons do not overlap at 320–430 px widths, and a Playwright test asserts that their boxes don't intersect.
