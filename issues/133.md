## Body

Split out of the adapter bug so the patch there does not quietly decide this.

The AFND harvest contains two families the allele-frequency observations store cannot represent:

- **KIR gene presence/absence** — 3,790 rows where `allele == gene`. KIR genes are copy-number
  variable, so what is reported is the fraction of individuals carrying the gene at all. That is a
  *carrier* frequency over individuals, not an allele frequency over chromosomes.
- **Cytokine promoter genotypes** — 4,517 rows, all genotype-level (`AIF-1/ -932 CC`, `-932 CT`).
  Diploid genotype frequencies, so there is no `(ac, an)` pair to recover without assuming
  Hardy-Weinberg, which is exactly the kind of silent substitution the invariants forbid.

Together about 8,300 rows, roughly 7% of the harvest. Both are being **refused** by the adapter,
which is the right default and not a permanent answer.

## Why they are worth having

KIR carrier frequencies have strong, well-documented geographic structure and are used in
transplantation matching and infectious-disease association work. Cytokine promoter variants are
the classic candidate-gene literature for inflammatory phenotypes. Neither is filler.

## What would need deciding

1. **Does the observations schema get a second measurement type**, or do these live in a separate
   store? An `(ac, an)` table cannot hold a carrier frequency without lying about the denominator.
   §4's separation of observation from inference argues for a distinct table rather than a flag on
   the existing one.
2. **Can Hardy-Weinberg ever be assumed** to convert genotype counts to allele counts? For the
   cytokine rows it would recover an allele frequency, but HWE is an assumption about the
   population, and populations under selection or with structure violate it. If we ever do this it
   has to be recorded as a derived value with its assumption attached, never as an observation.
3. **Does the burden engine have any use for carrier frequency directly?** It may — carrier
   frequency is closer to what a screening programme measures than allele frequency is. If so this
   is a feature rather than a data-cleaning problem.

No work scheduled. Filed so the refusal in the adapter is a recorded decision rather than an
oversight.
