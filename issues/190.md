## Body

## Evidence
During WP0 of #189, inspected genomeos/observations/sources/map_surveys.py at a071a7b (same tree as current main 8778d21). _radius_km maps missing, unrecognized, and large polygon area_type values to sqrt(500/pi), approximately 12.6 km. These values are then written as ordinary radius_km observations. The source docstring still says missing/unbounded extents are refused.

## Scientific impact
A fixed 500 square-kilometer assumed area is not an upper bound for an unknown or unbounded survey footprint. Treating it as measured geographic precision can understate support and defeat buffered geographic holdouts. This conflicts with the no-default uncertainty-radius invariant unless assumed and source-supported extents are explicitly separated.

## Expected behavior
Do not admit an assumed fixed radius as qualified reported sampling support. Preserve missing/unbounded extent information or refuse qualification; any proposed uncertain-footprint model must expose its assumptions and receive scientific review. Existing published artifacts must remain immutable.

## Scope
This is an audit finding, not a change to the adapter in the benchmark implementation. Related #21 and #37. The #189 inventory and future benchmark qualification must account for it before making resident/high-resolution claims.

### bschilder, 2026-09-10T05:34:23Z (https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/190#issuecomment-5613703859)

Related qualification point from the read-only #189 spatial-benchmark audit:
even a bounded **area** class is not, by itself, an upper bound on distance
from the reported coordinate. `_radius_km` converts area to the radius of an
equal-area circle; its comment says the upper-bound area is used because a
survey may occupy any of it. That does not establish a bounding radius.
For example, a 100 km × 1 km rectangular footprint has area100 km² but extends
about50 km from its centroid, versus sqrt(100/pi)≈5.64 km for an equal-area disc.
This is a geometric counterexample, not a claim about any specific MAP survey.

Thus the eventual qualification fix needs to distinguish a source-supported
bounding footprint/radius from an explicitly assumed equal-area disc, not only
replace the500 km² unknown/unbounded fallback. A model may study the latter as
an assumption, but a buffered geographic validation cannot call it a guaranteed
sampling-footprint separation without additional source evidence. The source
survey geometry/coordinate semantics were not reviewed by this code inspection.

No adapter, data, schema or published artifact was changed; this remains separate
from the current #211/#214 numerical-model work. Existing artifact immutability
and the unresolved source/ascertainment checks remain in force.
