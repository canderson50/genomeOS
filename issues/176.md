## Body

## Bug and requested marker option

Observation domes previously rotated into visibly different directions while the camera zoomed or panned. Dense clusters consequently looked like randomly tilted hemispheres instead of stable landmarks attached to the inferred surface.

The stabilized surface-mounted Dome presentation remains available, while the original full 3D marker is restored as a separate Spheres option. Both marker types must stay above the inferred statistical surface, cell outlines, and their sampling-radius rings.

## Expected

- the marker selector exposes distinct Spheres and Domes choices
- a Sphere is full 3D geometry with equal radii; a Dome is the stable screen-upright surface-mounted presentation
- Spheres are the default at 100% marker opacity; explicit shared URLs retain their selected shape and opacity
- both shapes follow the rendered height of the active inferred surface without intersecting it
- measured markers render above cell outlines and all sampling-radius rings while retaining globe/far-side occlusion
- marker size, color, metadata color, opacity, Earth opacity, hover, selection, and URL sharing work for both
- existing obsShape=hemisphere links continue to select Domes; Spheres use obsShape=sphere

## Contract

1. **Scientific/product claim:** measured observation markers remain visually stable and distinct from inferred cells while navigating the Atlas; marker shape changes presentation only.
2. **Measurable output and acceptance evidence:** focused tests cover default Sphere selection and full opacity, explicit URL preservation, equal-axis 3D geometry, surface-relative clearance, opacity, and overlay ordering; an oblique local preview verifies Spheres and Domes remain above the surface.
3. **Engineering component and public interface:** extend ObservationShape and the measured point control, then add a dedicated sphere primitive path while reusing the existing observation placement/lifecycle interface.
4. **Assumptions, refusal conditions, and downstream consumers:** preserve observation data and surface artifacts unchanged; do not disable depth testing globally or reveal far-side markers; circles and pins remain unchanged; shared URLs and the inspector consume the new shape value.

## Likely regression boundary

The surface anchor needs the geometry normal; the 2D Dome artwork does not need a camera-projected rotation. A true Sphere additionally needs its center lifted by its world-space radius, rather than placing its center directly at the shared marker anchor. Defaults must be changed consistently at both the URL-state and scene-policy boundaries to avoid a transient mismatch during catalog loading.
