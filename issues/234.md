## Body

## What

[`scripts/publish_artifacts.py:190-191`](https://github.com/bschilder/genomeOS/blob/main/scripts/publish_artifacts.py#L188-L193) rounds two values on their way into the artifact manifest:

```python
correlation_range_km=round(float(fit.correlation_range_km), 1),
prior_frequency_sd=round(float(fit.prior_frequency_sd), 5),
```

Both are data-derived, not round numbers — published manifests carry `prior_frequency_sd` values like `0.12518`, `0.12262`, `0.13781`, `0.15652`.

## Why it is worth a line of code

**Nothing published is wrong.** `cell_table()` computes the contraction from the live fit at full precision (`contraction = predicted["post_sd"].to_numpy() / fit.prior_frequency_sd`, `genomeos/surfaces/artifacts.py:143`), so every published `support` verdict and every per-cell number is unaffected. The rounding touches only the manifest's descriptive record. Verified before filing.

**But the manifest is the record of what a consumer can redo.** `cell_table`'s own docstring says the artifact "carries the distance as well as the verdict so a consumer can apply a stricter threshold without refitting." A consumer doing exactly that needs `prior_frequency_sd` to recompute contraction, and the only published source of it is the rounded manifest value.

**Magnitude is small and I want to be honest about it.** Rounding ~0.125 to 5 dp is a relative error of at most ~4e-5. Against a contraction threshold of order 0.5, only cells within 4e-5 of the boundary could flip, and a cell that close to the boundary has an essentially arbitrary verdict either way. So this is a fidelity issue, not a correctness one, and it is not urgent.

It is, however, free to fix: storing the unrounded float costs nothing and makes the manifest state what the fit actually used.

## Suggested fix

Drop both `round()` calls. If the rounding exists for manifest readability, round at display time in the `print` on the following lines, which already formats them (`rho {fit.correlation_range_km:.0f} km`), rather than in the stored record.

## Acceptance evidence

- A published manifest's `prior_frequency_sd` equals `fit.prior_frequency_sd` exactly.
- Recomputing `post_sd / prior_frequency_sd` from a published artifact plus its manifest reproduces the published `support` verdict for every cell, with no boundary disagreement.

Existing artifacts are unaffected and need no republication: this changes what future manifests record, and is not part of any identity.
