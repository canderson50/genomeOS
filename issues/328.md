## Body

## TL;DR

Taiwan Biobank will send you whole-dataset frequency files if you email them your name and
institution. That is unusually easy access for a national biobank.

The catch is that its frequencies come out as a single island-wide number. No county, no region. So
it is one more well-founded dot on the map rather than the sub-national detail we are short of.

Worth doing, but rank it below the sources that ship geography.

---

## What is available

From the live download page:

| Tier | Individuals |
|---|---:|
| Whole-genome sequencing allele frequencies | 1,492 |
| Array, TWB1 | 24,679 |
| Array, TWB2 | 100,288 |
| Combined imputed | 118,908 |

Delivered as whole-dataset VCF and ZIP files on a named email request. No formal data-use agreement
was found for this summary tier, and no reuse licence appears on the About, Download or Privacy
pages — a completed check that found no restriction beyond the request gate.

## What is not available

The variant browser returns one national figure. Tested on rs671, the well-known aldehyde
dehydrogenase variant: G at 0.7179, A at 0.2821, island-wide, with no county or region selector
anywhere in the query tool.

Taiwan Biobank recruits through a network of sites across the island, so the sampling geography
exists upstream. It simply is not in what they publish.

## Why it is still worth ingesting

A well-characterised Han Taiwanese population with a real denominator is not something we can get
elsewhere, and 1,492 sequenced genomes is a solid basis for a national estimate. It is a genuine
observation at country resolution. That is worth having so long as we are honest that it is one
point and not a surface.

## What this needs

1. Send the named request and record what comes back, including the exact release and assembly.
2. Decide which tier to ingest. The sequencing tier has the cleanest provenance; the imputed tier is
   much larger but imputation is a different measurement and should not be silently pooled with it.
3. A reviewed coordinate and uncertainty radius for Taiwan as a whole.
4. Ascertainment fields from the biobank's recruitment design, read rather than assumed.
5. Record the terms check as no restriction found, with the pages checked and the date.

Assessed in #3, section H.
