## Body

## Scientific objective

Keep every AFND population measurement traceable to a globally unique stable source record without silently dropping or merging distinct evidence.

## What I ran

I downloaded `raw/afnd_frequencies.tsv` and `raw/afnd_populations.tsv` from `bschilder/genomeos-data` revision `fc17bc1c1d96a0d0766746dcf26277ccdc669717`, then ran:

```python
from genomeos.observations.sources import afnd_frequencies
obs, report = afnd_frequencies.load(frequencies, populations, "hf-2026-09")
```

## What happened

Pandera raises because `source_record_id` is not unique. Exactly two retained rows produce:

```text
afnd-frequencies:1e92e3de60ac60dc6944bdc060a58757a556beec44ccecdb5bef1e5525c0cbf1
variant_id: hla:b-08-20
population_id: afnd-china-north-han
ac/an: 0/210
```

The duplicated rows are identical across the fields currently used by `stable_source_record_id()`.

## Expected

The adapter must either prove the rows are accidental source duplicates and refuse/report one through an explicit deterministic rule, or preserve a source-native discriminator that makes legitimately distinct measurements unique. It must not append row position or silently drop a row.

## Invariant and acceptance evidence

This currently violates the schema hard-error invariant and blocks the full AFND P1 build. A regression test should reproduce the duplicate pair, require an explicit refusal or stable source-native disambiguator, and show `retained + refused == total` with unique `source_record_id` values.

Downstream consumers are the P1 observation store, P2 surface inputs, and any published corpus metrics.
