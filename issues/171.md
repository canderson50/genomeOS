## Body

## Product claim

The Atlas More info panel should let users inspect reviewed gnomAD evidence from complementary analytical perspectives without conflating genetic ancestry with geography or external annotations with genomeOS observations and inferred surfaces.

## Measurable output and acceptance evidence

- Add a layered gnomAD evidence navigator with Overview, Genetic ancestry, Genomic constraint, and ClinVar views.
- Show non-sex-specific gnomAD v4 genetic ancestry groups with AF, AC, AN, and homozygous alternate counts.
- Explain that gnomAD genetic ancestry groups are broad aggregate analytical labels, not geographic populations.
- Show the surrounding 1 kb constraint interval as a two-ended -10 to +10 thermometer, centered at expected variation, with exact Z, observed, expected, and o/e values.
- Label constraint provenance as gnomAD v3.1.2 and mark the published Z=2.18 and Z=4.0 thresholds.
- Show the ClinVar aggregate classification, review status, release date, variation ID, and deduplicated conditions with MedGen links and supporting-submission counts.
- Generic condition labels remain available but sort below named conditions.
- Download displayed data exports exactly the validated evidence shown in the panel.
- Missing evidence sections render explicit unavailable states; no values are synthesized.
- Add contract, normalizer, component, accessibility, download, and end-to-end tests plus a committed reproducible review figure.

## Engineering interface

- Bump the immutable external gnomAD cache schema and extend scripts/fetch_external_variant_info.py.
- Validate the expanded payload in website/src/atlas/contracts.ts.
- Render the evidence navigator in website/src/components/atlas/ExternalInfoPanel.tsx while preserving the gnomAD/dbSNP source switch.

## Assumptions and refusal conditions

- External gnomAD and ClinVar evidence is contextual only and never changes the serving-path science.
- ClinVar assertions are not diagnoses.
- gnomAD ancestry groups are not geographic origin labels and must not be mapped as such.
- API identity mismatches, malformed counts, missing denominators, or invalid coordinates are hard errors during cache generation.
- Consumers are the More info panel and its reviewed JSON download.
