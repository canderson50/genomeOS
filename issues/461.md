## Problem

Since #456, the globe and Columbus view are drawn centred in the open area beside the desktop controls panel. The flat 2D map is not shifted: the controls panel still covers its left side.

Cesium resets the orthographic frustum on every 2D view change, so #456's frustum offset does not carry over to 2D.

## What to do

- Apply the same left-obstruction offset in 2D. Either re-apply it after each orthographic frustum reset, or offset the 2D camera's centre instead.
- Keep picking exact in 2D: a click selects the cell drawn under the pointer, as #456 pinned for the globe.

## Done when

- In 2D on desktop, the map's view centre sits in the open area beside the controls, matching the globe's shift.
- Picks in 2D select the cell drawn under the pointer.
- The phone layout is unchanged (no shift).
