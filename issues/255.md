## Body

This is the genotype acquisition/count-preparation continuation of the owner-authorized global AF program #189, consuming the frozen windows and successful index preflight from #254. It does not replace the original single-block B0/B0H experiment or admit a new real B0H fit before calibration passes.

Scientific objective: prepare traceable reference-panel development counts over the preselected autosomal windows while preserving source populations, sample stages, allele orientation, callable denominators and the original QC sensitivities. Broader physical coverage is not proof of independent loci, resident geography or global predictive skill.

Measurable output: a complete window/acquisition ledger, immutable per-source range and header/sample evidence, and the two cohort stages × called/quality count inputs with explicit window/dependency sidecars. The retained pilot inputs and outputs remain unchanged. All selected windows remain accounted for, including missing/unavailable acquisitions, no records, no PASS SNPs and AN=0; those states never become fabricated zero-frequency variants.

Engineering boundary: bounded generation-pinned public byte acquisition through scripts/gcloud_repo.py, native bgzip/tabix/bcftools extraction, pure count/QC derivation and thin artifact writers. Consume the strict #254 manifest, byte plan and retained verified indexes. Keep ReferenceCount and production P0/P1 schemas unchanged. No network, filesystem or environment access enters pure scientific functions.

Acceptance evidence:

- Independently reviewed, complete #254 preflight before any planned VCF bytes; consume its exact coordinates, source generations, ranges and hard 25 GiB planned-transfer cap. No window replacement, source fallback, automatic prefix expansion or unlogged retry. Record actual request bytes and partial/failure outcomes separately from planned/unique ranges.
- Bounded exact range reads with retained hashes and source identity; explicitly incomplete sparse staging must never be presented as a whole-object download or assigned a verified whole-VCF checksum.
- Qualified header/allele/sample joins for each source. Reconstruct the 4,117 technical-QC and 4,094 paper-ancestry cohorts from the same frozen metadata and release-specific exclusions; preserve all 80 literal population identities and known dependencies. Do not use display-name collisions as grouping keys or infer resident coordinates/dates.
- Preserve the original PASS biallelic A/C/G/T SNP and complete diploid dosage rules, missing calls excluded from AN, and distinct called versus quality sensitivities (GQ ≥20, DP ≥10, heterozygote each-allele AD/DP ≥0.2). Unknown/invalid encodings and unsupported fields refuse. Do not substitute INFO aggregates or a phased callset.
- Exact synthetic hand controls plus independent native cohort-total AC/AN checks at every selected retained variant; test row/sample/allele identity, orientation, QC/missingness, duplicate records, interval endpoints and source failures. Clearly state which population/quality checks are independently validated and which rely on the declared derivation.
- Immutable manifest-last artifacts with executable provenance validation, full planned-window accounting and explicit incomplete/refused outcomes. Do not use an incomplete acquisition to claim a completed benchmark dataset.
- Focused tests, smoke, lint, contract/module/privacy gates, full CI and an independent implementation/evidence review before a dedicated PR is called complete. No individual-level or restricted derived dataset is committed/uploaded.

Assumptions/refusals/consumers: the same gnomAD v3.1.2 original HGDP+1KG dense adjusted callset and its previously audited cohort metadata are required. Source/panel rights and research-only status remain binding; P1/publication eligibility stays false. Future development benchmarks must distinguish population holdouts from whole-window/chromosome holdouts and retain participant, kinship, discovery and QC dependencies. Exact acquisition, cache, native-tool and artifact contracts must be frozen before retrieval. This issue prepares data; model comparison, geographic qualification, external confirmation and promotion remain separate gates.

Design references: Atlas §§4–8,12; global AF plan WP0/WP1; #254; existing original-callset count and cohort-dependency qualification notes. The locally recovered cohort metadata matches its original hashes and reconstructs both saved sample lists byte-for-byte; this is preservation evidence, not newly qualified geography or independent sampling.
