## Body

The live phenotype manifest downloaded 2026-09-16 has 7,223 rows and `aws_path` / `aws_path_tabix`, with S3 URIs. `ingest_phenotype_manifest` only reads `aws_link` / `aws_link_tabix`, silently storing null source pointers for this release. Association ingestion also constructs `<sumstats URI>.tbi`, although the manifest places indexes in a separate `sumstats_flat_files_tabix` prefix.

Reproduce: download https://pan-ukb-us-east-1.s3.amazonaws.com/sumstats_release/phenotype_manifest.tsv.bgz and inspect/import its header. Expected: preserve explicit source pointers from the identified manifest schema; reject incomplete/ambiguous source pairs, never guess a Tabix location. This violates the strict source/provenance contract and prevents #282's real-data bootstrap.

Scope: support the two explicit source column schemas, require unambiguous pairs, preserve the manifest Tabix URI, validate real downloaded bytes against declared size/MD5, and record SHA-256 provenance. Keep tests synthetic and isolated; production inputs are real public Pan-UKB aggregate data. No methods or science gates change.
