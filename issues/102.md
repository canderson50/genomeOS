## Body

§6's `surfaces` schema names `post_mean` as the central estimate. Rendering it produced a **sickle-cell hotspot across Finland and northern Scandinavia** at 0.20–0.25 allele frequency, in a region with zero observations within 800 km.

## Measured, on the real fit

| place | mean | median | sd | observations < 800 km |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Finland | 0.206 | **0.108** | 0.233 | **0** |
| N Sweden | 0.253 | **0.144** | 0.261 | **0** |
| Madagascar | 0.127 | **0.107** | 0.087 | **0** |
| Nigeria | 0.133 | **0.129** | 0.029 | 28 |
| Ghana | 0.178 | **0.170** | 0.041 | 16 |
| India (Odisha) | 0.004 | **0.004** | 0.003 | 6 |

Where data is dense, mean ≈ median and sd is small. Where data is absent, sd reaches 0.26 **on a quantity bounded in [0, 1]**, and the mean runs to nearly double the median.

Allele frequency is bounded and the inverse-logit link gives its posterior a long right tail wherever the latent field is uncertain. The mean of a right-skewed bounded quantity is dragged upward — so **the less data a region has, the higher its rendered value**. The map's brightness ends up anti-correlated with evidence, which is the exact inversion §4 exists to prevent.

## Piel et al. hit this and documented it

From their supplementary appendix, fitting the 2010 inverse-logit model to this dataset:

> …the best fitting values result in **implausibly long right-hand tails** for the predictive distribution… strikingly high allele frequencies (**greater than 40%**) were predicted with small but practically significant probability… the long right-hand tail **contained enough mass to skew all of the standard summary statistics**. For example, **the predicted mean in some areas exceeded 5%, which is higher than all but the observed values in the dataset**.

Their Web Table 4 is explicitly "based on **median** estimates", which is independently what #93 found when extracting their table: medians with IQRs.

## Proposed

- §6's `surfaces` and `burden` schemas carry **`post_median` as the central estimate**, with `post_mean` retained only as a diagnostic — the gap between them is a useful skew warning.
- Carry `q25`/`q75` as well, which #92 needs anyway for like-for-like §8 scoring against published IQRs.
- `predict()` already returns all of these; the schemas and §6 need to catch up.

**Switching to the median reduced but did not remove the artifact** — Scandinavia still renders ~0.10–0.14 against a truth near zero. That residual is the link function, tracked separately in #103.
