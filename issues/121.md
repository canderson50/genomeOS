## Body

Found by screening ten HLA alleles from AFND (#40's sweep). `cohort_sd` was the worst-mixing
parameter in **four of five** convergence failures:

```
hla:drb1-15-01   r_hat 1.123   ESS 23   (worst: cohort_sd)
hla:drb1-13-01   r_hat 1.083   ESS 39   (worst: cohort_sd)
hla:drb1-10-01   r_hat 1.075   ESS 64   (worst: cohort_sd)
hla:drb1-13-02   r_hat 1.062   ESS 70   (worst: cohort_sd)
```

The cause is structural. AFND publishes **one study per population**, so `cohort_id ==
population_id` and the term has one level per observation. That is not §7.1d's study-level effect;
it is an observation-level overdispersion term competing with the beta-binomial `concentration`
for the same residual, and the two are not jointly identifiable.

`map_surveys`' docstring already says exactly this about the alternative it rejected: *"keying
cohorts by site would give one cohort level per observation, which is not a cohort effect at all
— it is an observation-level overdispersion term, unidentifiable as the study-level effect §7.1d
wants and free to absorb the spatial signal the GP exists to explain."* MAP has 419 cohorts for
1,071 surveys; AFND has 1:1.

**Fixed narrowly in c7beae4**: `fit_surface` estimates the term only when there is within-cohort
replication, warns otherwise, and records `beta_cohort_applied`.

## What still needs deciding

1. **Is dropping right, or should it be pooled?** A source with no study accession might still
   have *implicit* grouping — by publication, by country, by submitting laboratory. AFND's
   population pages carry `Author(s)` and `Publication details`, which the harvest does not
   currently collect and which would give real study-level grouping.
2. **Should the adapter refuse to set `cohort_id` at all** rather than setting it to the
   population and having the fit detect the degeneracy downstream? An explicit
   `cohort_id = None` meaning "this source cannot group" is more honest than a value that looks
   like grouping and is not.
3. **Does overdispersion still get absorbed correctly** with the term gone? The beta-binomial
   `concentration` should take it, but that is asserted rather than measured.

`wants-expert-review` on (3) in particular.
