## Body

On a 390×844 phone the `/app/` header overflows to 443 px, so the browser zooms the page out. The control dock covers 52% of the globe even with every section collapsed, and the inspector raises coverage to 91.5%. The Cesium explorer design §7.3 promised "a top entity selector plus collapsible bottom sheets", but that was never implemented.

**Scope:** Part A of the design spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-atlas-mobile-fast-load-gcs-design.md` (§A.1–A.3):
- no horizontal overflow or zoom-out at 360/390/412 px;
- the dataset selector at the top;
- a controls bottom sheet (peek/half/full, drag, keyboard);
- the inspector and More info as a second sheet;
- the legend strip and credits docked above the sheet;
- one Escape stack;
- WCAG 2.2 2.5.8 target sizes.

**Acceptance:** at peek, at least 65% of the explorer hit-tests to the canvas and the central region is all canvas. All A.3 Playwright tests and axe pass in Pixel 7, 360×780 and 390×844.

Related: #172 (transition and progress rules are unchanged).
