## Body

## Scientific objective and product claim

Prevent a spatial-GP benchmark fold from becoming admissible evidence when posterior sampling has not met its declared convergence requirements. A completed fold must mean both that prediction finished and that the fit passed retained, reviewable sampler diagnostics.

## Observed behavior

The frozen five-fold HbS `B2-current` run at source `d98794b18b64e230cbdbae8b23d37488a42de804` returned exit 0 and labeled all five folds `completed`. Its terminal log emitted the PyMC warning `R-hat > 1.01` once for every fit, and the fifth fit reported one divergence after tuning. The final checkpoint, manifest and summary retain neither numerical R-hat/ESS values nor per-fold divergence counts, so the warning cannot be localized or audited from the immutable scientific artifact.

Terminal run-log SHA-256: `03d8fe39843df71afb30ec2e5c30319433fbb28efdf2adfd801c5d0b4e4c8a1c`. The result report in draft PR #332 records the warning counts and leaves promotion unset.

## Expected measurable output and acceptance evidence

- Each spatial-GP fold retains numerical convergence diagnostics for every required parameter, at least maximum R-hat, minimum bulk/tail ESS, and divergence count, bound into the checkpoint artifact.
- A fold that violates the declared thresholds is `failed` with a specific reason and emits no admissible predictions; it may not be serialized as `completed` merely because sampling and prediction returned.
- Checkpoint resume preserves that terminal failure exactly and cannot selectively retry it under the same scientific identity.
- Tests exercise converged, R-hat-failed, ESS-failed and divergent fits without weakening thresholds.

## Engineering component and interface

Add an explicit convergence record/refusal boundary to the pure spatial-GP fold result in `genomeos/validation/spatial_gp_benchmark.py`, carry it through `genomeos/validation/spatial_gp_checkpoint.py`, and expose it in `scripts/benchmark_spatial_gp.py`. Keep fitting offline and preserve the existing prediction and summary contracts for genuinely completed folds.

## Assumptions, refusals and consumers

This issue does not authorize changing priors, sampler budgets, target acceptance, splits or promotion thresholds in response to the observed result. The current B2 output remains useful descriptive development evidence but is not convergence-admissible promotion evidence. Downstream consumers are #189, #331 and any later spatial challenger comparison.
