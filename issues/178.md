## Requested change

- reduce text in the left-hand Atlas options panel by 20%
- reduce text in the right-side Modeled Estimate / Measured Observation inspector by 20%
- condense that inspector horizontally while preserving readability and all presented fields

## Contract

1. **Scientific/product claim:** dense controls and evidence remain legible without consuming as
   much of the map viewport.
2. **Acceptance evidence:** computed typography is approximately 80% of its current size in both
   scoped panels; the inspector uses less horizontal space; browser tests preserve every label,
   value, focus target, and responsive no-overflow contract.
3. **Engineering interface:** Atlas control-panel and inspector CSS only, with focused browser
   assertions where stable.
4. **Assumptions/refusals/consumers:** do not shrink global navigation, dataset descriptions,
   dialogs, touch targets, or the external gnomAD panel; do not remove or abbreviate scientific
   fields.
