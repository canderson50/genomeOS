## Body

The private E4 native qualification helper fails its normal parent-exit self-test on Linux. The helper creates its ready pipe with `os.pipe()` and passes its descriptor through raw `os.execv()` without making it inheritable. Python closes non-inheritable descriptors during exec, so the native launcher cannot acknowledge protection.

Reproduced using the unchanged `e4_worker_bootstrap_v2.py` and `e4_parent_death_launcher_v2.c` in an offline local Linux arm64 container, Python 3.11.16 with a C compiler. Returned outcomes: `normal_parent_exit=false`, `setup_race=true`, `orphan_remaining=false`, `production_source_bound=true`, `test_binary_distinct=true`. This is local native evidence; it does not qualify the intended Linux x86-64 GPU target.

Expected: a verified protection acknowledgement followed by normal intermediary-parent exit and child SIGKILL; setup-race refusal and complete child cleanup remain required. A missing acknowledgement or forced cleanup must never count as a successful parent-death test.

The qualification helper also needs to test the retained production binary, since its existing temporary builds cannot establish behavior of the exact binary selected for E4. Keep the accepted scoring/native source and historical results immutable, and repair this through a narrowly reviewed qualification helper.

Advances #189 and the existing count-scoring qualification effort; no probability arithmetic, numerical thresholds, benchmark workload, or public runtime behavior should change.

The earlier local attempt used a non-executable temporary mount and is retained separately as an environment failure. The reported outcome above comes from the corrected executable temporary mount.
