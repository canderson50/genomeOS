## Body

1KG labels such as GBR and ASW are diaspora or urban *sampling* sites, not ancestral origins. Each needs `location_type` set deliberately, not by default — getting this wrong puts African-ancestry allele frequencies in Mississippi.

@bschilder  a scoping note on this one before anyone builds it. Having read the recruitment criteria, I think it hinges on a definition rather than on code.

### Nothing downstream reads `location_type` yet

The registry schema requires it (`sampling` / `ancestral` / `inferred`), and the HGDP, AFND and WBBC adapters set it. But no observation, surface or burden code consumes it. So marking a population correctly wouldn't, by itself, change where its frequencies land in a fit.

### What the populations' own recruitment criteria say

Coriell's collection page for each 1000 Genomes population records the recruitment criterion. Grouped by what the criterion documents (25 of 26 read; I couldn't reach CEU's page):

- **Grandparents born at the sampling place**, so the place is documented at two generations:
  - ACB: at least 3 of 4 grandparents African Caribbean and born in Barbados;
  - ASW: four African American grandparents "born in the same general area of the Southwest USA";
  - GBR: all four grandparents born in the same rural area;
  - IBS: born in the province with all four grandparents born there (50 provinces);
  - FIN: at least 3 of 4 grandparents born in Finland;
  - PUR: at least 6 of 8 great-grandparents Puerto Rican, by place of birth.
- **Grandparents born in a country, sampled in one city within it:**
  - CLM: Medellín, four grandparents born in Colombia;
  - PEL: Lima, four grandparents born in Peru;
  - JPT: Tokyo, donors told the aim was grandparents all from Japan, not asked.
- **Grandparents born in another country:**
  - MXL: Los Angeles, at least 3 of 4 grandparents born in Mexico.
- **Grandparents of a named ethnic or language identity, no birthplace recorded:**
  - BEB, CDX (Xishuangbanna), CHB (Beijing Normal University community), CHS (Hunan and Fujian), ESN, KHV (Ho Chi Minh City), LWK (Webuye), MSL, TSI (a town near Florence), YRI (Ibadan);
  - GWD (Mandinka parents of at least two generations);
  - GIH (Houston, at least 3 of 4 Gujarati grandparents, but "no attempt was made to clarify the meaning that donors attributed to their self-reported Gujarati identity").
- **Self and parents' identity only:**
  - ITU and STU (Telugu and Sri Lankan Tamil, sampled in the UK);
  - PJL (Lahore).

### This changes the premise of the issue

By the rule `afnd.py` already uses ("grandparents live at same location" → `ancestral`), ASW, ACB and GBR are sampled where their grandparents were born. The design's §4 example names GBR and ASW as diaspora sampling sites, but at grandparent depth neither is. Only MXL is documented as sampled outside its grandparents' birthplace. GIH, ITU and STU have an identity, not a place.

So the first decision is a definition: **what does `ancestral` mean?**
- **(1) Two generations,** matching AFND. Then ASW stays at the southwest US, and most rows are well defined from Coriell's criteria. Only MXL, GIH, ITU and STU need special handling.
- **(2) Pre-migration origin.** Then ASW and ACB would point to West Africa, and nearly every population's ancestral place becomes a judgment, not something recorded.

### Then, for the rows whose origin isn't their sampling place

- **(a)** record the sampling site as `sampling`, and exclude `sampling` rows from surface fits. They stay visible as measured observations;
- **(b)** record the documented origin region with a wide radius: Mexico for MXL as `ancestral` (birthplace is documented), but Gujarat, Telangana/Andhra or northern Sri Lanka only as `inferred`, since identity isn't a birthplace;
- **(c)** carry both, which is a registry schema change (and a `contract/` diff).

Whichever option is chosen, something downstream has to act on the flag, or none of it changes the surfaces.

### Coordinates and radii have to be curated

IGSR's API gives one display point per population, and several are markers rather than locations:
- GBR, ITU and STU all sit at the same point (Birmingham);
- CLM ("Medellín") sits at Bogotá;
- ESN sits at Abuja, not in Edo State.

#21 found the gnomAD metadata gives no footprint either. So every coordinate and `uncertainty_radius_km` needs a documented rule, at scales the criteria suggest: a town for TSI, a province pair for CHS, a country for FIN, IBS, CLM, PEL and JPT.

### Effort, once decided

- A curated 26-row input (coordinate, location type, radius with evidence, provenance, aliases, notice), citing the Coriell criterion per row: a few hours of popgen review, plus CEU.
- A `registry/sources/` module with the same `load(path, registry_version)` interface as `hgdp.py`, plus a fixture, tests and a `build_registry.py` flag: about a day.
- The downstream rule (option a) or the schema change (option c) on top.
