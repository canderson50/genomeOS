## Body

## Product claim

The selected-cell Google Maps control should identify the exact destination before it is opened. Display the cell centroid longitude and latitude as the clickable chooser text instead of the generic `Choose map view` label.

## Measurable output and acceptance evidence

- The chooser trigger shows longitude and latitude on two lines, each to four decimal places with axis labels.
- Activating the coordinate trigger still reveals both Centroid and Polygon options.
- The trigger retains an accessible name that explains that it opens Google Maps choices.
- Browser coverage asserts the visible coordinate pair and both existing targets.

## Engineering interface

Use the centroid already computed from the selected H3 cell in `InspectorPanel`; do not introduce a second coordinate calculation or alter either destination URL.

## Assumptions, refusal conditions, and consumers

The displayed coordinate is the H3 cell centroid, not an observation location. Polygon remains a separate option. Consumers are Atlas users inspecting inferred cells.
