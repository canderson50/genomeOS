## Body

## The evidence

Two consecutive literature-corpus PRs from two different contributors reimplemented a documented, existing tool.

`docs/literature-evidence-curation.md`, "Minimum curation workflow", step 1:

> Snapshot discovery with `scripts/fetch_pubmed_manifest.py`; every candidate starts pending.

That script exists, takes `--executed-at` as a required argument, keeps network I/O behind one function, validates that `esearchresult.count` matches the returned id list, and emits every candidate as `pending` so that screening is a separate immutable manifest version.

- **#188** wrote its own `capture_searches()` instead: wall-clock timestamps (so search ids change every run), no payload validation, a hardcoded personal Windows path, and screening decisions fused into the capture step.
- **#156** shipped no builder at all, so its 12 records and 228 field rows cannot be regenerated ([#237](https://github.com/bschilder/genomeOS/issues/237)).

Neither contributor was careless — both PRs are careful, well-documented work with correct science. When two out of two independent attempts miss the same section, that is a discoverability failure, not a contributor failure. I also reviewed #188 twice against the wrong baseline before finding the script myself, which is the same failure from the reviewing side.

## Why it is not reaching people

**There is no contributor entry point.** No `CONTRIBUTING.md`, no `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`, no issue templates. `.github/` contains only `workflows/`. `AGENTS.md` is the real contract, but GitHub surfaces `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the PR template in the flow where a contributor is actually about to submit — and both contributors here are working from forks, so they never see repo-local conventions unless something points at them.

**The guidance is split by length, not by audience.** The rules a corpus submitter needs are spread across `AGENTS.md` (361 lines), `docs/literature-evidence-curation.md` (221) and the design spec (748). The coverage-totals-must-reconcile rule that both PRs got wrong lives in §8 of the 748-line spec, not in the 221-line doc a curator reads.

**Step 1 reads as a suggestion.** It names the script but does not say it is mandatory, does not say not to write your own, and does not explain *why* discovery and screening are separate manifest versions — which is the design point both PRs collapsed.

## What to add

Smallest change that would have prevented both, in priority order:

1. **A PR template** with the gate checklist (`ruff check .`, `freeze_contract.py --check`, `check_module_size.py`, `check_private_files.py`, `smoke.py`, `pytest`) and, for data PRs, "used `scripts/fetch_pubmed_manifest.py` for discovery" as an explicit line. #188's blocking lint failure would have been caught by the contributor.
2. **A short `CONTRIBUTING.md`** that does nothing but route: read `AGENTS.md`, then the doc for your workstream, and check open *and* closed issues first. Keep it short enough to stay true.
3. **In the curation doc, make step 1 binding and say why.** State that discovery uses the existing script, that screening is a later manifest revision over an all-pending snapshot, and that a bespoke fetcher is not acceptable. Add that the raw ESearch payloads get committed alongside the manifest so it can be replayed — currently documented nowhere, though `docs/audits/lct-rs4988235-pilot.json` models the practice by recording its exact `"command"`.
4. **Move the coverage-reconciliation rule into the curation doc**, next to the search-manifest columns. Two for two on getting it wrong.

## Out of scope

Not proposing a rewrite of `AGENTS.md` or the design spec. They are accurate; the problem is the route to them. Also not proposing that engineering-side findings from the same review ([#231](https://github.com/bschilder/genomeOS/issues/231), [#234](https://github.com/bschilder/genomeOS/issues/234), [#235](https://github.com/bschilder/genomeOS/issues/235)) move into contributor docs — those belong where they are.

## Acceptance evidence

- A new corpus slice can be submitted correctly by someone reading only `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the curation doc, without prior knowledge of this repo.
- The curation doc states the discovery step is mandatory and explains the discovery/screening split.
- The PR template's checklist covers every gate `AGENTS.md` lists as mandatory.
- The next corpus PR uses `fetch_pubmed_manifest.py`. That is the actual test.
