## Body

## Problem

After the map appears, three downloads start together and share the connection:
- the country borders: `ne-50m-admin-0.geojson`, 1.03 MB gzip;
- the f64 detail tier: 3.0 MB gzip for HbS, fetched with `priority: 'low'` after `atlas:surface-visible`;
- the six starfield textures: about 870 KB, loaded when the sky box is revealed at ready.

On a phone this slows the borders, which every visitor sees, behind a 3 MB file that only feeds the hover readout and inspector, and behind cosmetic textures.

## Change

After `surface-visible`, load in a fixed order, each starting when the one before settles:

1. **Borders** (Natural Earth context).
2. **The detail tier** for the displayed artifact.
3. **The starfield** (sky-box reveal).

Ordering rules:
- A failure or stall at any step never blocks the next one.
- On a later artifact switch, the new detail tier starts as soon as that surface is visible, because the borders are already loaded.

## Acceptance

- The fixed order is pinned by tests: a unit test of the sequencing, and an e2e check of request start order.
- Every failure and stall path is tested.
- The spec (fast-load design §B.2 fetch table and §B.6.9) states the order.
- The cold-load harness shows no regression in observations-visible or surface-visible, and records when borders and exact values become available, before and after.
