## Body

## Scientific objective

Build a publication-evidence corpus for G6PD that preserves X-linked denominators, sex/sample
strata, and the distinction between genotype measurements and aggregate enzyme-deficiency
phenotypes. This supplies evidence for #46; it does not claim G6PD parity is complete.

## Measurable output and acceptance evidence

- Publish a versioned discovery/screening manifest and curate a predeclared set of Howes-linked or
  otherwise original population measurements with all 19 field-evidence decisions.
- For variant-specific rows, verify GRCh38 allele orientation and record exact called AC/AN. A
  male-only X-linked denominator may use `allele_denominator_from_hemizygous_males` only with the
  required explicit evidence. Mixed-sex samples require sex/genotype-level accounting rather than
  a diploid fallback.
- Keep enzyme-deficiency phenotype observations separate from single-variant allele observations;
  do not assign the phenotype to one of the roughly 200 causal G6PD alleles.
- Resolve every population through P0, preserve independently recruited sample strata, and publish
  a compact comparison to the Howes reference inputs/patterns.
- Run the P1 round trip, then exercise #46's X-linked P2/P3 acceptance. #46 closes only when its
  independently declared parity tolerances pass.

## Engineering interface

Use the frozen literature evidence contracts and `publications.load(...)` from #149. If the
existing phenotype observation contract cannot represent a required measurement honestly, stop
and propose a versioned measurement-type change rather than forcing it into allele AC/AN.

## Assumptions and refusal conditions

- Never infer sex composition, ploidy, assay, sampling design, called denominator, or cohort from a
  population label or publication era.
- A percent/decimal frequency is not an exact allele count even when multiplication happens to
  yield an integer.
- Missing P0 geography/radius, original source location, independent verification, or checked
  reuse terms refuses promotion.
- Clinical, screening, and population samples remain distinct ascertainment designs.

## Expert review

Clinical-genetics/population-genetics review should check X-linked denominator derivations,
female heterozygote handling, variant-versus-phenotype boundaries, and comparability to Howes.

Depends on #149. Feeds #46.
