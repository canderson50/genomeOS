## Problem

`reduce_b0h_study` is CPU-bound for minutes on the committed 1,938-case empty
campaign fixture. On 2026-09-14, the test
`test_empty_campaign_accounts_all_records_without_science` took about six
minutes to complete five deterministic reductions on an Apple M4. The focused
runner suite remained on one CPU core at about 99% utilization.

The reducer eagerly expands planned descriptive cells into many validated
`DescriptiveAggregate` objects even when no descriptive rows exist. This makes
local verification and campaign reduction unnecessarily slow.

## Scientific contract

1. **Objective:** preserve honest planned/actual/failed denominators for every
   declared B0H descriptive cell without changing any calibration decision.
2. **Acceptance evidence:** benchmark the committed empty-campaign reduction
   before/after; preserve identical `reduction_bytes`; pass the reducer, runner,
   full repository, smoke, contract, module-size, and privacy gates.
3. **Component/interface:** optimize the pure offline
   `genomeos.validation.heterogeneity_reduction.reduce_b0h_study` path; do not
   change its public signature or version-1 output contract.
4. **Assumptions/refusals/consumers:** all 1,938 ordered case identities and all
   planned aggregate rows remain explicit; malformed inputs still fail closed;
   no serving-path inference, schema default, retry, threshold, or scientific
   method changes. The runner CLI and later B0H comparison/report layers consume
   the exact output.

## Expected behavior

Use precomputed/vectorized indexing and avoid repeated general-purpose model
validation for values derived from already validated inputs where an equivalent
explicit invariant check can preserve fail-closed behavior. Do not remove
planned empty cells or weaken `reduction_bytes` validation.
