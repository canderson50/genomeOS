## Problem

Since #456, desktop draws the globe centred in the open area to the right of the controls panel. The inspector, which opens in the right rail when a visitor selects a cell, is not counted as an obstruction.

On desktops narrower than about 1,340 px, an open inspector covers the shifted globe centre. At 1280 px wide it sits over the point the camera looks at. That is where a selected cell, and the next one a visitor is likely to pick, are drawn.

## What to do

- Count the open inspector as a right-hand obstruction, using the same mechanism (`AtlasSceneController.setLeftObstruction`, `src/atlas/view-offset.ts`). Centre the globe between the controls panel and the inspector while the inspector is open.
- Ease the shift when the inspector opens and closes, so the globe does not jump. Under reduced motion it can move at once.
- Keep the picking and wheel-zoom guarantees #456 pinned.

## Done when

- At 1280×800 with the inspector open, the selected cell's centre is visible and not covered.
- The existing globe-offset unit tests and the pick tests still pass, with new cases for the right-hand obstruction.
