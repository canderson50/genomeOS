## Body

Found while checking what the AFND harvest actually contains. Two faults, one cosmetic-looking and
one substantive.

## AFND is four data families, not one

The harvest carries a `group` column stating which, and the adapter ignores it:

| `group` | rows | genes | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hla` | 111,399 | 8 | classical HLA alleles |
| `kir` | 6,714 | 18 | KIR genes: 2DL1-5, 2DS1-5, 3DL1-3, 3DS1, 2DP1, 3DP1 |
| `cyt` | 4,517 | 29 | cytokine promoter variants: IL-6, IL-10, TNFalpha, TGFbeta1, IFNgamma, IL-1beta and more |
| `mic` | 872 | 2 | MICA / MICB |

109 distinct locus strings in total.

## Fault 1: every id claims to be HLA

`afnd_frequencies.variant_id()` hardcodes the prefix:

```python
return f"hla:{re.sub(r'-+', '-', slug)}"
```

So `KIR2DL1` becomes `hla:2dl1`, `MICA*A4` becomes `hla:mica-a4`, and an IL-6 promoter genotype
becomes `hla:il-6-174`. At `min_populations=30` only 4 variants over 121 observations are affected
today, because the coverage filter removes most non-HLA rows — but the id is a false statement about
what was measured, which §4 makes a bug rather than a naming preference. Lower the threshold and it
grows.

## Fault 2: three of the four families are not allele frequencies

This is the substantive half, and the mislabelling was hiding it.

- **Cytokine rows are diploid genotypes, not alleles.** `AIF-1/ -932 CC` and `AIF-1/ -932 CT` are
  genotypes; **0 of 4,517** cytokine rows are gene-presence rows, they are all genotype-level. So
  `ac = round(af * 2n)` is a category error on every one of them: it reconstructs allele counts from
  a genotype frequency.
- **KIR is mostly gene presence/absence.** KIR genes are copy-number variable, and **3,790 of 6,714**
  KIR rows have `allele == gene` (`2DL1`, `2DL1`), which is the fraction of individuals *carrying the
  gene*, not an allele frequency. The remaining 2,924 are allele-level (`3DL1*007`) and are
  legitimate allele frequencies.
- **MIC is fine.** 872 rows, all allele-level, same measurement type as HLA.

This is also why AFND publishes both `indivs_over_n` and `alleles_over_2n`: the first is a carrier
frequency and the second an allele frequency, and which one is meaningful differs by family. The
adapter reads one of them for everything.

## Fix

1. Carry `group` through the adapter and prefix ids by family — `kir:`, `cyt:`, `mic:`. Needs
   `VARIANT_ID_PATTERN` extended in `observations/schema.py` and `contract/` refrozen.
2. **Refuse the whole `cyt` family** in this adapter rather than mis-reconstructing counts from
   genotypes. A refusal is a valid output (§9); a wrong number is not.
3. **Refuse KIR gene-presence rows** (`allele == gene`) for the same reason, and keep the 2,924
   allele-level KIR rows with a `kir:` prefix.

Both refusals are counted and reported like every other, so the retained/refused arithmetic stays
auditable.

What to do with the refused families is #133, filed separately so this patch does not silently
decide it.
