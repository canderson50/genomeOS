## Body

The existing count scorer refused some high-concentration inputs before diagnostics could complete. This draft adds a complete-support beta-binomial recurrence and independent numerical validation, but the candidate **does not meet its declared probability-accuracy contract and is not ready to merge**. Both CPU and GPU validation retained 11 mismatched laws; neither passing log checks nor backend parity waives those failures.

Advances #194 and #211 without closing either. Implements the numerical/validation responsibilities of Atlas design §§7–8 and the scoring prerequisite of the global AF modeling plan. It does not qualify a new model, calibration study, population-frequency surface or clinical layer.

The recurrence computes below/equal/above partitions with bounded support/draw/row batches, preserves analytical endpoints and mixture quantile ties, and keeps the larger-count legacy domain separate. The validation and plotting adapters bind exact sources and inputs, preserve failed/refused/incomplete outcomes, and distinguish the original execution records from subsequently improved accounting. Public documentation now discloses the failed candidate and observed runtime regressions.

Every ordinary public/partition log check passed; mismatches concern subnormal masses/tails. These executions used f8e7afd. - Final adapter tests: 32 passed; related controls: 11 passed. Smoke, Ruff, frozen contracts, module size and privacy checks passed.
