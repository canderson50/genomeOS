## What this advances

Environmental grids and temporal-genetics methods need qualified measurement and validation boundaries before they can inform resident allele-frequency models. This PR records those boundaries and adds a reproducible diagnostic of CHELSA monthly versus annual precipitation storage. Advances #189 WP3/WP7 and Atlas design §§4–9, 12; closes no issue.

Eight companion research notes and focused plan clarifications cover:

- **Terrain/elevation/water:** current CDSE DGED releases, the older AWS COG mirror, and Cesium display assets are distinct products. Preserve surface-model versus bare-earth interpretation, grid/datum semantics, editing/fill masks, accuracy support, and applicable reuse terms.
- **Demographics:** WorldPop candidates differ in modeled dates, constraints, and source lineage. Population weighting, predictor inclusion, and recruitment assumptions remain separate uses. No existing denominator is replaced; the negative-count ingress defect is tracked in #241.
- **Climate and temporal coverage:** present-day normals, historical reconstructions, and ancient climate through human/non-human-primate divergence remain separate tracks. The inspected sources do not establish continuous fine local coverage across that interval. Present location, uncertain ancestral exposure, and site-constant evolutionary context are different inputs.
- **20CR decoding:** inspected parent precipitation values represent three-hour amounts. Some January 1980/1981 checks agree with complete parent intervals; January 1806 discrepancies remain unexplained. Annual checks retain monthly discrepancies despite small signed annual residuals. The original diagnostic tolerances lack retained proof of pre-result specification.
- **CHELSA decoding:** two January V2.1 files were fully acquired and decompressed within the original 512 MiB cap. Predetermined selected values and later zero/nodata controls agree through distinct GDAL and Pillow/LibTIFF readers. Exact object hashes, geometry, explicit packing, and nodata-derived masks are retained; native land/coast support remains unqualified.
- **CHELSA annual consistency:** a same-period audit of twelve monthly precipitation files and bio12 falsifies `annual_raw == floor(sum(monthly_raw)/10)` at **822,830 of 902,015,154 comparable cells (0.0912213%)**, with 846 excluded cells. Residual `10*annual_raw - sum(monthly_raw)` spans -10 through 0, and every formula failure is -10. The public aggregate contains all 3,485 native-block summaries, the histogram, and thirteen exact source identities. Annual physical packing and exact generating lineage remain unresolved.
- **Temporal genetics:** full CLUES2 main-article and supplement readings distinguish direct ancient likelihoods from genealogy inputs, marginal posteriors from joint histories, and selection inference from held-out geographic prediction. Published-source attribution and numerical impact remain open in #261. No stochastic reproduction or temporal implementation is claimed.

## Evidence boundary

All source qualification remains `automated_proposal` / `pending`, with scientific model and publication eligibility false. No climate feature or genetic source is admitted, no fitter or production schema changes, and no allele-frequency gain is claimed. Future comparisons must preserve outcome populations, controls for missingness and spatial structure, source dependence, and independent confirmation.

Only original prose, source links/metadata, whitelisted aggregate diagnostics, the owned plot, and its PNG are included. Raw rasters, downloaded source bodies, participant or genotype records, credentials, and private execution stores remain untracked. The aggregate permits figure reproduction; independent replication of the global calculation still requires the thirteen exact source bodies.
