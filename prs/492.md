## Body

## What users get

These changes apply only when Cesium World Terrain is on. The default smooth globe draws everything as before.

- **Honmoon contour lines now show on the terrain.** Under World Terrain, Honmoon draws its contour lines into the surface laid over the ground, as the smooth globe draws them. Phase 2 drew only a faint fill there. Line-only Honmoon shows the basemap between its lines, as on the smooth globe.
- **No stutter as a new surface fades in.** Each new draped surface draws one frame that cannot be seen (α 0.001) before its 1 s fade, so Cesium compiles the globe shader before the fade instead of during its first frame.
- **Phones keep World Terrain as it is.** Phones were measured against the 96 MiB imagery budget and are well inside it, so none of the design's phone fallbacks was needed: phones keep the same tile cache, outlines in their own layer, borders to level 11, and World Terrain itself.
- **The World Terrain budgets are now checked by the test suite.** On a hardware GPU every budget fails the test when it is missed. Under CI's software renderer only the memory budget is checked, pending the owner's ruling below. The budgets hold on the test fixture's small grid. At production density, one run missed three timing budgets (see owner decision 3).

## Changes per task

- **Task 27: Honmoon shading for texels** (new `geometry/raster/contours.ts`).
  - `honmoonTexel` ports the smooth-globe Honmoon fragment (`scene/surface-appearance.ts`) to one RGBA texel: 36 bands, derivative clamp [0.006, 0.22], fill rgb × 0.55 at α 0.28. It matches the shader oracle within one byte. Two known differences: `Uint8ClampedArray` rounds halves to even, and the shader's 1.35 overshoot saturates at 255.
  - `HONMOON_CONTOUR_BANDS` and `honmoonModeForGeometry` move to the Cesium-free `geometry/surface-buffers.ts` so the worker can use them, and `surface-appearance.ts` re-exports them (phase 4 decision 1). The smooth-globe shader text is identical to `origin/main`'s.
- **Task 28: contours in the surface raster** (`geometry/raster/fill-raster.ts`).
  - With `contours: true`, both Honmoon geometries interpolate the value with the colour and shade each texel with `honmoonTexel`. The shader's `fwidth` becomes the value's change over one texel, the fan triangle's exact gradient from `triangle-scan.ts` (a central difference on a pole-holding row).
  - A tile with no texel of α > 0 is now null, so line-only Honmoon between its lines adds no tile.
  - `contours: true` on a non-Honmoon geometry throws.
  - Parity is checked against a brute-force fan oracle, including across the antimeridian and at a pole.
- **Task 29: shader pre-warm** (`scene/draped/draped-imagery-layer.ts`, `draped-surface-group.ts`).
  - `DrapedImageryLayer.prewarm()` draws a layer for one rendered frame at `PREWARM_ALPHA` (0.001), keeps any α set meanwhile, and settles on destroy.
  - `DrapedSurfaceGroup.isReady()` turns true only once the layers the fade will draw (the surface, and the outlines when edges are on and cells are supported) have loaded and drawn that frame together. A test pins Cesium's shader cache key.
- **Task 30: the budget evaluator** (new `tests/support/terrain-budgets.ts`). `terrainBudgetFailures` turns one measured run into a list of failures. Times round up and fps down, so no value outside a budget rounds back into it. A NaN fails. A run that measured no restyle or no raster sample never passes.
- **Task 31: wiring and the tour** (`scene/surface-groups.ts`, `tests/atlas-terrain-performance.spec.ts`, `tests/atlas-terrain-helpers.ts`, `src/atlas/e2e-hooks.ts`).
  - `drapedSurfaceSpec` sends `contours: honmoonModeForGeometry(geometry) !== 0`.
  - An e2e test shows the posterior SD's contour line crossing the summit.
  - The terrain spec is one tour per project: the smooth globe, World Terrain from the chooser, an orbit, three zoom-ins, relief 1 → 3, a palette and a Honmoon restyle, and Home. It records the peak resident Atlas imagery every frame. It checks that the phone run really is a coarse pointer.
  - The e2e camera-motion hook now compares the camera's pose between drawn frames at 1e-12. Cesium's `moveEnd` uses 1e-15, which the camera's float drift kept failing on a hardware GPU, so `cameraSettled` could wait past 30 s.
- **Tasks 32–35: phone fallbacks.** Skipped: no mobile fallback was needed (see Memory).
- **Task 36: the budgets become assertions.** On a software renderer the test records a "timing budgets skipped" annotation naming the renderer, then asserts what is left. Interaction design §5.2 records the contours, the pre-warm, the memory result and what is asserted.
- **Integration and lane review fixes**: 49709b0 (the fake draped layers gain `prewarm`), 50b6871 (a layer destroyed inside its pre-warm frame writes and requests nothing), 16ad689 (directional rounding), bf3b4b6 (docstrings say how closely the port matches), 531e42f and eb9d7c8 (tests for the derivative clamp and for a pole cell's line width), f5be9d4 (the Honmoon fill constants have one owner), f82dd1a (the pose-based motion hook, and data visible waits for the fade, past the pre-warm frame), 76b900c (§5.2 records the production-density misses).
