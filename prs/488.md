## Body

## What users get

These changes apply when Cesium World Terrain is requested. The default smooth globe draws the same data as before, built the same way.

- **The data lies on the ground.** The inferred surface, its evidence-support masks and its cell outlines are drawn into map tiles and laid over the terrain. Mountains no longer hide the data, and the hillshade from phase 1 lights it. This is the #483 repro, and it now passes.
- **Restyles cross-fade.** After a palette, metric, geometry or support change, the new surface fades in over the old one once its tiles have loaded, or after 3 s at most. The first load fades in over 1 s. With reduced motion, or in a background tab, it shows at once.
- **Some controls do less on World Terrain.** Data elevation is off: the URL keeps the setting, and the control says "Not available with World Terrain". Extruded columns draw as flat hexagons, and that option reads "Columns (smooth globe only)". Honmoon shows its fill; its contours come in phase 4.
- **Loading and failures.** A link with `terrain=world-terrain` drapes the data straight away and never builds the smooth-globe geometry first. If World Terrain cannot load, or a tile fails, the smooth globe comes back with a warning. Picking another terrain clears the warning.
- **Clicks pick the cell under the cursor**, through `globe.pick`, the ground pick from phase 1.

Not draped yet; phase 3 does these: country borders, sampling rings, symbols, labels and the highlight still sit at fixed heights.

## Changes per task

- **Task 6: contracts, protocol and client** (`scene/types.ts`, `worker/protocol.ts`, `worker/client.ts`, `worker/handlers.ts`).
  - Adds the draped contracts: `RasterImageryProvider`, `DrapedImageryLayer`, `DrapedSurfaceGroup` and `DrapedRuntime`.
  - Adds the raster requests, `prepare-raster`, `raster-tile` and `release-raster`, and their client methods.
  - The worker answers them with placeholders until Task 15.
- **Task 7: tiling and grid index** (`geometry/raster/tiling.ts`, `grid-index.ts`).
  - Tiles are 256 × 256 on the geographic scheme, levels 0 to 11, with rows stored south first.
  - The index finds the grid rows in a rectangle, including their ±360° copies. Each row's box is padded by 10%, enough to cover the class III distortion vertices (worst measured: 5.03%).
  - Polar rows and drawn bounds are handled. Bad rectangles and bad rows throw.
- **Task 8: worker raster runtime** (`worker/raster-handlers.ts`).
  - Prepare, tile and release run through a FIFO queue that yields between tiles, with the rasterisers injected.
  - Drawn tiles go back as transferred `ImageBitmap`s made with the pinned options.
  - A cancelled prepare stores nothing. A release waits for the tile in flight, so no `tile-ready` ever follows a `raster-released`.
- **Task 9: provider, imagery layer, drape stats, Cesium pins** (`scene/draped/raster-imagery-provider.ts`, `draped-imagery-layer.ts`, `drape-stats.ts`, `tests/atlas-cesium-internals.test.ts`).
  - The provider keeps at most 8 tiles in flight, resolves a cancelled tile as empty, and raises `tileLoaded`, which requests a render.
  - The layers keep their fixed order above the basemap.
  - Below them sits a whole-globe base guard at α 0. Without it, Cesium makes the first shown layer the base layer and stretches its edge texels over the globe.
  - Readiness is `tilesLoaded` from the second frame, or a 3 s cap.
  - Every Cesium internal the layers rely on is pinned by a test.
- **Task 10: `resolveDrapedView` and the surface controls**. While draped, the controls follow the UI rule: data elevation off and extruded drawn as hexagons, with the labels above.
- **Task 11: ScientificLayers over `SurfaceGroup`** (new `scene/surface-groups.ts`; `RevealWave.progress()`).
  - `#show` gains the draped branches.
  - On a restyle, the outgoing layer stays opaque until it is retired.
  - A layer toggle rebuilds the surface when it changes which cells are baked into the texture.
  - A raster failure goes to `onDrapeFailure`.
  - `waitForReady` counts only time the tab is visible and takes an abort signal (see "For the owner").
- **Task 12: fill and mask rasteriser** (`geometry/raster/fill-raster.ts`, `mask-texels.ts`, `triangle-scan.ts`).
  - Fills for triangles, hexagons and Honmoon are drawn at the legend bytes. A parity test allows at most 1 byte of difference from the smooth-globe path.
  - The Grid and Dot masks use the smooth-globe masks' `st` frame.
  - The hatch is lighter than the plan specified (see "For the owner").
- **Task 13: outline rasteriser** (`geometry/raster/line-raster.ts`). Outlines are anti-aliased at α 0.92. Their width grows from 1.5 to 2 texels between levels 3 and 11, and below level 3 there are none.
- **Task 14: `DrapedSurfaceGroup` and the draped runtime** (`scene/draped/draped-surface-group.ts`, `runtime.ts`).
  - The surface layer's α is 0.9 × surface opacity × fade × progress.
  - Outlines are prepared the first time edges turn on. A new outline style replaces the old one without a gap.
  - Failures are routed as follows: one during `prepare()` rejects it, and any later one, or a provider error, goes to `onError`.
  - A raster with nothing to draw adds no layer.
- **Task 15: worker integration**. The worker imports the raster runtime on the first raster request, so it stays out of the worker's first load. `ATLAS_RASTERISERS` replaces the placeholders, and an import that fails is tried again on the next request.
- **Task 16: scene integration** (`scene/atlas-scene.ts`).
  - The scene drapes as soon as World Terrain is requested. When the newest request settles, it follows the terrain actually on the globe.
  - A failed World Terrain load rebuilds the smooth-globe geometry once.
  - A raster failure reverts the terrain, shows `DRAPE_FAILED_WARNING` under the warning id `'drape'`, and logs the error.
  - The draped runtime is imported once, into its own chunk. The e2e build publishes `__atlasDrapeStats`.
- **Task 17: e2e and the performance report**.

  - `atlas-drape.spec.ts` covers a cold draped load, morphs, restyles, the outlines toggle and the return to the smooth globe.

## For the owner

Each of these needs a decision before phase 2 merges. The branch implements the plan as written; nothing below has been changed in code without a ruling.

1. **The restyle snap.**
   - The plan's Restyle row has the outgoing draped layer stay opaque until it is retired.
   - At the default opacity its resting α is a = 0.9 × 0.58 = 0.522. While the new layer rises over it, the basemap's share falls from 1 − a ≈ 0.48 to about (1 − a)² ≈ 0.23.
   - In the frame the old layer is retired, the share jumps back. The step is a(1 − a) ≈ 25% of (old colour − basemap).
   - Options: accept the snap, or keep the combined coverage constant by giving the outgoing layer opacity (1 − (1 − a)/(1 − a·p)) / a. The second needs the 0.9 surface α exposed through the `DrapedSurfaceGroup` contract.
2. **The hatch colour.**
   - Plan Task 12 sets the draped hatch to #a8b5c9 × 0.5, which is (84, 91, 101).
   - The branch (020dc0f) uses 0.5 × 2^(1/2.2), which is (115, 124, 138), so the hatch matches how the smooth-globe masks look under HDR.
   - The fills follow the spec's "exact legend colour" bytes instead. So a draped tile now mixes two colour targets, and on the smooth globe the fills look lighter than draped.
   - Options: pick one target for draped fills and masks, either the legend bytes or the smooth globe's HDR look, then amend Task 12's expected values.
3. **Readiness in a hidden tab, on the smooth globe too** (c42f302). `waitForReady`'s 30 s idle deadline now counts visible time only, and a wait can be aborted. This applies to chunk groups as well. Before, a smooth-globe build waiting in a background tab hid its group and failed with "Cesium geometry build timed out" after 30 s; now it waits for the tab to come back. I believe this is a fix (the chunk scheduler's stall timeout already works this way), but it is a smooth-globe behaviour change that no phase-2 decision lists.
4. **Smooth-globe budgets**: see "Not run" above.
