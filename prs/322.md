## TL;DR

We tested whether nearby surveys provide a useful simpler estimate of sickle-cell variant frequency. The rules set in advance produced no estimates for the 994 held-out survey results; a more permissive follow-up covered 21.9% and performed worse on zero-count observations. The evidence readers now reject inconsistent or altered results, and a separate follow-up reports where estimates were supported. This remains an incomplete comparator investigation, not a worldwide replacement.

## What this PR contains

This adds the source-neutral B1 local count comparator, with training-only bandwidth selection, explicit geographic refusal and the same count scorer and frozen folds as B0. The branch also contains the merged matched B2 development report (#332) and prospective GP convergence repair (#334).

Review fixes enforce the evidence contract at finalization, before artifact writing, and when reading results for the figure/report. Requested IDs come from the validated plan; emitted/refused IDs must form a unique, disjoint, exhaustive partition; every requested ID has one support row; refused rows have no posterior; scored predictions match emitted support, observed counts and split/cohort/region/variant identity. Completed support must satisfy the declared evidence thresholds and posterior concentration identity. Failed/infeasible results remain visible and unscored. B1 has no checkpoint/resume interface.

The renderer refuses existing/symlink/aliased/nested destinations and publishes complete outputs without replacement; failure at the second publication preserves competing evidence and removes only its own first output. The reader verifies the actual observation hash and size, all required artifact output hashes and sizes, complete manifests and role/nonpublication/split identities, and replays summaries at declared tolerances. It refuses wrong or missing coordinates, incomplete evidence, and altered or independently rehashed contradictions. Infinite and undefined paired scores remain explicit in the JSON and figure annotation.

A new [version 2 follow-up](https://github.com/bschilder/genomeOS/blob/886b6ecd5192adaffc6c1d89eaf419d6abfb7576/docs/research/hbs-local-count-benchmark-2026-09-20-v2.md) reports denominator and distance strata with requested/emitted/refused counts and paired score/coverage summaries. These are explicitly post-hoc descriptive bins. The original September 16 note, aggregate JSON, figure and private row-level artifacts remain unchanged. No model, prior, sampler budget, threshold, split, retry rule or scientific promotion claim was changed.
