## Why IL6 remains pending

The `cyt:il-6-174-c` strand evidence cites rs1800797's plus-strand G allele at **0.750 in TOPMED and 0.859 in 1000 Genomes**. dbSNP build 157 returns:

```
TOPMED        G = 200141/264690 = 0.7561
1000Genomes   G =   4316/5008   = 0.8618
1000G 30X     G =   5507/6404   = 0.8599
```

The verifier also checked esummary and gnomAD; none reproduces 0.750. The strand conclusion remains supported because the observed values are still far from 0.5 and irreconcilable with this repository's observed median of 0.304. The coordinate, build currency, naming citations, Terry 2000 haplotype citation, and rs1800797 reference-allele claim were also checked.

A provenance record whose numbers cannot be reproduced from its named source remains broken even when its qualitative conclusion is correct. The row therefore stays pending.
