## Body

## Problem and resulting behavior

HGDP input could omit spatial support and source provenance and still produce a valid-looking registry because the adapter filled every radius with 50 km and every citation with one hard-coded DOI. The adapter now requires explicit `uncertainty_radius_km` and `provenance` values, preserves literal labels and source/version locators, and refuses malformed or incomplete rows before the build writes output.

The existing adapter signature, canonical schemas, ancestral location type, and CARE notice are preserved. The six-row fixture uses explicitly synthetic radii and locators. The input guide explains migration; legacy four-column inputs require source curation rather than automatic conversion.

## Issues and design

Advances #21 and #219 without closing either: real population-specific support and coordinate-source qualification remain outstanding. Advances #189 WP0. The separately reproduced registry overwrite/version bug is tracked in #262 and addressed by the stacked follow-up PR #265.

Implements Atlas design §6 (required support and provenance), §12 (hard input errors), and §13 (preserved biocultural notice).
