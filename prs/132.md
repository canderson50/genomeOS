## Body

Closes #109.

Runs `scripts/validate_holdout.py` to completion on the 1,071-survey MAP HbS corpus and commits
the results, so the numbers are reviewable rather than living in a terminal. M=150, draws=1000,
tune=1000, target_accept=0.9, 5 folds, all three fold strategies.

Design sections: §7 (surface inference), §12 (convergence gating).

## The headline is calibration

#109 asked for calibration as the primary result, not error, and it was right to. The surface is
**well calibrated where it interpolates and badly overconfident where it extrapolates.**

| | spatial | grouped | random |
|---|---|---|---|
| folds scored | **4/5** | 5/5 | 4/5 |
| multi-site studies split | 7 / 143 (5%) | 0 / 143 (0%) | 135 / 143 (94%) |
| MAE | 0.0384 | 0.0285 | 0.0261 |
| baseline MAE (global constant) | 0.0394 | 0.0401 | 0.0399 |
| improvement over constant | **+3%** | +29% | +35% |
| **95% predictive coverage** | **0.74** | **0.96** | 0.98 |
| 50% predictive coverage | 0.40 | 0.62 | 0.67 |
| log-score skill | +71.9 | +113.9 | +106.1 |

Spatial folds hold out whole regions, so they are the honest test of what the map claims to do.
There, a 95% interval covers **74%** of held-out surveys, one fold as low as **0.478**, and MAE
beats a global constant by 3% — within noise of no skill at all. Grouped folds keep studies intact
but leave near neighbours in training; there coverage is 0.96 against 0.95 nominal and MAE is 29%
better than the constant.

A reader of a rendered map cannot see MAE. They see an interval, and away from data that interval
is wrong by a wide margin.

## Autocorrelation share: 37%

Measured spatial-versus-**grouped**, not spatial-versus-random. That distinction is the reason this
number is meaningful for the first time: random folds shatter **94%** of multi-site studies here, so
a spatial-versus-random gap mixes spatial leakage with the loss of cohort identifiability that #127
documented and cannot be attributed to either. #131 added grouped folds, which hold studies intact
while still leaking neighbours, isolating the spatial component.

## Two folds excluded under §12

Spatial fold 3 (ESS 186) and random fold 4 (ESS 175), both against the floor of 200, and in both
cases **`lengthscale` is the worst-mixing parameter**. That is #111 recurring at draws=1000 rather
than the 400 it was originally reported at, so the sampling budget alone has not fixed it. Note
this is a *mixing* problem, not an identifiability one — the ridge test in #122 ruled the latter out.

The honest arm therefore rests on 4 folds, which is stated in the artifact rather than smoothed over.

## What this implies

The measured undercoverage away from data is a direct argument that **#59's data-support mask must
be on by default**, not optional. §7's support states exist to encode exactly this distinction, and
we now have the number that says the distinction is real: 0.96 coverage near observations, 0.74
away from them.
