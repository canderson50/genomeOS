## Body

## TL;DR

This records the exact version, usage terms, and intended role of Google's Satellite Embedding dataset so any future download can be reviewed and reproduced. It downloads no satellite data and provides no evidence yet that the dataset improves genetic predictions.

---

The WP3 campaign had no executable boundary between “an asset exists in the Earth Engine catalog” and “this exact version is eligible for extraction or modeling.” This change adds that boundary for the one currently prioritized family, Satellite Embedding V1 Annual, while keeping its state explicitly `candidate_only` and `not_run`.

Advances #290 and #189. It does not close either issue: no raster was extracted, no population footprint was evaluated, and no predictive benchmark or admission claim was made.

## Scientific and engineering contract

- **Objective:** freeze exact, reviewable source identity and terms before paying extraction or benchmark cost.
- **Measurable output:** canonical asset metadata, deterministic offline inspection, strict separation of catalog facts from extraction receipts and benchmark admission, and commercial compatibility tracked independently.
- **Interface:** immutable `CovariateAssetRegistry` / `CovariateAsset` models, packaged canonical JSON, and `scripts/inspect_covariate_asset.py`.
- **Assumptions/refusals:** all 64 embedding bands are one jointly interpreted vector; recent surface conditions are not historical ancestry, migration, malaria exposure, or fine-scale genetic evidence; malformed/coerced metadata, invented observed scale, or completed extraction without a receipt are refused.
- **Consumers:** later fold-local WP3 extraction and benchmark tooling only. Serving performs no Earth Engine access or inference.

## What is frozen

- Earth Engine asset `GOOGLE/SATELLITE_EMBEDDING/V1/ANNUAL`.
- Current `DATASET_VERSION=1.1`, AlphaEarth model v2.1, annual 2017–2024 support, nominal 10 m pixels, and ordered bands `A00…A63`.
- The annual layer is a tiled collection of approximately 163,840 m square source images in per-image local UTM projections. The registry makes this structure machine-readable so an extractor cannot silently treat a year as one global image or select the first intersecting tile.
- Mandatory per-image capture of dataset, model, processing-software, UTM-zone, and temporal properties. A collection-wide processing-software value is deliberately absent because the catalog does not supply one.
- CC BY 4.0 terms, verbatim required attribution, and commercial compatibility.
- Candidate limitations, required comparators, and `not_run` extraction state.

The canonical registry SHA-256 is `fe29fc9721beaeed823a7ec8763ef67e0cdd8ad83724447f9f0ee11d4e506db2`. The source note is `docs/research/satellite-embedding-candidate-2026-09-17.md`.

## Remaining #290 gates

The next slice must inspect one exact image, choose time semantics, perform one vectorized footprint reduction over several reviewed supports wholly contained by that image, and retain content-addressed export receipts. A later global extractor must batch by exact tile/UTM projection or validate an explicit mosaic/reprojection and overlap policy; it may not select the first intersecting image. Only then can unchanged buffered splits compare the genomic baseline, coordinates, all 64 bands, missingness, a structured negative control, and simpler interpretable sources. MAP-published assets remain unscheduled without their own mechanism and utility case.
