## Body

Implementation plan for the first of three phases, derived from the design spec in #5.

**Plan 1 = P0 + P1.** Nine TDD tasks: scaffold + CI → registry schema → HGDP adapter (establishing the adapter contract) → registry build with alias-collision detection → observations schema with mandatory ascertainment fields → gnomAD HGDP+1KG adapter + registry join → MAP HbS survey adapter → H3 resolution ladder → partitioned store + frozen contract.

**Acceptance signal** is deliberately unglamorous: the pipeline prints `{'healthy_reference': 4, 'population_random': 3}`. Two distinct sampling designs in the corpus is exactly what makes `β_design` identifiable, so it is the gate for Plan 2 starting at all.

Three choices worth reviewing:

1. **The MAP survey adapter is in Plan 1, not Plan 2**, which looks out of place next to the golden tests it feeds. It's here because MAP surveys are the corpus's only `population_random` source, and §7.1's ascertainment correction is identified by *contrast between* designs — without it, Plan 2 cannot start.
2. **One deliberate deviation from spec §6:** `source_labels` is normalised out of a nested `array<struct>` into a `population_aliases` table. One label mapping to two `population_id`s would silently split a population across two map points — the worst silent corruption available in P0 — and a long table makes it a detectable hard error. §6 should be amended to match.
3. **The curated variant set is deliberately excluded**, despite §15 listing it under P1. It needs clinical-genetics review to decide which ClinVar P/LP variants have defensible penetrance, and it gates P2 rather than P1. It sits at the top of Plan 2 alongside the INLA runtime decision.

**Flagged, not decided:** the spec specifies INLA-SPDE and R-INLA is R-only. Containerising R keeps the method and its defensibility to reviewers; reimplementing in PyMC/GPyTorch keeps one language but gives that up. Real cost either way — top of Plan 2.
