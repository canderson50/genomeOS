## Body

## TL;DR

Spatial-activity fits no longer finish sampling and then leave the GPU idle while one CPU core
tries to enumerate millions of possible allele counts. Count intervals now use the repository's
bounded exact CPU/GPU scorer, resolve zero-mass quantiles early, record the chosen backend in the
campaign manifest, and refuse a missing GPU before sampling starts.

## Problem and resulting behavior

`ActivityCountPredictive` called SciPy's generic beta-binomial CDF. For an allele denominator of
2,571,112, SciPy constructed the integer support from zero to the queried count. During the #384
probe, a converged strong-signal fit therefore spent more than eight minutes on one CPU core with
0% GPU utilization and wrote no task artifact.

This change evaluates the same marginalized law without a full-support allocation:

`mean((1 - q) + q * F_beta-binomial)` for nonnegative thresholds, and exactly zero at `AC=-1`.

- the CPU backend is a bounded exact reference;
- the CuPy backend batches rows, posterior draws, and finite-tail support with explicit memory caps;
- fractional activity probabilities weight each posterior draw before integration;
- exact support boundaries, inactive-zero mass, and high-concentration routing remain explicit;
- Cantelli upper brackets and a direct `CDF(0)` check reduce exact quantile-search work without
  approximating the returned endpoint;
- `--cdf-backend` is required, frozen in the campaign manifest, and passed through every worker;
- an explicit CuPy request performs float64 device arithmetic before graph construction or NUTS.

The failed target-acceptance-0.90 campaign remains failed. This does not rescue or reinterpret it,
and it does not permit a real HbS fit or publication.

## Actual-device evidence

Tests ran on an NVIDIA A100-SXM4-80GB with CuPy 14.2.0:

- the complete GPU predictive regression module passed;
- exact activity diagnostics crossed the 256-draw and eight-row chunk boundaries;
- CPU/GPU count quantiles, coverage, seeded samples, and Boolean outputs were identical;
- the largest numeric discrepancy was `1.11e-16`;
- an explicit denominator-2,571,112 regression passed without full-support allocation;
- the activity GPU test workload fell from 184.72 seconds to 59.77 seconds after the exact-zero
  quantile shortcut.

A complete synthetic campaign-shape profile used 500 draws and 232 held-out observations. Cold and
warm GPU diagnostics took 316.27 and 315.41 seconds. The sampled additional device-memory high-water
delta was 176,160,768 bytes, while live telemetry held roughly 85–94% GPU utilization. A small
complete-workflow comparison took 0.0559 seconds on GPU and 0.4760 seconds on CPU. The nearly equal
cold/warm times show that exact tail arithmetic, rather than initialization, remains the next
performance ceiling; this PR does not claim that five-minute full diagnostics are negligible.
