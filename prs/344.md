## Body

## TL;DR

Some studies recruit people across a geographic area, but the mapping model treated every study as if it came from one exact point. It can now average predictions across explicitly reviewed locations and weights for that area, while continuing to reject missing or guessed geography.

---

Current surface fits treat every observation coordinate as a pinpoint measurement, even when a reviewed source may describe recruitment over a spatial footprint. This change adds an explicit optional support contract: the GP evaluates a fixed-width set of spherical support points, composes the existing design/cohort/nugget effects, applies inverse-logit pointwise, and supplies the normalized probability mean to the existing Binomial or BetaBinomial likelihood.

Advances #37 and #189. It does not close either issue: real MAP/HGDP support semantics and a paired predictive benchmark remain separate gates.

## Scientific and engineering contract

- **Objective:** represent reviewed spatial recruitment support without claiming that every stored radius is a recruitment footprint.
- **Measurable output:** deterministic spherical-area quadrature, externally supplied population-weighted support, exact row alignment, frequency-scale averaging, persistent operator metadata, and fail-closed validation.
- **Interface:** `ObservationSupport`, `uniform_area_disc_support`, `weighted_point_support`, and keyword-only `fit_surface(..., observation_support=...)` under design §7 and §7.1.
- **Assumption:** the first supported count law is `independent_location_per_trial`; common/shared-location and clustered-location interpretations are refused rather than approximated.
- **Consumers:** controlled WP2 observation-operator comparisons. This PR does not qualify source radii, select population rasters, alter effective sample sizes, or publish a fitted surface.

## Implementation

- Builds uniform spherical-area discs with one broadcasted Gauss-Legendre radial × periodic azimuth calculation; there is no observation-by-support Python loop.
- Accepts reviewed external point weights through the same immutable, normalized support contract.
- Requires support IDs to exactly match validated P1 row order.
- Evaluates the GP once over flattened support points, reshapes once, and reduces the support axis once after inverse-logit.
- Preserves the existing point-fit graph and records no footprint metadata when support is absent.
- Persists operator convention, weighting, count-location model, support version, and points per observation through current and legacy cache loading.
