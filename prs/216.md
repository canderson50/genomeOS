## Interfaces and failure semantics

- Public validated Beta quadrature nodes serve the existing finite-product
  posterior reference and new continuous dependence reference. No production
  likelihood/scorer is used to establish the reference.
- Posterior dependence h uses joint/evidence/conditional-integral terms and
  literal rational anchors under both symmetric and asymmetric priors.
- All64/128/256 values, component terms, convergence gaps and raw ordering
  signs remain available. Unresolved precision is not converted into a tie.
- Exact count-pattern separability and identical parameter inputs establish
  literal ties; all other comparisons require stable signs and the numerical
  guard. The guard is empirical, not a uniform error proof.
- Four-draw randomized ranks use five bins and an integer discrete null,
  inclusive plus-one p-values and the declared twelve-test adjustment.
  A smaller successful subset cannot silently use the full-study null.
- Scalar ranks preserve exact Python/NumPy integer ordering. Dependence points
  accept only losslessly normalized binary64-or-narrower float scalars; exact
  rationals, longdouble and custom conversion domains are explicitly refused.
  Mixed supported NumPy/Python point types cannot manufacture identity ties.
