## Body

## TL;DR

Allowing allele frequencies to differ between source populations improved the main probability score and average absolute error in every matched comparison. It also made already-too-wide uncertainty intervals wider, so this is promising evidence for population-aware modeling but does not promote the model.

## Result

This publishes the completed 24-pair B0H-versus-B0 development campaign as a plain-English report, a compact aggregate JSON file, and a reviewable figure. The report exposes the crucial absolute calibration result: median 50% coverage rises from 92.79% to 94.98%, so the positive coverage delta is a deterioration rather than an improvement.

The primary `Beta(1, 9)` track improves integrated log score in all 12 pairs (median +0.2454 nats) and MAE in all 12 pairs (median -0.000293 frequency units). RMSE is mixed. The wider `Beta(1, 4)` sensitivity prior worsens RMSE in all 12 pairs and widens intervals more.

This advances #211. It does not close the issue because the requested expert review is still outstanding.

## Scope and design

Implements the reporting and immutable-artifact boundaries in Atlas design §§5, 7–8, and 12. The committed JSON contains only aggregate metrics and integrity hashes; the 1.41 GB row-level report, predictions, and posterior outputs remain private and untracked.

Expert review should check the population-exchangeability assumption, the count-calibration target, and the correct validation unit for linked variants and reused source populations.
