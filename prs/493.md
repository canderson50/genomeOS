## What this changes, and why

`scripts/build_surfaces.py` defaulted to `--draws 400`, used for both warm-up and retained draws. That's the budget at which, in [#111](https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/111#issuecomment-5419630790), HbS missed the ESS floor at 199 and G6PD never mixed (r_hat 1.492, ESS 8). The configurations that do converge use 1,200:
- the HbS parity run (`docs/research/hbs-piel-parity-2026-09-16.md`: 4 × 1,200 after 1,200 warm-up, R-hat 1.0059, min ESS 767.6);
- the data-store rebuild in `docs/data-store.md`, which passes `--draws 1200` explicitly.

So anyone who ran the script without `--draws` got a known-failing budget, and §12 would exclude their surfaces. This PR changes the default to the budget the project already uses.

- `DEFAULT_DRAWS = 1200`, still shared by warm-up and draws, with a comment citing [#111](https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/111) and the two documents above.
- Argument parsing moves into `parse_args(argv)` and the shared `FitConfig` into `batch_config(draws, n_inducing)`, so a test can pin the default without running a fit. Behaviour with an explicit `--draws` is unchanged.

**Scientific contract**
1. *Claim:* the batch builder's default sampling budget is one that has converged on real HbS data, not one known to fail the §12 gate.
2. *Output and acceptance evidence:* omitting `--draws` yields `FitConfig(draws=1200, tune=1200)`; an explicit `--draws` still sets both together. Two tests pin this.
3. *Component:* `scripts/build_surfaces.py` (`DEFAULT_DRAWS`, `parse_args`, `batch_config`). No science module, schema or gate changes.
4. *Assumptions and consumers:* 1,200 is the budget recorded as converging for HbS, on the 994-survey parity input. The budget behind the 1,071-survey `v3` surface isn't recorded. It is not shown to rescue G6PD, which [#116](https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/116) attributes to the pooled phenotype rather than the budget. Convergence gates (`max_rhat` 1.05, `min_ess` 200) are untouched. Consumers are future batch refits; published artifacts are immutable and unaffected.
