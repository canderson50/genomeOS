## Body

The frozen B0H calibration reduction needs a reviewable view of rank outcomes and case stages, including missing outcomes and refusals. This adds a lossless canonical decoder and an offline, hash-required renderer that produces separate rank and accounting figures, preserves the exact input, and records source/output hashes in a receipt written only after successful rendering.

The deterministic demonstration contains authored synthetic reporting states. Both figures label that distinction, retain the existing claim reasons, and report publication eligibility as false. Existing scientific calculations, gates, serializer, runner, collection, dependencies, and contracts are unchanged. This reporting work does not establish executed calibration, model acceptance, or geographic predictive skill.

Advances #211 and #189; neither issue is completed by this change. Implements Atlas design §§5, 7–8, 12 and the calibration-report specification §§1–4. The branch is reconciled directly onto `main`.
