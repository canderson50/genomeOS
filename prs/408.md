## What this changes, and why

On a phone, `/app/` was barely usable:
- The site header overflowed to 443 px on a 390 px screen, so the browser zoomed the whole page out.
- The control dock covered 52% of the globe even with every section collapsed.
- Opening the inspector raised that to 91.5%.

The Cesium explorer design §7.3 had promised a top dataset selector with collapsible bottom sheets, but that was never built.

This PR builds it. The existing dock and right rail are restyled in place, not duplicated, so every id, landmark and test hook survives.

- **No zoom-out.** Every page now emits `initial-scale=1`. Phones from 320 to 412 px fit without horizontal scroll. `/app/` is a `100svh` column with no page scroll, and pull-to-refresh is off on `/app/` only.
- **Top selector.** On phones the dataset picker moves to the top through a portal (rendered once) and is reachable without opening anything.
- **Controls bottom sheet.** It has three heights: peek, half and full. You can drag it by the handle, tap it, or use the keyboard. Content below the peek row is `inert`, never `aria-hidden`. Focus moves before a region goes inert. A flick settles at the next height in its direction.
- **Panel sheet.** On phones the inspector and More info open as a second sheet, never both at once. They have focus restoration, and focus never lands on `<body>`. Closing the inspector clears the selection.
- **One Escape stack.** Each Escape closes only the innermost layer: popover, then dialog, then external panel, then inspector, then sheet.
- **Legend and credits.** The legend strip and the attribution block dock above the active sheet. The legend popover is placed by measurement, so it never clips.
- **Globe reachable.** At peek, at least 65% of the explorer hit-tests to the globe, and its centre is entirely globe. Tests assert both.
- **Touch targets.** Targets are at least 24 px, which also fixes an existing desktop WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8 violation. Sheet handles and peek rows are at least 44 px.

Desktop is unchanged except for the listed deltas in §A.1.13: 24 px info targets, the Escape stack and legend Escape, a flex shell with +1 px explorer height, and an empty top slot. I checked this against a build of `5a10b15` for geometry, tab order and screenshots.

**Verified:**
- All gates above.

- Sheet cycle by tap, keyboard, mouse drag and CDP touch drag.

**Not verified:**
- A real iOS or Android device. Everything ran in Chromium emulation, so Safari's `svh` and overscroll behaviour and real touch hardware were not exercised.
- Landscape phones 833–932 px wide. They keep the desktop dock, which is out of scope per the spec.
- The interaction spec §9.1 asks for 16 px interface text. The dense UI is 13.25 px, the tests pin that, and this PR keeps it. The conflict is recorded rather than resolved here.
- Two latent minor findings are recorded for later:
  - Focus carry across the 52rem switch relies on MediaQueryList firing order. That order is correct today.
  - The interface contract doc does not list Part A's new helper exports.
