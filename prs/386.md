## Body

## TL;DR

This adds a simulation-only test bed for a proposed map model that can turn down low background signal in some locations. It compares the existing count model with the new candidate on fixed synthetic scenarios and geographic holdouts, records failed fits and one allowed retry, and checks whether predictions improve without pretending that any individual zero count proves absence.

This does **not** fit the real HbS data, promote a scientific map, or give either hidden model component a biological meaning.

## Problem and resulting behavior

The compact-support B1G candidate was worse than the current B2 model because it raised error on zero-count surveys. Issue #384 therefore requires a narrower simulation gate before another real-data fit: a spatial activity probability `q(s)` that is integrated out of the count likelihood rather than assigned to observed zeros.

This PR adds the executable simulation boundary after the predictive-law foundation merged in #385:

- an analytically marginalized, differentiable activity beta-binomial likelihood for PyMC;
- matched ordinary and spatial-activity HSGP graphs that share the conditional-frequency field, concentration prior, and replicated-cohort term;
- aligned posterior extraction, fixed convergence gates, and shared new-cohort prediction;
- a frozen 18-condition grid covering seeds 42/43/44, exact `q=1` null truth, weak and strong component separation, low/high denominators, and cohort heterogeneity;
- zero/positive count diagnostics, marginal truth recovery, component dependence, and false inactive support under the null;
- exact reuse checks for the outer dependency-aware folds and outcome-blind three-inner-fold construction inside each outer training partition; and
- a single-fold runner with content-derived seeds and at most one logged doubled-draw/tuning retry for convergence failure.

Observed zeros are always scored through the marginalized count law. Synthetic latent states are retained only because the simulator owns their known truth.

## Scientific contract

- **Objective:** determine in controlled simulation whether a spatial activity gate can suppress background signal without erasing positive-count prediction.
- **Measurable output:** proper count log score, MAE, 50/80/95% coverage, zero/positive strata, marginal/component recovery, convergence, posterior component dependence, and null false support.
- **Engineering interface:** pure array/configuration functions under `genomeos.surfaces` and `genomeos.validation`; no file, network, HTTP, serving, or UI path.
- **Refusal conditions:** invalid truth/count contracts, changed outer dependencies, infeasible three-inner-fold nesting, nonconvergence after one retry, or prediction/scoring failure remain explicit terminal results.

Implements design §§4, 7–8, and 12. Advances #384 and #189; closes neither.
