## Objective and product claim

Publish a reproducible CesiumJS Atlas where measured population observations and inferred geographic surfaces remain visibly and contractually distinct. This implements Atlas design §§5, 6, 7, 9, and 11.

## What this delivers

- Publishes a grouped, explained 30-dataset catalog: MAP HbS/G6PD plus 28 AFND cytokine, HLA, and KIR maps.
- Republishes the AFND maps as immutable v3 WorldPop-backed surfaces with their validated measured observations; MAP and AFND observation/surface contracts remain separate.
- Adds globe, map, and perspective views plus a visual basemap/terrain gallery, Cesium ion imagery and terrain, and a token-free OpenStreetMap fallback.
- Adds basemap opacity/brightness, ocean color, default-on day/night lighting, earth opacity, and independently togglable/styled country outlines.
- Adds flat/extruded hexagons, smooth triangles, Honmoon, Honmoon fill, and the Golden palette.
- Adds hemispheres, circles, and 3D pins; configurable point size/color/opacity; metadata-aware hover/inspectors; physical sampling radii; and correct globe-side occlusion/layering.
- Adds smooth control disclosures and render transitions, cached batched scene updates, persistent selection alongside hover, and transient hover dismissal.
- Adds compact but comprehensive external-information panels with downloads, gnomAD/dbSNP gating, place context, and Google Maps centroid/polygon routes.
- Adds URL-shareable presentation state, including an explicit `dayNight=false` override even though lighting is now enabled by default.
- Passes both `CESIUM_TOKEN` and `GOOGLE_MAPS_TOKEN` repository secrets only into the production Pages build; neither credential is committed or printed.
- Publishes the API/read-path container to GHCR as `latest` and immutable `sha-...` tags for AMD64 and ARM64 after `main` CI succeeds, with SBOM and provenance attestations.

## Scientific assumptions and refusal conditions

- Observations and inferred surfaces remain separate schemas, artifacts, and layers.
- Missing population support is not replaced by an administrative centroid or invented radius.
- Masked/unsupported cells remain excluded according to the Atlas artifact contract.
- External links are omitted unless immutable artifact metadata carries the exact supported identifier.
- AFND reuse retains source attribution and CARE/Biocultural notices under the completed owner decision in #66.
- No fitting or other scientific inference runs in the browser or serving path.

## Downstream consumers

- The public GitHub Pages `/app/` explorer.
- Static and future API-backed `AtlasDataProvider` implementations using the same versioned contracts.
- Reproducible artifact publication and Hugging Face synchronization workflows.
- Cloud Run, local integration, and collaborator workflows consuming `ghcr.io/bschilder/genomeos`.
