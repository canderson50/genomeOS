## Scientific limits and expert review

These are mechanics and compatibility tests, not evidence of AF accuracy, calibration,
resident representativeness, geographic resolution, or migration. No qualified sealed resident
benchmark exists yet. Geographic-footprint #190, shared-schema #192 and independent empirical
validation remain separate work. No source data or genotype/surface artifacts are included.

cohort/nugget/concentration identifiability and the existing approximation's joint-field limits.

## Rulings I made

1. Use a stacked follow-up branch so #197's review head stays fixed. Cost if wrong: additional
   rebase/base coordination after that parent merges.
2. Require exact fresh-to-fresh and cache-to-cache legacy comparisons separately because the
   unchanged parent already differs across execution modes. Cost if wrong: mode-specific
   regressions require both arms to catch; both controls and the discrepancy remain retained.
3. Refuse nonfinite intermediate composition arithmetic, without a hidden precision fallback.
   Cost: some mathematically finite cancellation cases are refused and need explicit rescaling
   or separately reviewed higher-precision support.
4. Permit copied inference-data NumPy materialization on save to meet exact clean-process
   replay. Cost: host memory/copy time and metadata-preservation risk, covered by live-fit
   nonmutation and group/coordinate/attribute/container tests. This does not repair old caches.
