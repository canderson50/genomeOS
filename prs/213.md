## Scientific interpretation and expert review

The target is operational reference-panel populations, not the resident worldwide population. Rho is a working heterogeneity parameter, not inferred FST, historical migration or ancestry. The fixed primary/sensitivity priors and exchangeability assumption need domain review before real-data comparison. Computational vectorization is not independent-locus evidence or a joint LD model.

Even a passed exact sampler test is not proof of empirical accuracy or broad calibration. General quadrature/SBC/stress tests, benchmark-runner integration and paired real comparisons remain open #211 work. The new methods note identifies data-dependent calibration checks and correlated-rank pitfalls from two fully read primary methods papers.

No map is emitted: this core has no coordinate input or surface output. There is no renderable scientific layer or geographic-resolution claim to illustrate.

Merging this research core does not publish a model or scientific release. Preserve the worktree and ignored research/run evidence for subsequent experimental units.
