## 1. G6PD — a second variant, chosen because it is a generalization test

![G6PD observations](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bschilder/genomeOS/d4ff98b86a3e148c8e87e26143e0f72fdb94eb74/docs/figures/g6pd_observations.png)

910 surveys, 254 cohorts, 316,448 male alleles, median frequency 0.0435. The geography is
visibly **not** HbS's — Mediterranean, Middle East and South/Southeast Asia alongside Africa —
which is the point: if the pipeline only worked for HbS, this is where it would show.

Three things HbS cannot exercise:

- **X-linked inheritance.** Males are hemizygous, so `ac = number_males_deficient` over
  `an = number_males` is a direct allele frequency needing no assumption at all. Females are
  excluded because a female recorded "deficient" by an enzyme assay may be homozygous *or* a
  heterozygote with skewed X-inactivation, and those imply very different frequencies.
- **An enzyme assay, not a genotype.** `assay="enzyme_activity"`: an activity assay detects the
  phenotype whatever allele caused it, and its sensitivity varies with the assay used.
- **A phenotype rather than a variant.** G6PD deficiency is ~200 alleles. `variant_id` gains a
  `phenotype:` form, deliberately not parseable as a locus so a composite cannot be mistaken for
  a variant in a filename or a join key. The contract diff is the review surface. **§6 does not
  describe this case** — filed as #116, which is the more important half of this change.

## 2. Recovering data that was being discarded

The refusal counts were being reported as though every one were principled. Investigating found
four in HbS and two in G6PD that were not.

**HbS: 857 → 1,071 surveys (67% → 83%), 344 → 419 cohorts.**

| refusal | what it actually was | recovered |
|---|---|---|
| `excluded_from_piel_2013` | a *filter* for comparability with a published analysis, not a defect | 158 |
| `incomplete_genotypes` | required `hbaa`, but `ac = hbas + 2·hbss` never touches it | 53 |
| `partially_genotyped` | **the denominator was wrong** — see below | ~86 |
| `genotypes_exceed_sample` | 3–14% excess is a rounded `sample_size` beside exact counts | 4 |

**The denominator is now the people actually typed, not the people approached.** Dividing
observed carriers by people who were never tested understates the frequency. The pathological
case is still refused and now named for what it is: a US newborn-screening row typed 1.5% of its
cohort *because only screen-positives were typed*, so the subset is carrier-enriched by
construction and yields an HbS frequency of 0.31 for the United States. What separates that from
ordinary incomplete fieldwork is how small the typed share is — the real distribution has a
median of 82% — so refusing all of them to catch that one discarded eighty-odd good surveys.

**⚠️ `piel_2013_subset_only` now defaults OFF. Golden test 1 must pass `True`**, because a
parity comparison has to be scored on the reference's own inputs. Nothing else should.

**G6PD: 875 → 910, i.e. 99% of rows that report any counts.** Admin0/Admin1 centroids are placed
at country scale rather than refused — MAP's `area_size` is capped near 3,800 km², so for a
country centroid it understates location uncertainty by orders of magnitude (Nigeria's rows say
20 km² for a country whose equivalent radius is 538 km). Natural Earth 1:110m also omits
microstates entirely, and **Singapore alone is 35,714 males, the largest single survey in the
corpus**, so eleven published land areas are checked in explicitly.

I also corrected my own misleading headline: **827 of G6PD's 1,749 rows are metadata-only stubs**
with nothing in them. Reporting "50% retained" against those made principled refusals look like
carelessness and hid the real figure.

## 3. Batch orchestration (#40)

Every variant that enters leaves as **either a fit or an exclusion carrying a reason** — §12's
requirement stated as an invariant and asserted as a test. `run_batch` catches per variant,
because in a batch the alternative to catching is not a clean crash but 999 variants never being
fitted; the catch is a redirect into an audit trail, not a swallow. `strict=True` re-raises for
single-variant debugging.

The exclusion list is an **artifact, not a log**: written next to the surfaces, written even when
empty (an absent file is ambiguous between "nothing excluded" and "never produced"), and sorted
so two runs over the same inputs produce identical bytes.

The floor drops from 8 observations to 5 and is now justified *structurally* — the model has five
free parameters — rather than by a round number. Above it a fit is attempted and allowed to fail
on convergence, because an exclusion backed by r_hat is evidence and one backed by a threshold is
a guess.

## G6PD Bayesian posteriors

Fitted from `main` at 1,200 draws x 4 chains, `n_inducing=150`, `lengthscale_sigma=0.4`. Batch result: **2/2 variants fitted, 0% excluded**. Figures are on branch `figures/g6pd-1200`, pushed from the pod that produced them.

### Parameter posteriors, by chain

![G6PD parameter posteriors](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bschilder/genomeOS/24c1d34669a916c1e570f9ad9eb1bb9c88a4e08c/docs/figures/g6pd_posteriors.png)

Drawn **per chain rather than pooled**, deliberately: pooling hides exactly the disagreement r_hat measures, and this variant's whole difficulty was chains disagreeing about one parameter.

| parameter | r_hat | ESS | |
|---|---|---|---|
| correlation range | 1.024 | **206** | PASS, barely |
| amplitude | 1.001 | 1296 | PASS |
| intercept | 1.006 | 1039 | PASS |
| cohort_sd | 1.002 | 1487 | PASS |
| concentration | 1.004 | 1632 | PASS |

**The correlation-range panel is still the weak one and the figure shows why.** Its density spikes sharply near 400-500 km but trails a long tail out past 2,500 km, and its ESS of 206 sits barely above the gate of 200 while every other parameter clears it six- to eight-fold. That tail is the residual of the identifiability ridge described in #116 — a very long range being hard to distinguish from a constant `intercept`. Tightening the prior bounded it enough to sample; it did not make the question go away.

Anyone re-running this should expect that panel to be the first to fail. It is not a comfortable margin.

### Fitted surface

![G6PD fitted surface](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bschilder/genomeOS/24c1d34669a916c1e570f9ad9eb1bb9c88a4e08c/docs/figures/g6pd_surface_geodesic.png)

910 surveys, correlation range **517 km**, H3 res-3 cells, 4,400 cells left as bare grey land where there is no claim.

The geography is the point, and it is **not** HbS's. A Mediterranean and Middle Eastern belt through Greece, Turkey, Iran and the Arabian peninsula; sub-Saharan Africa; South Asia; and a strong Southeast Asian focus across Thailand, Laos, Cambodia, Malaysia and Papua New Guinea — with near-zero across northern Europe, East Asia, the Americas and Australia. That is the malaria-endemic distribution G6PD deficiency is known for, recovered independently of HbS, and the peaks sit higher in the Middle East and Southeast Asia than in Africa, matching Howes et al.

The two variants also recover **different spatial scales from the same machinery** — G6PD 517 km against HbS 680 km — rather than inheriting one imposed number.

### What this does not establish

- **Not validated.** Golden test 2 needs Howes et al.'s national estimates extracted as a parity target, the equivalent of #93 for HbS. That has no issue yet and no numbers exist.
- **#116 stands.** A prior that keeps the field spatial is not an argument that ~200 alleles of differing clinical severity share one spatial scale. A converged fit makes that question more urgent, not less, because there is now a surface someone could be tempted to publish.
- **Held-out skill is unmeasured for G6PD**, and #109 shows HbS predicting held-out surveys barely better than a constant. That gate applies here too.
