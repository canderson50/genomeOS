## Body

## TL;DR

This adds a reproducible test of the current sickle-cell allele-frequency map and its simpler local alternative. The current spatial model predicted all 994 held-out survey rows and substantially beat a pooled baseline, while the local model could predict only 218 rows even after a post-hoc wider search. The spatial model is the stronger development baseline, but it is **not approved for publication or production** because its uncertainty intervals and retained convergence evidence do not yet pass the scientific gates.

## What changed

The offline benchmark leaves whole study cohorts out of training and predicts them as unseen data. It records every completed, failed, and infeasible fold; exact input, split, configuration, source, environment, and output hashes; checkpoint identity; convergence diagnostics for future runs; and `scientific_promotion_decision=not_made`.

This integrated branch also adds:

- a strict evidence reader and report for the local-count B1 comparator;
- fold-boundary checkpoint and resume support for long spatial runs;
- sampler-diagnostic retention and refusal when convergence evidence is missing or invalid;
- exact beta-binomial scoring for the full supported count domain at ordinary concentrations;
- direct complete-support arithmetic for extremely concentrated draws through `AN=65,536`;
- adaptive vectorized CuPy batching for count CDFs; and
- the current `main` observation-footprint likelihood without conflating measured observations with fitted surfaces.

It implements Atlas design §§4–5, 7–8, and 12. It adds no malaria-specific rule, environmental covariate, or source-specific shortcut. Private row-level inputs and predictions remain untracked.

## Development findings

The five-fold B2 current-GP run completed all **994** held-out rows. Against pooled B0, the balanced macro integrated log score improved by **316.44 nats**, MAE fell from **0.04156 to 0.03281** (**21.1% lower**), and RMSE fell from **0.05864 to 0.04587**. All five geographic blocks improved log score.

The result does not qualify the model. Its 50%, 80%, and 95% interval coverage was **61.6%, 87.3%, and 96.8%**: the middle intervals are too wide under the existing tolerance. All five fits warned about R-hat above 1.01, one fit diverged, the old artifact did not retain numerical per-fold convergence diagnostics, dependencies were not reviewed, and geography remained algorithmic development evidence. The prospective diagnostic repair cannot retroactively qualify that artifact.

The prespecified B1 local-count model emitted **0/994** rows. A post-hoc 2,000 km sensitivity emitted **218/994** rows, improved positive-count cases, and worsened zero-count cases. B1 is therefore rejected as a global model; issue #307 remains open for any narrower residual mechanism.
