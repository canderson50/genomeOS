## Body

## TL;DR

A Chinese sequencing study publishes how common genetic variants are in four broad regions but not the underlying whole-number counts. This PR imports those regions only for an explicit list of variants, reconstructs counts only when the source arithmetic is unambiguous, and preserves the study's wide geographic uncertainty and disease-enriched sampling. An independent audit found 324 valid source rows omit an unused quality field; the importer now accepts that exact layout and applies a tighter precision check based on the original VCF.

---

WBBC publishes four regional allele-frequency fields with denominators, but no regional allele counts; its VCF also omits INFO declarations, its WGS cohort is disease-enriched, and its four labels cover very large multi-province areas. This adds a fail-closed P0/P1 path that reconstructs counts only under the audited rounding contract and represents each region with explicit province-covering geographic uncertainty.

Closes #325.

## Resulting behavior

- A reviewed P0 adapter admits exactly North, Central, South and Lingnan as `location_type=inferred` populations. Their Natural Earth-derived covering radii are 2,025, 350, 1,300 and 650 km.
- The P1 join requires those four labels to map one-to-one to four distinct reviewed population IDs; missing, unexpected or collapsed aliases fail before any observation is emitted.
- A chunked P1 adapter scans one or more chromosome VCFs for an explicit curated variant set and emits all four regional rows, including zeros and the true denominators 448, 100, 8,070 and 126.
- Regional `AC = round(AF × AN)` is accepted only when the reported global `AC/AF/AN` control passes and the regional residual is below 0.000005 alleles. `RR/RA/AA` must independently reproduce both `NS` and `AC`.
- The parser accepts the two ordered INFO endings observed in the original file: numeric `VQSLOD`, or missing `VQSLOD` after `DP`. It still refuses reordered, unknown or malformed fields.
- The adapter preserves `sampling_design=convenience`, `disease_ascertainment_excluded=false`, and `cohort_id=wbbc:wgs-frequency-release-v1` for the disease-enriched pooled WGS release.
- `build_registry.py` and `build_observations.py` expose the new inputs. `--wbbc-vcf` is repeatable because the public release is chromosome-partitioned.

This implements Atlas design §§4, 6 and 7.1. It keeps measured observations separate from fitted surfaces, gives geography no runtime default, and fails on missing variants, unmapped regions, source-schema drift, non-PASS requested records, inconsistent genotype tallies, or ambiguous count reconstruction.

## Scope and source controls

WBBC is admitted only as a source-isolated cohort. #326 remains open, and this PR does not allow WBBC to be pooled with PGG.Han, ChinaMAP, NyuWa or CMDB while participant independence is unresolved.

The primary article is CC BY 4.0. The processed-frequency page requests citation and gives no processed-data licence or explicit redistribution/commercial-use restriction; the review records `no_restriction_found` and separately retains `licence_not_stated`. No WBBC source VCF, participant row or participant-linked supplement is committed. The checked-in VCF fixture is synthetic.
