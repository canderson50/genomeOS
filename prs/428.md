## What changed

The site header showed a text-only "genomeOS" wordmark, the browser tab used a placeholder ring icon, and the header button read "Launch Atlas" with a rocket. This PR replaces all three with the owner's genomeOS Foundation artwork.

The owner's requests, as recorded in #418:

> - Use the dark transparent SVG logo across the site (header and footer).
> - Show "FOUNDATION" under genomeOS in the header: larger when the page first loads, shrinking a bit as the reader scrolls.
> - A favicon made from the "OS" part of the logo. The first version, the whole "OS", was too small in a 16 px tab, so the owner chose the globe "O" alone, on a dark circle so it shows on light tab bars too.
> - Rebrand the "Launch Atlas" header button as **genomeOS Atlas**: still eye-catching, but in keeping with the dark site. A black button with an animated aurora effect coming from its edges. From three live variants the owner chose **Northern lights**: green, cyan and violet curtains that drift and spill past the edges. The label is the globe mark, the name and an arrow; on phones, the globe mark alone.

### Logo and lockup
- **Assets** (`website/public/brand/`): the owner's genomeOS Foundation artwork, unedited (`genomeos-foundation-dark.svg`), plus two crops of it: the header lockup (genome + OS + FOUNDATION, with the tagline group dropped) and the wordmark (genome + OS). Each crop changes only the root `width`/`height`/`viewBox` and the `<title>`/`<desc>` text. Every path it keeps, the gradient and the groups stay byte-identical to the owner's file. The viewBoxes come from `getBBox()` measured in Chromium, padded 2%.
- **Header, every page except the Atlas:** the FOUNDATION lockup. It is large while the page is at the top (5.5rem bar, lockup 3.6rem, FOUNDATION ~8.9 px) and compact after scrolling (3.9rem bar, lockup 2.6rem). The change animates over 200 ms and does not animate under reduced motion.
  - **No flicker.** The header is `position: fixed`, and a `.site-header-spacer` holds its landing height, so a size change never moves the page and scroll anchoring has nothing to correct. `MotionController` adds hysteresis: the header compacts above scrollY 56 and expands below 40.
  - Linked sections and focused controls land below the fixed header (`scroll-padding-top`). Print puts the header back into the flow.
  - Landscape phones (under 30rem tall) get the shorter 4.75rem bar.
  - Narrow rows shrink the logo instead of wrapping the nav. Phones get the lockup too, with FOUNDATION ≥ 5 px at 390 px.
  - The home link's accessible name is "genomeOS Foundation", the text it shows.
- **Atlas (`/app/`):** keeps the compact wordmark crop at its previous size and its 86.5 px phone bar, so the globe keeps its space.
- **Footer:** the full lockup, with the tagline ≥ 10 px tall.

### Favicon
- `favicon.svg` is the globe "O" alone, clipped from the logo's `#wordmark-os` group (paths and gradient byte-identical), on a dark `#020712` circle. Every gradient stop clears 3:1 on both light and dark tab strips.
- 16, 32 and 48 px PNGs, `favicon.ico` (PNG-in-ICO) and a 180 px `apple-touch-icon.png`. `npm run build:favicons` (`scripts/build-favicons.mjs`) regenerates them byte for byte.
- The set is linked on every page: SiteLayout pages, the Starlight docs (through `src/lib/favicons.ts`) and `/app/polygon/`. Every link goes through `sitePath`, so the `/genomeOS/` fallback resolves them.

### genomeOS Atlas button
- A black pill with the globe mark, "genomeOS Atlas" and an arrow, lit by three **Northern lights** curtains (green, cyan, violet) that drift and spill past its edges. Its accessible name is exactly "genomeOS Atlas" (→ `/app/`).

### Menu fixes
These turned up while fitting the fixed header:
- The Menu panel scrolls inside short viewports, such as 200% and 400% zoom, so every link stays reachable.
- It closes on Escape, on a press or focus outside it, and when a resize hides it. Escape returns focus to the summary only from inside the Menu or from `<body>`, so it never pulls focus out of an Atlas dialog.
- An open Menu paints above the Atlas catalog pickers and is the first layer Escape closes.
- The Menu shows on every header up to 72rem, so no nav label wraps beside the Atlas status chip.
- At large text sizes, genomeOS Atlas moves into the Menu so the home logo keeps a usable width.
- The panel's `dvh` height cap has a `vh` fallback.
