## Body

## TL;DR

This makes the upcoming B1G result impossible to compare on the wrong surveys. It checks that B1G, B0, and B2 used the same data and the same five geographic test groups, compares B1G with both existing models, and draws improvements at the real survey locations. It does not choose a winner while dependency review and subgroup power rules remain unresolved.

## Problem and resulting behavior

B1G now runs as five immutable parallel folds, but its final artifact alone does not contain enough information to prove that a later comparison reused the frozen B0/B2 split geography. A plausible-looking score report could therefore be produced from mismatched inputs or folds.

This change binds the B1G result to its integrity-checked checkpoint header, checks the complete split ledger against both baselines, and then runs the shared paired benchmark reducer twice: B1G versus B0 and B1G versus the checkpointed current B2 result.

## Scientific contract

- **Objective:** test whether B1G improves held-out HbS count prediction without changing observations, geographic folds, support, or prediction semantics.
- **Acceptance evidence:** all five folds must complete; both comparisons must contain the same held-out rows; the report retains balanced macro metrics, the exhaustive 3,125-resample outer-block interval, zero/positive strata, regional summaries, and 50/80/95% calibration.
- **Interface:** `compare_b1g_benchmarks` is a pure research-side comparison boundary. `scripts/plot_hbs_b1g_benchmark.py` verifies files, replays published summaries, adds measured coordinates, and renders the private review output.
- **Refusals:** header, input hash, split, fold status, held-out count, or row identity drift is an error. Dependency-aware certification and adequately powered subgroup noninferiority remain unresolved rather than being inferred after seeing results.

Implements design §§4–8 and 12. This advances #331 and #189; it closes neither issue.

## Review figure behavior

Both map panels use circles at measured held-out survey coordinates. Red means B1G is worse and green means B1G is better. An undefined paired score remains visible as an outlined circle with an explicit legend entry. No fitted surface or heatmap is mixed with observations.

The real HbS report and figure are not included because the campaign artifacts remain private, nonpublication evidence. The checked-in script will generate them immediately after all five folds finish.
