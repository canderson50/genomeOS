## Body

## TL;DR

The spatial activity model failed its simulation gate and will **not** be fitted to real HbS data. It behaved safely when the ordinary model was true, but under strong localized truth its average absolute error was 41–52% worse, truth recovery was 120–177% worse, and its intervals were much too wide. It improved scores for positive counts by making zero-count predictions worse—the same tradeoff it was meant to solve.

All 720 registered task artifacts were retained. 704 fits completed successfully; 16 failed convergence after the single allowed doubled-budget retry. The sealed result records 21 preregistered refusal reasons.

![Aggregate spatial activity simulation result](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bschilder/genomeOS/e0ad2c655776af8ddbaa8b1c976e993e915cf0f2/docs/figures/spatial-activity-preflight-2026-09-25.png)

## Result

- The null safety condition passes: the model does not manufacture substantial inactive support when the ordinary model is correct.
- The weak localized condition does not establish an improvement: its log-score interval includes harm, MAE is 0.22% worse, and 50%/80% intervals severely overcover.
- The primary strong condition is negative: MAE is 47.3% worse and marginal-truth recovery is 139.9% worse.
- High denominators and cohort heterogeneity do not rescue the model: MAE remains 52.2% and 41.4% worse.
- The low-denominator comparison is incomplete because terminal convergence failures are preserved rather than dropped.

The aggregate report and JSON contain no observation-level predictions, private inputs, task shards, logs, or pod metadata. The renderer authenticates the exact registered manifest and all 720 content-addressed artifact records before emitting the six aggregate comparisons.

## Decision

Do not promote or retune this activity parameterization, and do not launch a real HbS fit. This closes the bounded preflight while advancing the broader model investigation in #103 and benchmark-first program in #189. B2 remains the strongest completed comparator; the variant-set, rs334 publication-round-trip, resident-frequency, sealed-confirmation, and HbS parity gates remain open.

Closes #384.

Implements design §8's fail-closed validation and review-evidence boundary.
