## Body

Design spec for v1, following the three decisions taken in brainstorming:

- **v1 flagship** — Mendelian + single-variant burden (not polygenic)
- **Surfaces** — precomputed INLA-SPDE posteriors over a curated variant set, real uncertainty
- **Engine** — DuckDB-on-Cloud-Run for interactive reads, BigQuery for batch

**Definition of done is HbS parity:** reproduce Piel et al. 2013 national AS/SS neonate estimates end-to-end through our own pipeline, with concrete acceptance thresholds (§8). G6PD is the second golden test and exercises X-linked inheritance.

Two things I'd especially like reviewed:

1. **§8's acceptance thresholds** (≥80% of national point estimates inside published intervals, ≥95% CI overlap) — I picked these; they're arguable and they gate everything downstream.
2. **§9's HWE + F=0 default** for the recessive burden expressions. Shipping with no consanguinity correction is defensible but it will visibly under-predict in specific regions, and I'd rather that be a decision than a surprise.

Also registers P6–P12 (time axis, globe, semantic/polygenic, chromosome, chromatin, individual upload, conversational agent) purely to fix their boundaries — §16 pins three constraints on the agent now because retrofitting them later is expensive, chiefly that the variant-class policy must be enforced in the API rather than in a prompt.
