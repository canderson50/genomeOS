## Body

## What users get

These changes apply only when Cesium World Terrain is on. The default smooth globe draws everything as before.

- **Country borders lie on the ground.** They are drawn into map tiles and laid over the terrain like the surface, so mountains no longer hide or cut them. They follow the country colour and opacity; a colour change cross-fades once the new tiles have loaded.
- **Sampling areas lie on the ground too.** Each survey's circle is drawn on the terrain in the sampling colour, above the surface and the borders. The floating rings stay until the ground shows the circles.
- **Symbols, labels and the highlight follow the ground.** Observation symbols stand 7,650 m above the rendered ground, country labels 2,500 m above it, and the hover and selection outline 300 m above it. They move as finer terrain tiles load, after a view change, and when Physical relief changes.
- **Physical relief now carries all the data with it**, so phase 1's "World Terrain can hide map data on high ground" warning is gone.
- **Failures** still bring back the smooth globe with a warning, now for borders and sampling areas as well as the surface.

Left for phase 4: Honmoon contours, the shader pre-warm, the mobile memory measurement and fallbacks, and asserted terrain budgets.

## Changes per task

- **Task 18: border and footprint rasterisers** (`geometry/raster/line-raster.ts`, split into `segment-index.ts` and `footprint-rings.ts`).
  - Borders stroke 1.65 texels, opaque, through a 1° segment index that checks every vertex and refuses a segment jumping more than 180° of longitude.
  - Footprints are 48-vertex rings, 2 texels at α 0.9, unwrapped so rings across 180° or round a pole draw on every side.
  - Both reuse the outline stroke law. Their bounds reach 1° beyond their vertices.
- **Task 19: the worker serves border and footprint rasters** (`worker/raster-handlers.ts`, `geometry-handlers.ts`, `handlers.ts`, `geometry/raster/types.ts`).
  - Borders draw from the Natural Earth copy the geometry worker keeps from `parse-context`. Before it is parsed, a border prepare fails as internal.
  - A border tile deeper than the spec's `maximumLevel`, and footprint circles that are not finite `lon, lat, radius_km > 0` triples, fail as validation.
- **Task 20: ground heights** (new `scene/ground-heights.ts`).
  - `globe.getHeight` is sampled under each point, NaN where no tile has loaded, with longitudes wrapped into ±180° so the antimeridian finds its tiles.
  - The samples are re-read as tiles load, after a morph and on demand, at most every 50 ms unless final.
  - `afterNextRender` runs a callback after the frame that applies a relief change.
- **Task 21: the highlight on the ground** (`scene/highlight-layer.ts`). The outline is densified to 16 points per edge, 300 m above the ground, and stays continuous across the antimeridian.
- **Task 22: the observation group on the ground** (`scene/observation-symbols.ts`, `observation-layer.ts`).
  - Symbols are lifted by the ground with today's clearance kept. Only symbols whose ground changed are re-placed.
  - The group owns its `atlas-footprints` layer: α = fade × style opacity, shown while the group and its sampling areas are, and the rings hide while it is set.
- **Task 23: the context overlay drapes the borders and clamps the labels** (`scene/context-overlay.ts`, new `scene/border-drape.ts`, `scene-transition.ts`).
  - `setDraped` adds `atlas-borders` once the worker has parsed the context, and neither line renderer is built or shown while draped.
  - Labels take `RELATIVE_TO_GROUND` in a `LabelCollection` built with the scene.
  - An opacity change is instant. A colour change adds a new layer at α 0 and retires the old one once `whenLayerReady` sees its tiles.
  - A layer still preparing when a newer call supersedes it is dropped with its provider. The first failure of each layer goes to `onDrapeFailure`.
- **Task 24: ScientificLayers drapes the observations** (new `scene/observation-drapes.ts`; `scientific-layers.ts` stays at 986 of its 986 logical lines).
  - Each draped observation group in view gets its ground heights and its footprint layer.
  - A new sampling colour builds a new layer and keeps the old one until the new one is ready.
  - Groups on the smooth globe, out of view or pruned are released, and their footprints destroyed.
- **Task 25: scene wiring and relief** (`scene/atlas-scene.ts`, `earth-style-catalog.ts`).
  - Only the newest terrain flip reaches the overlay. The highlight is re-grounded as tiles load.
  - After each relief frame, the ground is re-sampled once the next frame has re-exaggerated the loaded tiles, and once more when the slider rests.
  - A border failure reverts the terrain like a surface one. The hidden-data warning is retired.
- **Task 26: end-to-end** (`scene/synthetic-terrain.ts`, `e2e-hooks.ts`, `tests/atlas-drape.spec.ts`).
  - The e2e build publishes `__atlasWindowOf` and `__atlasLabelClearance`.
  - On the synthetic peak at relief 6, then 10, a sphere grounded above the summit is picked, and Spain's label stands 2,500 m above the flank.
  - Footprints and borders drape in the design's layer order, a footprint ring shows on the summit's ground, and every Atlas layer leaves with World Terrain.
