## What changed

- Adds a shared Atlas activity model for initial catalog loading, dataset replacement, scene rendering, metric and geometry changes, marker updates, basemap and terrain changes, and view transitions.
- Reports real streamed-byte, primitive-readiness, and blend-animation progress where measurable; operations with no trustworthy total remain indeterminate.
- Keeps the current rendered map visible while replacement data loads and prevents superseded requests from overwriting newer operation state.
- Adds accessible progress semantics, an anti-flicker delay, responsive navbar treatment, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Splits runtime and place-resolution responsibilities out of AtlasExplorer to keep the component within the repository module budget.

Implements Atlas design §11.
