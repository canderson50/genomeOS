## Body

Closes #18, closes #112

Five commits. The through-line: AFND turned out to be reachable after all, which took the corpus
from two hand-curated variants to 767 alleles — and running the pipeline on uncurated input
immediately found things two variants could not.

## 1. AFND is not gated (#18)

The earlier finding that this data required a login was wrong, and wrong subtly: fetching a
population page and grepping for `Access Denied` matches, but **that string sits inside an HTML
comment** — an inert login template on every page. Two independent passes tested for a string
rather than for the absence of data.

Two public hops: `pop6001b.asp` lists every population as a `?pop_name=` link in one request;
`pop6001c.asp?pop_name=<name>` prints the coordinate. **1,821 of 1,825 harvested (99.8%)**, the
four refused genuinely printing no coordinate.

**Licence: AFND publishes none.** Proceeding on an assumed-open basis is the repo owner's decision
of 2026-08-26 (#117), recorded in the module docstring so it can be revisited. It governs
redistribution as much as collection; #66 still applies.

Retention went 1,127 → 1,821 by fixing two over-refusals: `Urban/Rural: Unknown` (534 rows, 29% of
the corpus) now takes the widest class in AFND's own vocabulary rather than a refusal, and
`unmappable_ascertainment` (160 rows) no longer drops populations from the **registry** over a
field the registry never stores — that guard belongs at P1 and still holds there.

## 2. Artifacts that outlive the process (#112)

§5 says artifacts are immutable and keyed `(variant_id, model_version, data_version)`, and nothing
wrote them. `publish()` **refuses to overwrite**; a refit publishes under a new `model_version` and
coexists with what it supersedes.

The HbS fit is **121 MB against a 1.1 MB artifact** — 110×, because the posterior carries a draw of
`f`, `p` and `freq_pred` per observation. Artifacts are committed; fits stay local. Both HbS
(ρ=680 km) and G6PD (ρ=547 km) are published here, the first surfaces that exist anywhere but a
stopped pod.

`.gitignore` needed `data/*` rather than `data/`: git will not descend into an excluded directory,
so a negation below an excluded parent silently has no effect.

## 3. Allele-frequency adapter — 80,382 observations, 767 alleles

Joined to the coordinates on population name, AFND's own key, shared by both sides. Two parsing
bugs found, both producing a *plausible refusal report* rather than an error:

- **Thousand separators.** AFND prints `3,732`; parsing without stripping refused 33,514 rows —
  27% of the table — as having *no sample size*, indistinguishable in a report from data that
  genuinely lacks one.
- **Double-counted refusals.** Conditions were evaluated over the whole frame rather than the rows
  still kept, so retained + refused exceeded the input. Retention read 46%; it is 65%.

Counts are **reconstructed** (`ac = round(af * 2n)`) because AFND publishes a frequency, not a
count; recorded in `assay` as `frequency_reconstructed`. `variant_id` gains an `hla:` form, since
an HLA allele is a haplotype of many linked variants named by the WHO committee, not a
chr-pos-ref-alt triple.

## 4. The cohort effect was not identifiable, and it mattered

Screening ten alleles: **5/10 converged**, and `cohort_sd` was the worst-mixing parameter in four
of five failures (r_hat to 1.123, ESS 23). Structural, not numerical — AFND publishes one study
per population, so `cohort_id == population_id` and the term has one level per observation.

`map_surveys`' docstring already described this about the design it rejected: *"one cohort level
per observation … is an observation-level overdispersion term, unidentifiable as the study-level
effect §7.1d wants and free to absorb the spatial signal the GP exists to explain."* MAP has 419
cohorts for 1,071 surveys; AFND has 1:1.

`fit_surface` now fits the term only with within-cohort replication and records
`beta_cohort_applied`, the same treatment §7.1a gives an unidentifiable design effect.

## Verified

`pytest` — **346 passed**. `ruff`, module-size, private-file, contract, smoke all clean. Both

## Not done, and filed rather than hidden

- **#122** — the five screening "successes" fitted ρ of 1,351–2,619 km against HbS's 680, every one
  tripping the spacing warning. That is the near-globally-constant regime of #116: they may have
  converged to a *degenerate* answer, and nothing flagged it. `lengthscale_sigma` cannot be
  hand-tuned across 767 alleles.
- **#123** — DKMS German donors carry `an` = 6,912,132, **23,040× the median**. A binomial
  likelihood weights by `an`, so one population can outweigh thousands. `needs-human-decision`.
- **#121** — whether dropping the cohort term is right, or whether AFND's `Author(s)` field (not
  currently harvested) would give real study grouping.

No golden-test validation for any HLA allele, and #109's finding that HbS predicts held-out
surveys barely better than a constant applies here too.

## For an expert reviewer

Whether a beta-binomial `concentration` correctly absorbs the overdispersion once the cohort term
is dropped — asserted here, not measured; and whether reconstructing counts from a four-decimal
frequency is acceptable for a likelihood that assumes measured counts.
