## Summary

On desktop, the globe is now drawn centred in the open area to the right of the controls panel, so the panel no longer covers its left side. The camera does not move. Only Cesium's projection centre shifts, so the URL view, fly-to, keyboard controls and picking keep their meaning.

Closes #452.

## How it works

- **Shift amount:** half the canvas width the panel covers, so the globe sits centred in what is left. It is 0 when the panel is hidden and in the phone layout. It is recomputed when the panel or canvas resizes, and when the panel is hidden or shown. The transient inspector does not move the globe.
- **`website/src/atlas/view-offset.ts`:** Cesium-free arithmetic.
- **`useGlobeOffset`:** the island measures the panel against the canvas and passes the covered width to the scene through a new `AtlasSceneController.setLeftObstruction`. It does this once when a scene binds, so the very first frame is already shifted.
- **`website/src/atlas/scene/globe-offset.ts`:** holds all the Cesium work. `atlas-scene.ts` gains three lines.

Cesium 1.145 does not honour a frustum `xOffset` end to end. Five gaps were found and closed, each pinned by unit tests on real Cesium objects:

1. **The offset is kept at any near plane.** Cesium renders through cloned frustums at other near planes, and its clone drops `xOffset`. A frustum subclass now keeps the offset as a fixed fraction of the frustum width and survives those clones.
2. **Pick rays account for the shift.** `Camera.getPickRay` ignores the offset; a per-camera override casts rays through what is drawn under the pointer.
3. **Pick passes cull the right region.** `scene.pick`, `drillPick` and the translucent depth pick cull to their own scissor rectangle.
4. **No white limb.** The globe's per-fragment ground atmosphere assumed a centred frustum and painted the far limb white. The shader is patched to use the real frustum planes; unshifted output is unchanged (mean difference 0.05–0.07/255).
5. **The camera controller works in a centred frame.** Without this, wheel zoom drifted 74–134 px; drift now matches a centred globe.

**Risk:** this relies on private Cesium 1.145 members. They are pinned by unit tests, and the shader patch throws on an unrecognised source, so a Cesium upgrade fails loudly rather than silently.

## Owner's calls

- **The inspector can cover the globe's centre on narrow desktops.** At 1280 px wide, an open inspector covers the shifted centre; it clears above about 1,340 px. The approved design counts only the controls panel. Counting the inspector as a right-hand obstruction would be a follow-up.
- **The 2D map is not shifted.** Cesium resets the orthographic frustum on every 2D view change, so that would be a separate change. Columbus view is shifted.
