## Body

What this changes, and why

https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/39 asks for evidence before contraction_threshold (still the provisional 0.9) is chosen, and before deciding whether the contraction test should also apply to observed cells. The canonical HbS run produced 0 prior_dominated cells, so the threshold has never been exercised. bschilder's https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/39#issuecomment-5690652787 asks that the next experiment "report contraction by provenance and observation AN before choosing the threshold". This PR adds that report so the next HbS fit can produce it.

genomeos/surfaces/mask.py gains contraction_report(cells, observations, config), a pure summary of the frame evaluate_cells already returns. Nothing is refitted or re-predicted.

    One row per support state, every state present even when empty (an empty group reports NaN quantiles, not a number): cell count, contraction quantiles (5/25/50/75/95%) and the share below the threshold.
    One row per allele-count bin of observed cells (AN_BIN_EDGES = 0, 50, 200, 1,000, ∞), where a cell's AN is the summed an of the observations whose centres fall in it.
    observed_not_contracted: observed cells at or above the threshold, i.e. the cells the first option in https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/39 would reclassify as prior_dominated.

It refuses missing columns, non-finite or negative contraction, unknown support states, and an observed cell that holds no observation centre at the configured resolution.

Scientific contract

    Claim: the threshold and the observed-before-contraction policy can be chosen from measured contraction by provenance and survey size, not from first principles.
    Output and acceptance evidence: a per-state and per-AN-bin table for any evaluated fit; 7 tests on hand-built cells pin counts, bins, quantiles, the reclassification count and every refusal.
    Component: contraction_report and the constants AN_BIN_EDGES and CONTRACTION_QUANTILES in genomeos/surfaces/mask.py. No change to classify_support, the threshold, aggregation or any schema.
    Assumptions and consumers: the AN bins are reporting bins, not thresholds, and are open to change. The consumer is the next HbS fit (the https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/45 reproduction or comparison run), whose report would go to https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/39. It decides nothing by itself.
