## Body

A sweep is hours of sampling that regenerates only by spending them again. The 17 AFND HLA fits
from the locus-stratified screen cost about two hours and lived in a **session scratchpad**, one
cleanup away from gone, because `SYNCED` covered `data/store/fits` and nothing else under
`data/store`.

Adds two entries:

- **`store/screen`** — the fits plus `sweep.json`, which carries the fitted range, observation
  count and inducing-point budget per allele. That summary is small and survives even if the
  pickles are pruned later, which matters because the pickles are 288 MB and the summary is 4 KB.
- **`store/surfaces`** — the rendered PNGs and their index.

Push grows from 227 MB to 552 MB. The repo stays **private**, which is what makes this storage
rather than redistribution under the assumed-open AFND decision in #117.

Also adds `data/raw/afnd_freq_cache` to `EXCLUDED`, so that list agrees with the
`SUMMARISE_ONLY` fix in the artifact-publishing PR. `EXCLUDED` is documentation rather than the
mechanism — `SYNCED` is an allowlist — but a stale one is misleading.

Verified: `ruff`, `smoke` and `check_private_files` pass; `push --dry-run` lists all nine entries.
The real push has run and the HF dataset now holds `store/screen`, `store/surfaces` and 22
artifacts.
