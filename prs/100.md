## Scientific and engineering contract

- maps P0–P5 scientific objectives to engineering interfaces and measurable acceptance evidence
- makes uncertainty, refusal, ascertainment, provenance, and governance explicit cross-cutting goals
- clarifies module dependency direction and the Pan-UKB evidence boundary

## Agent and code discipline

- requires objective-first planning and explicit assumptions/refusal conditions
- defines typed, versioned module boundaries and configuration rules
- targets 500 logical lines per module, with an enforced review ceiling of 800 lines or 50 KiB
- installs the matching Codex skill locally outside Git

## Verification and privacy

- adds a mandatory smoke runner and CI step
- adds module-size and tracked-private-file CI gates
- blocks Codex state, real environment files, credentials, keys, common token formats, and personal
  email addresses
- removes hard-coded personal GCP account identities; account selection is now explicit and local
