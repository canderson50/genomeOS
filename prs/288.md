## TL;DR

We publish a diagnostic picture that is supposed to show why a particular modelling shortcut is
wrong. The picture was confusing in a way that undermined its own argument: the left map coloured
distance to every survey site, while the map beside it was actually driven by 64 specific locations
the model uses. The two maps looked geographically unrelated even though one is derived from the
other.

This rebuilds the figure so the whole chain is visible, from the 684 real survey coordinates,
through the 64 model locations derived from them, to the artifact those locations produce.

---

The prior-normalization review figure obscured its own mechanism: panel A colored distance to all survey sites, while the mapped artifact is driven by the 64 retained model locations. That made the two maps look geographically unrelated even though the model locations were derived from the survey geography.

This revision rebuilds the diagnostic around the 684 retained public MAP HbS survey coordinates and makes the full chain visible. Panel A shows the survey sites, the 64 geographically balanced model locations derived from survey-containing H3 cells, and distance to those retained locations. Panel B repeats the same model locations over the scalar-denominator artifact. Panel C shows the error against the same computational distance and retains the exact local-ratio control at 1.0. Country borders are explicitly identified as orientation only because the covariance uses spherical distance and has no political, terrain, coastal, or ecological covariate.

The 64 locations are selected by deterministic maximin traversal over one vectorized pairwise great-circle distance matrix. They are passed through production H3 placement with an equal budget, so model locations remain collocated with survey-supported cells. Reversing the survey input order leaves the locations and ratios unchanged. This is a figure-only stability measure; it does not claim to resolve the production placement issue tracked in #280.

The measured allele-frequency values do not enter this no-update counterexample. The input CSV matches the committed inventory SHA-256 `ab92edd3059aa59a37f3e2d10361d67e490c573fc3c03bf13eb902e3820e98fd`.
