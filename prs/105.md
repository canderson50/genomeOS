Stacked on #98 (`feat/observation-figures`). Merge that first.

The HSGP basis cannot reach the resolution this map needs, and no amount of GPU changes that. Replaces it with an inducing-point GP whose points sit on the H3 geodesic sphere.

## Was the model wasting compute on the sphere's interior?

**Yes, and it's measurable.** The HSGP domain is a cube of volume 26.6; the unit sphere is 16% of that; and the data lies on the sphere's **zero-volume shell**. At m=32 that's 32,768 basis functions where a 2-D manifold at the same angular resolution needs ~m² = 1,024 — roughly **32× spent describing the Earth's interior and the empty corners of a box.**

The inducing GP fixes it by construction: every inducing point has radius 1.000000, exactly on the surface.

## Why H3 rather than some other geodesic sphere

**H3 already is one.** It subdivides an icosahedron, so cells are near-uniform in area with no polar pile-up — the failure mode of a lat/lon grid, where cells collapse to slivers at the poles and the model spends its degrees of freedom on the Arctic.

It's also the grid §6 already renders, so the inducing points and the artifact cells become the same objects, and §7's resolution-promotion rule can drive the **model** rather than only the mask: seed at res 4, promote to 5/6 where observation density justifies it.

## Results

| | HSGP m=32 | inducing, H3 M=1000 |
|---|---|---|
| parameters | 32,768 | **1,000** |
| spacing | 597 km, uniform everywhere | **47 km** |
| coverage | — | **857/857 observations within 300 km** (median 17 km) |
| fitted correlation range | 1,756 km | **436 km** |

The correlation range collapsing is the diagnostic that matters: the model was never *choosing* to over-smooth, it had no basis to express anything shorter.

H3 also beats k-means placement, which was my first attempt:

| placement | spacing median | IQR/median | at M=1000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **h3** | 47 km | **0.06** | fine |
| kmeans | 30 km | 3.28 | **degenerate** — 857 of 1000 points, p10 spacing 2 km (stacking) |

## One bug worth recording, because the symptom looked like success

Selecting cells with `sorted(cells)[:n]` truncates by **lexicographic H3 index**, which is geographically arbitrary. It picked a contiguous block on a single icosahedral face and put **every inducing point in the Arctic** — a median 8,800 km from the nearest observation, with *zero* observations within 300 km — while reporting beautifully uniform 44 km spacing, because the points were all neighbours in one small patch.

**Uniform spacing is necessary and nowhere near sufficient.** Selection is now by distance to the nearest observation, and the regression test checks *coverage*, not spacing.
