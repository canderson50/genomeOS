## TL;DR

This stack lets the exact beta-binomial scorer handle observations with very large denominators, then carries the same exact-count contract through faster interval search, GPU batching, immutable fold checkpoints, convergence gates, and the retained B1/B2 development reports. The final numerical repair fixes the large-count/high-concentration cancellation found in independent review without changing the probability law, fitting budget, split, seed, score, or promotion rule.

- **Bounded beta-binomial log scoring (#314):** starts from the nearer endpoint and evaluates two equivalent rising-factorial factorizations using a 16-factor exact prefix and fixed Euler–Maclaurin tail. Near zero, the tail-log ratio uses one algebraically differenced `log1p`; when the same argument has magnitude at least 0.5, it directly subtracts the two `log1p` terms. This avoids forming a rounded complement near negative one. Draws remain vectorized, and work/memory do not grow with AN. The old AN=65,536 GP/B0H preflight limit is removed while MAX_COUNT and shape validation remain.
- **Verified quantile bracket (#316):** computes the exact predictive-mixture mean and variance, proposes an upper count bound using Cantelli's inequality, verifies it with the selected exact CDF, and falls back to AN if needed. The existing left-continuous binary search still determines the answer. The 100% endpoint remains the exact mixture support. No normal approximation or sampled quantile is substituted.
- **Exact CuPy batching (#318):** plans row/draw/support chunks from current free device memory, capped at 8 × 256 × 2,048. It refuses insufficient memory and has no silent CPU fallback. Inactive complementary-tail rows contribute zero support. It does not reduce draws, precision, or distributional fidelity.
- **Fold checkpoints (#319):** completed, failed, and infeasible folds are immutable terminal artifacts. Public write/load/finalization and CLI pre-fit validation bind the complete ordered split identity to the frozen header. Resume rejects changed inputs, configuration, source/package identity, malformed ledgers, and corrupt artifacts; terminal failures are not retried. Final publication remains atomic.
- **Prospective convergence safeguards (#333):** completed folds must retain numerical R-hat, bulk/tail ESS, and divergence evidence satisfying their frozen gates. Checkpoint and final GP manifest schemas are v2; schema-v1 checkpoints are explicitly refused. The GP summary remains v1. Frozen production table contracts do not change.
- **B1 and B2 reporting (#307 and #189):** the local-count comparator preserves explicit support/refusal and count-predictive semantics. Its public finalizer and reader reject identity contradictions and incomplete output. A separate version-2 B1 report adds post-hoc denominator/distance strata without rewriting the historical result. The matched B2 report remains development evidence and remains unqualified.

## Numerical evidence

An independent 220-digit Decimal oracle, shifted to 256 before 15 Bernoulli corrections, reproduced the prior high-count failure and audited 194 deterministic endpoint, interior, reflection, high-concentration, tiny-shape, million-scale, MAX_COUNT, and seeded random cases. All 194 are within the declared `1e-5` absolute log-mass envelope; the worst error is `7.569789886474609e-6`.

The two former blockers now have errors of `2.6197549729545244e-6` and `1.757405698299408e-6`, down from `0.05461049364834736` and `0.07381805684417486`. The committed 75-case high-shape cross-product has maximum error `8.158385753631592e-6`; the 2,571,112-allele central stress case has error `9.313225746154785e-10`. Component-ratio probes immediately below and above both ±0.5 branch boundaries agree with the independent oracle, mixed vector evaluation equals scalar evaluation, and no floating warning is emitted.

Permanent tests cover AN=1 Bernoulli identities, MAX_COUNT endpoints/interiors, million-scale counts, component ratios, high-shape mixtures and allele reflection. Existing tests retain small-support normalization, exact degenerate/impossible outcomes, near-one complement precision, bounded allocation, and strict validation. The final patch changes only two algebraically equal evaluations of the same tail-log ratio; an ordinary 2,048-draw × 10-observation comparison was bit-identical to the prior head.

The repair adds a small CPU cost because NumPy evaluates the direct terms needed by the guarded branch. A paired local microbenchmark over 21 alternating rounds measured a 2.78% median slowdown for `CountPredictive.log_prob` on 2,048 draws × 10 heterogeneous-denominator observations. The separately reported 4.7% delta is therefore plausible as workload/run variation. Both are microbenchmark overheads, not a change in the statistical model or an end-to-end benchmark result.
