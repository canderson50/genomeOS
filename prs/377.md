## Body

## TL;DR

When a B1G model fit fails the project’s convergence checks, it now gets the one retry promised by the modeling plan: twice as many sampling and tuning steps, with a new deterministic seed. Both attempts are saved for review. Any second failure remains a failure, and other errors do not trigger a retry.

Closes #376.

## What changed

- Add a pure two-slot fit boundary with `initial` and `retry` attempts.
- Admit the retry only for the typed `B1GConvergenceError`; structural, runtime, prediction, scoring, and interruption failures do not retry.
- Double only `draws` and `tune`, use a distinct deterministic seed, and keep the model, priors, chains, target acceptance, convergence thresholds, data, and basis unchanged.
- Apply the same policy to inner candidate fits and untouched outer fits.
- Record both attempt configs, seeds, statuses, reasons, and sampler diagnostics in checkpoint schema v3.
- Validate attempt evidence against the frozen config and derived seeds, and expose it in `fold_status.tsv` and `inner_folds.tsv`.
- Freeze the retry policy and source hash in the campaign manifest.

Terminal checkpoint shards remain immutable. The retry happens inside the originally requested fold execution; resuming a completed or failed shard cannot create another attempt.

## Scientific contract

- **Objective:** apply WP2’s one-retry convergence policy without changing scientific gates or reacting to held-out scores.
- **Acceptance evidence:** accepted initial, accepted retry, failed retry, non-retryable failure, prediction failure, deterministic seed, immutable checkpoint, and serialization tests all pass.
- **Interface:** `run_b1g_fit_attempts` executes the frozen pair; `validate_b1g_fit_attempts` verifies retained evidence.
- **Refusals:** malformed/missing attempts, changed budgets, reused seeds, invalid status sequences, diagnostics that contradict convergence gates, and any third attempt fail explicitly.

Implements design §§4–8 and 12 and WP2 of `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-09-global-af-modeling.md`.

## Expert review requested

Please confirm that WP2’s “one logged retry with doubled draws/tuning” should apply to both nested candidate fits and untouched outer fits, and that a distinct deterministic retry seed preserves the intended benchmark estimand. No convergence threshold, promotion threshold, prior, or target-acceptance setting changes here.
