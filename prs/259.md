## The safeguard that earned its place

The round-trip check — complement the printed alleles under the recorded strand, and they must equal reference and alternate — **has no power for palindromic alleles**, because `A/T` and `C/G` complement to themselves. Those rows must carry separate `strand_evidence` or they refuse.

This caught the plan's own seed row on the first attempt: HbS is `T/A`, and the plan supplied empty evidence.

For `cyt:il-6-174-c` (`G/C`) the strand rests on two independent arguments, both stated in the row. A co-named non-palindromic locus (rs1800797, −597) has plus-strand reference `A`, which a minus reading of that promoter scheme cannot produce. And an orientation test against this repo's own published observations: a minus reading would put the allele the id calls minor at 0.75–0.86 globally, against a published median of 0.304 across 83 populations, contradicting `MINOR_ALLELE_RULE`. The row says out loud that the second argument uses a method design §6 rejects for step 1, and why that does not apply at 0.30 versus 0.86.

## What this does not do

**The four cytokine loci are not yet usable**, by design and in two separate ways.

They are `pending`, and #242 must settle whether agent-resolved rows can be agent-verified before any can be `verified`. And separately, #256: the branch gates on the registry but never *consumes* it, so even a verified row would still be refused by an equality check in the exporter and its TypeScript twin. Both are documented in `docs/audits/variant-normalization-2026-09.md`; neither is a surprise to discover later.
