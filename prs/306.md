## TL;DR

We tested whether the model should use a special explanation for surveys that found no copies of the sickle-cell variant. It handled those zero results better but predicted new survey data worse overall, especially where the variant was observed, so this approach is rejected.

---

The canonical HbS fit leaves a supported non-endemic background near 1%, and the Piel flexible-link preflight in #305 moves that operating point in the wrong direction. Issue #103 names a hurdle/nonstationary model as a later alternative, but treating every zero count as absence would be scientifically invalid. This PR runs the smallest safe precursor: a cohort-blocked scalar comparison of the existing beta-binomial against a zero-inflated beta-binomial that preserves ordinary sampling-zero probability.

The result rejects the candidate before any spatial fit. Across 963 eligible observations from 384 whole-cohort groups, the zero-inflated model changes cohort-macro held-out log score by **−0.00133 nats/observation** and row-weighted score by **−0.00784**. It improves zero rows by +0.0742 but worsens the 763 positive rows by −0.0293; four small fold gains are outweighed by one −0.0931 fold. The full-data mixture estimates 7.9% extra-zero mass, raises its conditional mean from 4.36% to 4.75%, and leaves the marginal mean slightly higher at 4.37%. The predeclared gate therefore records `advance_to_spatial_mixture: false`.

The pure module provides vectorized normalized beta-binomial/mixture mass, deterministic multi-start scalar fitting, a descriptive structural-zero profile, exact row-order reproducibility, and hard refusal when a cohort is split across folds. The offline runner emits only aggregate folds, profiles, source/input hashes, a receipt, and a review figure. It explicitly records no spatial fit, no publication eligibility, no observed-zero absence labels, and no environmental covariates. This advances #103; it does not close it because localized/nonstationary alternatives and expert review remain open.

This implements Atlas design §§7–8. It gives MAP no source-family priority: only the count-bearing HbS observations enter because they are the benchmark under diagnosis. MAP malaria-risk, EVI, friction, and other modeled layers remain separate WP3 hypotheses requiring variant-specific mechanism and controlled incremental validation.
