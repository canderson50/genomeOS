## Scientific objective

Closes #159. Implements the literature-evidence design (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-literature-evidence-design.md) §§5.1-5.3, 6, and 8-10: a real, small, predeclared corpus slice — 12 rs4988235 (LCT/MCM6 -13910 C>T) population measurements transcribed from the ORIGINAL paper's genotype counts (Enattah et al. 2007 Am J Hum Genet 81:615, PMID 17701907), staged under the frozen literature contracts.

Why genotype counts: our pilot corpus (manpreetbola/protective-alleles-gnomad-v4) was transcribed from compiled frequency tables (Liebert 2017 Supp Table 3), which print allele frequencies, not counts. Frequencies cannot promote as exact counts. Enattah 2007 Table 3 prints N and CC/CT/TT genotype counts, which yield exact T-13910 counts via the allowlisted derivation `allele_count_from_genotypes`. Comparing our compiled-table values against the original genotype counts for all 34 pilot rows citing this paper: 24 match exactly, 9 were corrected upstream (pilot repo v3, commit 31eda87), 1 (French) is internally inconsistent in the source and left for adjudication.
