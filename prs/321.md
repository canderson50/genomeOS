## TL;DR

A long map evaluation can retain each finished test group and resume after an interruption. Saved results are now checked against the complete frozen split record, so a caller cannot reuse a result with different training data, exclusions or geography. Failed groups remain saved and are never quietly retried.

## Checkpoint behavior and review correction

Every terminal fold—completed, failed or infeasible—is published as an immutable, integrity-hashed artifact before the next fit begins. Fresh runs require an explicit `--checkpoint-dir`; resumes require `--resume-from`. The header covers actual input bytes, resolved configuration/backend, ordered splits, seed schedule, source/code identity, packages and qualification. Final benchmark publication uses a separate staging directory.

The review correction binds every supplied `BenchmarkSplit` field to the canonical record at its exact header ordinal: training/test IDs, held-out `block_id`, excluded IDs and reasons, input fingerprint, buffer, minimum edge separation, data version and split ID. Missing, duplicate, extra or reordered caller splits are refused, even for an empty checkpoint. The complete planned-ledger shape and digest are validated independently. There is no separate `held_out_block_ids` field in this schema; `block_id` names the held-out block.

The public `finalize_checkpoint_benchmark` adapter reloads the complete header-bound terminal ledger before calling the pure finalizer. The runner validates its caller split ledger before fitting and uses that adapter before publication. Mismatches raise; they are not rewritten, migrated or retried. Existing atomic publication, negative-infinite count scores, and valid failed/infeasible reuse remain intact.

The current checkpoint schema and final GP manifest are **v2**; the summary document remains v1. Schema-v1 checkpoints are refused because they lack retained convergence evidence. The earlier prospective convergence repair requires completed folds to retain passing R-hat, bulk/tail ESS and zero-divergence diagnostics at write, load and finalization. No sampler budget, prior, split-construction rule, threshold, score, source priority or promotion criterion changed in this review fix.
