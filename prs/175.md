## Scientific and product contract

Measured observations must remain visibly separate from inferred surfaces and must never be clipped by the surface presentation. Coordinates, marker encodings, sampling radii, provenance, and artifact values remain unchanged; globe depth testing stays enabled so far-side observations remain occluded.

Closes #170

## What changed

- Samples the exact rendered H3 triangle beneath each observation with barycentric interpolation for smooth triangles and both Honmoon modes.
- Aligns studs and pins to the active local surface normal and updates that axis during elevation animation.
- Keeps flat hexagon and extruded-cell anchors on their cell top without paying the smooth-mesh calculation cost.
- Replaces the circular sphere sprite with a low elliptical surface-mounted stud plus bounded eye offset.
- Renames the visible Hemispheres option to Studs while preserving the shareable internal `hemisphere` value and `obsShape=hemisphere` URL contract.
- Rebuilds observation anchors when surface geometry changes while retaining in-place opacity, size, and elevation updates.

Implements Atlas design §11 and the observation ordering/elevation contract in the Cesium explorer interaction design §§7.2–7.3.
