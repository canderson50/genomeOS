## TL;DR

We tested whether forcing the sickle-cell map to vary less across geography would remove its false background signal. It instead raised that background and weakened high-frequency peaks, so both tested settings are rejected without another full training run.

---

The canonical 994-survey HbS fit has a supported ~1% Nordic background and flattened endemic peaks. Issue #103 names a tighter spatial-amplitude prior as a cheap possible remedy; this PR freezes and runs the smallest reliable preflight before another GPU fit.

The result rejects that route. Self-normalized importance reweighting of the exact 500 draw-aligned global fields remains numerically usable for both candidates, but the posterior compensates for smaller amplitude by raising its intercept:

| HalfNormal scale | Importance ESS | Nordic median | Endemic peak median | Peak − Nordic | Decision |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|:---:|
| 1.00 | 500.0 | 0.9843% | 10.9717% | 9.9281 pp | reference |
| 0.75 | 473.7 | 0.9979% | 10.9294% | 9.8880 pp | reject |
| 0.50 | 285.6 | 1.0223% | 10.8311% | 9.8331 pp | reject |

Both tighter priors therefore raise the unwanted background and flatten peaks further. No full refit is recommended. This advances #103, #45, and #189 without closing them; a localized/nonstationary comparator and held-out count validation remain separate work.

The implementation adds a pure vectorized sensitivity core, a hash-bound offline adapter, failing-test-first coverage, a preregistration, deterministic aggregate evidence, and a geography-explicit review figure. The two diagnostic cell strata are reduced in one matrix multiplication; there is no observation-by-cell or draw-by-cell Python loop. No model, surface, serving path, schema, or environmental covariate changes.

Implements Atlas design §§7–8 and advances the global AF plan WP2/WP4.

## Independent audit

The September 20 audit used the retained private fit, draw manifest, global frequency matrix, and cell table. All four files match the SHA-256 values frozen in the preregistration. Running this PR's adapter over those inputs reproduced the committed JSON and PNG byte-for-byte.

An independent NumPy/SciPy calculation reproduced the prior-density ratios, weighted summaries, importance ESS, and maximum weights. A separately labeled post hoc bootstrap with 5,000 draw resamples found:

- scale 0.75: Nordic median change 95% interval **+0.21 to +2.41 basis points**; peak **−8.35 to −0.36**; peak-minus-Nordic **−8.34 to −1.13**;
- scale 0.50: Nordic median change **+1.16 to +7.28 basis points**; peak **−20.98 to −3.44**; peak-minus-Nordic **−26.56 to −4.01**.

The pooled direction is stable under resampling. Chain subsets are noisier at the 0.75 scale, so this remains an aggregate posterior sensitivity and not a claim that every chain independently resolves the small change. It is still sufficient for the preregistered stop decision: neither candidate shows the required lower-background, preserved-contrast direction.
