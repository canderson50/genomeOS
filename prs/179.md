## Summary

- restore the original world-sized 3D observation marker as a separate **Spheres** option
- make **Spheres** the default at 100% marker opacity while preserving explicit shared URL choices
- keep **Domes** as the stable screen-space option and preserve existing obsShape=hemisphere links
- anchor both Spheres and Domes above the exact rendered statistical surface, cell outlines, and observation-radius rings while retaining far-side globe occlusion
- give Spheres mutable equal radii and surface-normal clearance so size/elevation transitions update in place
- make marker opacity the complete body alpha for Domes and Spheres
- widen the statistical-elevation height slider across the full surface-control grid
- replace the Atlas introduction with “Visualize measured and predicted allele frequencies across the world”
- reduce left-control and right-inspector typography to 80% of its previous rendered size, and cap inspectors at 23.2rem on desktop

## Scientific contract

1. **Product claim:** measured observations remain visually distinct, stable, and legible above inferred surfaces; fresh sessions begin with fully opaque 3D Spheres.
2. **Acceptance evidence:** geometry tests verify equal-axis Spheres whose lowest point clears the surface/rings; camera/elevation tests verify screen-upright Domes; URL tests verify Sphere and opacity defaults plus explicit-link preservation; browser tests verify selector behavior, physical units, compact typography, and a height slider occupying at least 72% of the available surface-control width.
3. **Engineering interface:** ObservationShape, URL state, measured-point controls, Cesium observation primitives, scientific-layer orchestration, and responsive Atlas control styling.
4. **Assumptions/refusals/consumers:** observation coordinates, scientific values, color encodings, sampling radii, surface artifacts, and depth occlusion remain unchanged. Spheres use physical kilometre radii; Domes, circles, and pins use screen pixels. Explicit shape and opacity URL parameters continue to win over defaults.
