## TL;DR

We compared the worldwide sickle-cell map with a simple model that uses one average everywhere, using the same 994 survey results kept out of training. The map predicts substantially better and reduces average error by 21.1%, but its uncertainty ranges and some fitting runs remain unreliable, so it is promising rather than publication-ready.

---

The current HbS spatial-GP benchmark needed an exact matched comparison with its pooled B0 baseline; otherwise a global average could hide row drift, zero-count regressions, or geographic failures. This PR implements the reduction frozen in [issue #189](https://github.com/bschilder/genomeOS/issues/189#issuecomment-5705882045) while only two of five B2 folds existed, then publishes the hash-verified terminal result. It advances #189 and #333 without closing either.

All five folds completed on the exact 994 matched held-out rows. B2 improves balanced macro log score by **+316.44 nats** with an exhaustive paired five-block interval of **[+120.78, +543.84]**, and reduces macro MAE by **21.1%**. Both positive-count and zero-count strata improve. B2 repairs B0's extreme undercoverage, but 50% and 80% coverage overcorrect to 61.6% and 87.3%; only 95% coverage meets the existing ±3-point gate. All five fits also emitted R-hat-above-1.01 warnings and the final fit reported one divergence. Dependency review remains incomplete, so scientific promotion stays `not_made`.

The reducer requires the two exact frozen observation serializations, byte-identical assignments/dependencies, identical parsed split fingerprints and memberships, exact held-out keys and AC/AN, complete folds, valid artifact hashes, summary replay within explicit machine-rounding tolerances, and the hash-bound terminal sampler-warning ledger. It reports balanced macro metrics, all 3,125 ordered five-block bootstrap resamples, zero/positive-count and regional strata, and every finite/infinite paired-score state. The compact public JSON excludes row predictions, fold test IDs, and cohort-level records.
