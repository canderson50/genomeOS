## Body

## TL;DR

A benchmark fold could be saved as complete even when its stored reliability checks showed that the fit had not converged. Completed folds now need passing checks when they are produced, saved, loaded, and included in the final report. Contradictory saved results raise an error before more fitting or publication.

## Problem and result

The spatial-GP benchmark retains maximum rank R-hat, minimum bulk and tail ESS, the responsible parameter names, and the divergence count. Fresh fits that violate their declared gates become immutable failed folds with no predictions.

The artifact boundaries now independently enforce the same rule. Checkpoint writing and loading use the frozen header's explicit convergence thresholds; finalization uses the frozen plan. Missing or inconsistent gate metadata is refused. An integrity-hashed artifact labeled `completed` cannot pass when its retained diagnostics fail those gates. Failed and infeasible folds remain reusable without retry; schema-v1 checkpoints remain unsupported.

Closes #333.

## Scientific scope

Implements design §§5, 7–8 and 12. Existing general surface-fit admission remains unchanged; zero divergences and the tail-ESS floor are enforced at the benchmark evidence boundary. This change does not alter priors, sampler budgets, thresholds, splits, seeds, prediction semantics, retries or production serving.

The old B2 result is **not retroactively convergence-qualified**. Its immutable artifacts and descriptive development status are preserved. A new diagnostic-preserving run would create separate evidence; no new scientific fit or promotion is included here.
