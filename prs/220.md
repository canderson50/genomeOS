## Scientific objective

Bind each B0H simulation fit invocation to its declared case, training rows,
configuration and seed; retain the exact fit, failure or identity-rejection
evidence. Advances #211 and #189, closes neither. Implements Atlas design
§§5,7–8,12. Stacked on #218 (`feat/global-af-sbc-generation`).

This is a one-attempt orchestration component. B0H remains a nonspatial
baseline ablation within the larger modeling program.

## Implementation

- Pure public planner, dataset-bound fit-identity guard, one-call attempt
  boundary and separate structural-refusal check, with frozen typed outcomes.
- Exact initial/retry budgets and predeclared purpose-4 seeds. One explicit
  attempt call invokes the public fitter once; no automatic retry or reseeding.
- All sixteen training rows, including AN0, are passed unchanged. Returned
  axes, labels, unavailable IDs, positive-AN counts and configuration must match.
- Actual known failures retain class/message and available convergence evidence;
  unknown exceptions propagate to the future execution adapter. Typed rejected
  fits stay auditable but cannot be used as accepted results.
- Actual all-unavailable public refusals are tested with graph/sampler entry
  sentinels. Other fit returns are controlled fixtures, not measured convergence.

Matching labels and totals is not proof of rowwise input bytes. Later artifact
consumers must bind source/runtime/dataset content and rerun the public guard.
Durable retry authorization, quantities, controls and the full study remain
separate work. No fitter, generator, prior or numerical gate changes.
