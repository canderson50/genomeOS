## What the adapter does

Reads a TSV whose columns are named for AFND's own population-page field labels (`Population:`, `Latitude:`, `Longitude:`, `Family:`, `Urban/Rural:`, and `Source:` under Sample Data) plus `pop_id`, the accession in the page URL. Coordinates stay in AFND's printed sexagesimal form (`6º 25' S`) rather than being converted upstream, **because the printed precision is what bounds the coordinate and converting first throws it away.**

### `uncertainty_radius_km` — derived per row, never defaulted

```
radius = max(settlement-class extent, coordinate-precision floor)
```

**Settlement-class extent** comes from AFND's own `Urban/Rural` controlled vocabulary ([`datasets.asp` §1c](http://www.allelefrequencies.net/datasets.asp)):

| AFND value | radius | why |
|---|---|---|
| `Rural (Villages, hamlets)` | 50 km | the same kind of object HGDP places at 50 km; using a different figure for the same object would be arbitrary |
| `Urban (Cities, towns)` | 100 km | a city draws on a metropolitan catchment |
| `Urban and Rural` | 250 km | AFND records the sample as spanning both settlement types, so only a regional extent bounds it |
| `Unknown` / blank | **refused** | §6 gives the field no default |

§7 places each observation as a disc of this radius, so a too-large radius spreads an observation's evidence and makes it *less* influential, while a too-small one lets a diffuse sample act as a pinpoint measurement — erring coarse is the safe direction, the same reasoning already reviewed in `map_surveys`.

**Coordinate-precision floor.** AFND prints coordinates at whatever precision the submitter supplied, and much of the corpus is degrees-only (`8º 0' N`). The floor is the radius of the disc containing the printed quantisation cell — half the diagonal of a cell `q` tall by `q·cos(lat)` wide, one arcminute of latitude being one nautical mile (1.852 km) by definition. **Trailing zeros are not significant figures**, so `8º 0' N` is read as degree precision, giving a ~78 km floor at that latitude that exceeds both the village and city extents. A coordinate cannot be more certain than the way it was written down.

This is a per-population radius rather than a panel-wide constant, which is the direction #21 asks for; #21 stays open for the panels that still use the 50 km default.

### `location_type` — from AFND's `Family:` field

`Grandparents live at same location` is the only per-population statement AFND makes that the coordinate is where the population is *from* rather than merely where it was collected, so that alone yields `ancestral`. `Parents live in the same location` (two generations) and `Not Known` yield `sampling`; §4's warning about diaspora sampling sites means the default direction has to be `sampling`.

### Ascertainment (§7.1) — recorded, not invented

`POPULATIONS_SCHEMA` has no ascertainment columns; they belong to `OBSERVATIONS_SCHEMA` (P1). So the adapter **cannot** emit `sampling_design`, `disease_ascertainment_excluded` or `cohort_id`, and it does not fabricate a place to put them. Instead it publishes the reviewed mapping from AFND's `Source:` controlled vocabulary ([`datasets.asp` §1f](http://www.allelefrequencies.net/datasets.asp)) and refuses what the vocabulary cannot express:

| AFND `Source:` | `sampling_design` | `disease_ascertainment_excluded` |
|---|---|---|
| Anthropology Study | `convenience` | false |
| Blood Donor / Blood Donor Registry | `healthy_reference` | **true** |
| Bone Marrow Registry | `healthy_reference` | **true** |
| Stem Cell Donors | `healthy_reference` | **true** |
| Solid Organ Unrelated Donors | `healthy_reference` | **true** |
| Coord Blood Donors *(AFND's spelling of cord blood)* | `healthy_reference` | **true** |
| Controls for Disease Study | `clinical_control` | **true** |
| Disease Study Patients | `clinical_case` | false |
| `Other` / blank / outside the vocabulary | — | **row refused** |

Three judgement calls worth arguing with:

1. **Donor registries are flagged disease-depleted.** Donor-eligibility criteria remove significant disease *by policy*, which is exactly what §6 means by "any panel that removed disease cohorts by policy". This is the bone-marrow-registry ascertainment §7.1 exists to record, and it is a large share of AFND.
2. **`Other` is refused, not folded into `convenience`.** §7.1a identifies `β_design` by contrast *between* designs; pouring populations of unrecorded design into a named level biases that level's estimate. A population AFND does not ascertain can never yield a schema-valid observation, so admitting its coordinate would only invite a later adapter to substitute a default.
3. **`Anthropology Study` maps to `convenience`, not `population_random`.** These are community panels recruited through local contact — the same kind of object as HGDP, not surveys drawn from a sampling frame. `population_random` is the anchor that identifies `β_design` (MAP's screening surveys), and over-claiming it would corrupt the anchor.

Disease cohorts are **recorded rather than refused** — a clinical cohort is a valid observation whose bias `β_design` exists to correct.

### Refusals

`load()` returns `(populations, aliases, RegistryReport)`; the report counts every refusal by reason, so nothing is silently dropped (§12). Reasons are tested most-fundamental-first: `unusable_pop_id`, `missing_population_name`, `unreadable_latitude`, `unreadable_longitude`, `no_sampling_area`, `unmappable_ascertainment`. `genomeos/registry/sources/__init__.py` now states this as part of the adapter contract; `hgdp` refuses nothing and still returns the pair alone.

`population_id` is `afnd-<pop_id>` — AFND's own accession, the way `map_surveys` keys on the survey id. The population *name* goes to the aliases table, which is what it is for, and a repeated name therefore surfaces as an `AliasCollisionError` from `registry.build` rather than silently splitting a population.

## What I could NOT verify, and why

- **The adapter has never been run against the real AFND corpus.** No licence, no bulk download, frequency data behind a login, and 1,324 pages — see the licence table. Everything below therefore rests on AFND's *documented* schema plus three population records I read directly, not on the full 1,324:
  - **The refusal rate is unknown.** 56% retention on a fixture built to exercise refusals says nothing about the real corpus. I would expect `no_sampling_area` to dominate — one of the three real records I read (`India Tamil Nadu Nadar`, pop_id 1500) has `Urban/Rural: Unknown` — but I cannot put a number on it.
  - **The share of degrees-only coordinates is unknown**, and it drives how often the precision floor binds. Two of three records I read carried arcminutes; one carried `8º 0' N`.
  - **The `Source:` vocabulary's real distribution is unknown**, so I cannot say how much of AFND the donor-registry `healthy_reference` branch actually covers, nor how many rows `Other` will refuse.
  - **Display-form variants may be incomplete.** AFND's CV page says `Blood Donor Registry`; the population pages print `Blood Donor`. I key both, plus `Coord Blood Donors` and the `Cord Blood Donors` correction — but I have only seen two of the nine terms rendered in the wild, so other short forms may exist and would currently be refused as `unmappable_ascertainment`. That is the safe direction (refuse, don't guess), and the report makes it visible the first time someone runs the real corpus.
- **No figure.** A map of five licence-limited fixture rows would show nothing true about the corpus, and the registry is coordinates rather than a rendered layer; the derivation table above is the reviewable artifact instead. When the corpus arrives, an AFND observations layer belongs in the #117 PR with a real map.

## What an expert should check

- **The three extent figures (50 / 100 / 250 km).** The *direction* is argued from §7 (coarse is safe) and the rural figure is anchored on HGDP's existing 50 km, but the urban and urban-and-rural numbers are my judgement, not a citation. If a popgen reviewer prefers different numbers, they are three constants in `SAMPLING_AREA_RADIUS_KM` and the tests are written against the relative ordering as much as the values.
- **"Trailing zeros are not significant."** This coarsens ~1 in 60 genuinely-exact arcminute coordinates to degree precision. I think erring coarse is right, but it is an assumption about how AFND submitters wrote their coordinates.
- **`Anthropology Study` → `convenience` vs `population_random`** — this one materially affects `β_design`.
- **Donor registries → `disease_ascertainment_excluded = true`** — is donor eligibility "removed disease cohorts by policy" in §6's sense? I argue yes; it is worth a second opinion.

## Scope

`genomeos/registry/` only.
