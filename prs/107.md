## Objective

Prove the minimal serving path from immutable Atlas artifacts to a browser without inference,
cloud storage, or a product frontend on the interactive path.

## What changed

- versioned manifest plus deterministic demo observation/surface Parquet artifacts
- concrete local artifact catalog with bounded, projected DuckDB queries
- readiness, variant, observation, and surface endpoints
- server-side surface eligibility check
- structured JSON service, request, readiness, and artifact-query logging
- correlated `X-Request-ID` responses without logging headers, cookies, bodies, or configuration
- dependency-free SVG diagnostic preview at `/preview`
- lean serving dependency extra; no PyMC/JAX in the serving image
- read-only artifact mount in Compose and container HTTP smoke in CI
- Docker build-context exclusions for local/private state

## Deliberate limits

- local files only; no GCS abstraction yet
- no burden endpoint or polygon aggregation
- no BigQuery, CDN, or cache layer
- no Next.js, deck.gl, MapLibre, or product UI
- demo artifacts are synthetic diagnostics, not scientific results, and are mounted rather than
  baked into the image
