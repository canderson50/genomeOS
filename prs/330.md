## TL;DR

We investigated whether the same people may appear in more than one of five large Chinese genetic datasets. No pair can yet be confirmed as independent, so the datasets must not be pooled until the providers clarify participant overlap; PGG.Han is the largest unresolved risk.

---

PGG.Han, ChinaMAP, WBBC, NyuWa, and CMDB cannot safely be pooled while participant reuse is unknown: duplicate people can preserve a plausible allele-frequency estimate while making its uncertainty falsely narrow. This adds a primary-source audit of all five resources and all ten pairs, then keeps every pair in a fail-closed state until a custodian statement or permitted participant-level comparison establishes independence.

The audit identifies two concrete next steps. PGG.Han's 102,586 prior-GWAS controls lack a constituent-study inventory, making it the main unresolved overlap hub. ChinaMAP and NyuWa merit the first targeted pairwise query because NyuWa accession OEP002803 includes MARCH trial genomes while ChinaMAP draws from large metabolic cohorts; the document treats that as a question, not evidence of shared participants.

This advances #326. It does not close the issue because no pair has participant-disjoint evidence yet. No custodian questions have been sent.

Design context: this supports the observation/surface separation and P1 provenance requirements in design §4 and §7.1. It changes no schema, adapter, model, or serving path.

Expert review should assess:

- whether the constituent-cohort summaries are accurate and complete;
- whether `source_inventory_incomplete`, `no_named_overlap_found`, and `participant_independence_verified` are appropriate decision states;
- whether written custodian confirmation is sufficient for a given pair or genomic fingerprinting should be required;
- whether the MARCH/ChinaMAP follow-up question is appropriately scoped.
