## TL;DR

We found that the paper did publish its exact formula, so this PR now tests that formula directly instead of relying only on reconstructions. It gives the same bad answer as the alternatives: it raises the model's false background signal from about 1.00% to 1.19% and turns a 20% high-frequency signal into 14.40%. A costly full training run with this formula is therefore not justified.

---

The current HbS fit has a diffuse ~1% background and weak endemic peaks. Piel's published cubic and Stukel's generalized logistic link were plausible link-family explanations, but every tested arm moves that failure in the wrong direction before a spatial refit.

This revision corrects the source record. Web Appendix 1 page 15 publishes the exact cubic:

`-1.48556762*x^3 + 0.28125179*x^2 + 0.02261485*x + 0.02125477`

The relevant source-data branch is decreasing, so the implementation inverts only that branch and reverses the quantile orientation when aligning it to the current inverse-logit latent scale. The fitted source latents remain safely above the branch turning point, and inversion recovers the target logits to `4.4e-15` maximum absolute error. The appendix PDF SHA-256, formula, coefficients, branch bound, alignment direction, and source/output hashes are frozen in the receipt.

At the canonical inverse-logit background of 0.9961%, the exact published cubic gives 1.1919%. It maps inverse-logit operating points of 5%, 10%, 15%, and 20% to 5.379%, 9.071%, 11.961%, and 14.401%. The two monotone cubic reconstructions give 1.213% and 1.194% at background; quantile-aligned Stukel gives 1.205% and 1.188%. All five candidate arms therefore raise diffuse background and compress peaks.

The Stukel implementation matches published reference values and makes its identifiability boundary explicit. Every fitted HbS latent quantile is negative, so the negative-tail parameter is estimated while the positive-tail parameter is fixed at the inverse-logit value zero and reported as unfitted. Comparisons use affine empirical-quantile alignment; they do not apply separately fitted links at an incomparable raw latent number.

The pure modules and offline runner emit immutable aggregate curves, quantiles, coefficients, input/source hashes, and a replayable receipt. The committed figure labels the current inverse-logit, exact published cubic, reconstructed cubics, and aligned Stukel fits directly. The private 994-row count artifact is not committed; the public aggregate JSON, CSVs, and PNG reproduce from its recorded hash.

This advances #103 without closing it and implements Atlas design §§7–8. No production fitter, observation, surface, burden result, serving path, or environmental covariate changes.
