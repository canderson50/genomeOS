## What this changes, and why

§8's third golden test is the only one that exercises the §7.1 ascertainment correction, and its target did not exist. Task 34 in the plan was a one-line stub, and #47 carried six candidate sources listed from search results and marked unverified.

This adds the target: **six truth rows from five screening programmes**, each with the design in the paper's own words, the counts the paper prints, whether the count is F508del-specific, and whether the frequency is the paper's or divided from its counts. Plus a loader with closed vocabularies and 14 tests.

Three things in the data decide how §8 has to be scored, and a bare table would not have said them.

1. **A panel total is not a carrier frequency.** Victoria's 342 is a CFTR panel count and NE Italy's are panel counts too. A panel total always exceeds any single variant inside it: in a North American referral population F508del's share of detected carriers fell from 68.7% on a 32-mutation panel to 60.5% on a 69-mutation panel (doi:10.1038/gim.2013.188). `count_is_f508del_specific` marks which rows a single-variant estimate can be scored against, and `f508del_specific_rows()` is the parity column. Three of the six qualify.
2. **NE Italy is a route contrast, not two estimates of one frequency.** Same area, same surveillance years, 4.19% against 12.27%, a 2.93x gap, which the paper attributes to who was offered a test. The rows stay separate, because pooling them would average a contrast into a frequency.
3. **`frequency_source` records what was divided by whom.** Delhi, Brazil and Mendoza print a frequency. NE Italy and Victoria print counts, so their frequencies are computed here and marked `computed`. Anyone re-deriving the table from the abstracts will not find 4.19% printed anywhere, and that absence is not a transcription error.

**What this does not do.** No comparison, because there is no CF estimate to compare against yet. No full texts: all five papers are closed access and not in PMC, so every row is abstract-level and enters the literature-evidence contract as `automated_proposal` / `pending`, with no page locator. And no surface: [#180](https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/180) excludes CFTR/F508del from surface eligibility and [#3](https://github.com/genomeOS/genomeOS/issues/3) grades CFTR2 G1 for being patient-denominated, which is why the work belongs here rather than in a layer.

The design note is `docs/research/cf-carrier-screening-2026-10.md`, with the reading record, the arithmetic this PR performed, and what the table does not establish.

## One question for review

The closed `sampling_design` set in `docs/literature-evidence-curation.md` has no value meaning "unselected sample that is not a screening programme", and three rows are exactly that. They use `population_random` provisionally, with the paper's own words kept in `design_detail`. If the distinct concept matters, that is a schema change rather than a data one, and I would rather it be decided on #47 than assumed by a row.
