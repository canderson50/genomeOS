## Cytokines: counting, not Hardy-Weinberg

AFND reports these loci as diploid genotypes (`IL-6/ - 174 CC`, `CG`, `GG`), which is why
`afnd_frequencies` refuses them — there is no per-row allele count to read. But where all three
genotype classes are present, the allele frequency follows by counting:

```
p(B) = f(BB) + f(AB) / 2
```

**No assumption is involved.** HWE would be needed only to go the other way, or to recover a
frequency from an incomplete set. And 99% of locus × population pairs carry all three classes,
summing to 100.0% with a 5th–95th percentile of 99.9 to 100.1.

```
1178/1463 locus x population pairs retained (81%) — 22 loci across 106 populations
  refused    133  ascertainment_not_stated
  refused     66  population_not_placed
  refused     65  below_min_populations
  refused     11  genotype_percentages_do_not_sum_to_100
  refused     10  incomplete_genotype_set
```

Includes TNFα −308 (114 populations), IL-10 −1082 (99), IL-6 −174 (98). Pairs with a missing class
are refused rather than completed.

## KIR presence: a separate table, because the denominator is people

KIR genes are copy-number variable, so a study reports the fraction of individuals carrying the
gene *at all*. That is a carrier frequency over individuals, not an allele frequency over
chromosomes, and Hardy-Weinberg cannot bridge them — the diploid model does not apply to a locus
that may be present in zero, one or several copies.

`CARRIER_OBSERVATIONS_SCHEMA` mirrors the allele-frequency schema apart from
`carriers`/`n_individuals` replacing `ac`/`an`. **A separate table rather than a unit flag**, because
`ac`/`an` would otherwise mean two things in one column and any consumer averaging across rows
would be mixing chromosomes with people.

```
3305/3790 presence rows retained (87%) — 16 KIR genes across 238 populations
```

Carrier fraction median **0.850**, and the shape is right: framework genes (2DL4, 3DL2, 3DL3,
3DP1) sit near 1.0, variable genes (2DS1, 2DS3, 2DS5, 3DS1) much lower, as the KIR literature
expects. Allele-level rows (`3DL1*007`) are real allele frequencies and stay with
`afnd_frequencies`.

The same GP fits it: a binomial over individuals rather than over chromosomes.

## The unit hazard, now enforced

AFND's two frequency columns carry **different units in one file**:

| column | unit | corpus max |
|---|---|---|
| `alleles_over_2n` | fraction | 1.000 |
| `indivs_over_n` | percentage | 100.0 |

A 100× difference. Reading the wrong one without dividing gives 63 where 0.63 was meant, and no
downstream check would catch it because the schema only bounds `ac <= an`. Both adapters now
validate the range at parse time and **raise rather than rescale** (§12), with the percent
conversion happening exactly once where the column is read.

Contract gains `carrier_observations.schema.json`.

## What an expert should check

- Whether `p(B) = f(BB) + f(AB)/2` picking the *second homozygote alphabetically* as B is a
  sensible convention. It is deterministic and recorded in the `variant_id` (`cyt:il-6-174-g`), but
  it is not the minor allele, and a reader expecting MAF would be surprised.
- Whether the 1-point tolerance on the genotype sum is right. Measured spread is 99.9–100.1, so it
  is generous, but a locus with genuinely heavier rounding would be refused.
- Whether carrier observations should reach the surface engine at all yet, given no consumer
  exists. The schema and adapter are here; nothing fits them.
