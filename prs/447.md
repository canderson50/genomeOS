## Summary

The Atlas globe is now built on Cesium's `CesiumWidget` instead of `Viewer`. Viewer's widget layer drops out of the bundle on the critical path: the toolbar, info box, animation, timeline, geocoder and their CSS. Nothing visible changes.

Closes #446.

## What changed

- **New `website/src/atlas/scene/globe-widget.ts` (`createGlobeWidget`).** It builds the `CesiumWidget` inside the same `.cesium-viewer` frame that `Viewer` built, with the same options: `requestRenderMode`, `resolutionScale`, the credit container and render-loop error handling. `atlas-scene.ts`, `camera.ts`, `scene-policy.ts` and `context-controller.ts` now take a `CesiumWidget`.
- **Imports stay on `'cesium'`.** Once nothing imports `Viewer`, the bundler drops the widget layer by itself. Importing from `'@cesium/engine'` produced the same chunk, so no dependency was added.
- **Cesium's Widgets CSS and assets are no longer shipped.** The Atlas's own credit-row CSS stays.
- **Teardown removes the frame on both failure paths.** `destroy()` removes the frame even if `CesiumWidget.destroy()` throws. A throw from the constructor also removes it. Either way, "Retry globe" cannot leave two frames stacked.
- **§B.8 bundle checks:**
  - the Cesium chunk has no widget-layer markers;
  - the Cesium chunk's gzip size is at most 1,032,528 B × 1.01, and the test prints the measured size;
  - `/app/` ships no widget-layer CSS.
- **Invisible differences from `Viewer`.** Its empty toolbar div is gone, and so are its entity-pick click handlers. The info box and selection indicator were already off, and every imagery provider sets `enablePickFeatures: false`.
