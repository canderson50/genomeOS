## TL;DR

**What it does:** Adds the design for making the Atlas's map data follow Cesium World Terrain instead of sitting at fixed heights. Today the data is buried wherever the ground is higher than 650–1,800 m. This PR is the design only; no code yet.

**How:** With World Terrain on, the data worker draws the data surface, support masks, cell outlines and borders into map tiles, and Cesium lays those tiles over the terrain. They follow the ground everywhere, in every view mode and at any vertical exaggeration, and the hillshade falls on the data itself. **The smooth globe, the default, keeps today's rendering unchanged.**

**Status:** For the owner's review. Four shippable phases, about 4–5 engineer-weeks:
1. test harness, lighting and picking;
2. draped surface;
3. borders, observations and exaggeration;
4. contours and hardening.

The fixes that need no draping are in a separate PR (hillshade lighting, the relief control, a zoom floor). Advances #483.

## Decided by the owner

- Drape the data onto terrain, in four phases.
- A 1 s fade replaces the radial reveal wave under World Terrain.
- Data elevation is off under World Terrain.
- World Terrain may be desktop-only if phones miss the memory budget.
