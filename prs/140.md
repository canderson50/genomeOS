## Publishing

`publish_artifacts.py` knew two hardcoded layers, so **none of the AFND fits could be published**
and the store held only HbS and G6PD. It now takes `--afnd` plus `--afnd-populations` and publishes
every allele that has a fit on disk.

Which alleles those are is decided by whatever sweep produced `--fits`, not here, so this stays a
publishing step rather than quietly becoming a selection step.

**20 artifacts** published as `hla:<allele>__v1__afnd-2026-08`, 11,680 cells and ~1.09 MB each: the
17 from the locus-stratified sweep plus the 3 remaining alleles from the earlier screen.

## Two things the support counts show that the figures did not

- **`prior_dominated` is not universally inert.** DPB1\*04:02 has **314** such cells and DPB1\*04:01
  has **164**, against 8 for C\*07:02 and none for most others. The state works; it is rare, and
  unlike `unknown` it does not track the fitted range. That refines the stronger claim in #137 that
  the contraction threshold never fires.
- **`observed` cell counts split by locus.** DRB1 and DQB1 alleles get 142-166 observed cells while
  A, B and C get 73-97, at the same H3 resolution. A/B/C sampling is more spatially clustered, with
  several surveys landing in one cell, so **mask fractions are not directly comparable across
  loci** — worth knowing before reading the range-versus-mask correlation as a pure model effect.

## Publishing despite #137

#137 shows at least one of these fitted ranges (C\*03:03, 4,189 km) is inflated ~2.5-3x against its
empirical variogram. These are published anyway, because that is what
`(variant_id, model_version, data_version)` versioning is *for*: a nugget fix publishes `v2`
alongside rather than mutating `v1`, and nothing citing `v1` stops resolving. Withholding them
would treat immutability as a reason not to publish, which inverts its purpose.

## Inventory bug

`SUMMARISE_ONLY` named `afnd_cache` but not `afnd_freq_cache`, which `fetch_afnd_frequencies.py`
created *after* that constant was written. So **544 cached HTML pages, 178.6 MB of them**, were
hashed individually into `INVENTORY.json`, inflating both the file count and the reported store
size. An inventory of the store should describe the store, not the scraper's scratch space.

Fixed, and the same directory is added to `sync_store.EXCLUDED` so the two lists agree. Inventory
drops from **645 entries to 101**.

## Verification

`ruff check .`, `scripts/check_private_files.py` and `scripts/smoke.py` pass. All 22 artifacts are
confirmed present on the HF store.

## What an expert should check

- Whether publishing surfaces with a known-inflated range is the right call even under versioning.
  My reading is yes, but it is a judgement about what a published artifact implies.
- Whether `model_version` should encode something more specific than `v1` — right now a nugget fix
  would be `v2` with nothing in the id saying what changed, only the manifest.
