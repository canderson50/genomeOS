## Summary

The marketing site is rebuilt around figures instead of long text, in three passes on one branch:
round 1 (the visual-first redesign and its shared figure kit), the Contact page, and round 2 (the
owner's review of the round-1 preview: Home for a non-expert visitor, a Projects page for the
technical story, an animated Contribute page, a light Docs theme). Every figure is drawn with the
Atlas's own marks: white studs for measured surveys, hex cells for modeled estimates and a hatch
where there is no estimate. Every number on a data figure comes from pinned data with its
provenance, or is tagged "Example".

### Site-wide

- **Navigation.** Projects · Contribute · Docs · Contact, then GitHub, then the genomeOS Atlas
  pill, in the site header, the Menu on phones, the Docs header and the 404. The footer adds
  Contact beside Privacy and Cookie settings, and its copyright line reads "© 2026 genomeOS
  Foundation", "Foundation" in the wordmark's face (ruling 52).
- **Routes.** `/project/` became `/projects/`; the old path is a `noindex` redirect page that keeps
  every old fragment (`#vision` maps to `#status`). `/working-groups/` redirects to
  `/contribute/#working-groups`, and `#data`, `#modeling` and `#ui` go to each group's column. Both
  redirect pages are out of the sitemap.
- **"Docs"**, not "Technical docs", everywhere.
- **One figure kit** (round 1): the encoding tokens shared with the Atlas (its values unchanged,
  tested byte for byte), Figure, Plate, Legend, a canvas hex field and the Pipeline, plus committed
  figure data checked against `catalog.json` sha256s and built offline. The data host comes from the
  environment, never from the repository.
- **One astronaut kit** (round 2, R2-6): eleven flat-vector characters (eight contributors, three
  experts in lab coats; six skin tones, the deepest on a contributor and on the senior expert),
  thirty poses and their props, and a micro astronaut for people in data figures, where a closed
  visor means ancestry is never drawn as skin colour.
- **One animation engine** (R2-7): the Web Animations API through a small in-house timeline and
  scene controller (no GSAP, no Lottie). Scenes load as they approach, play once at 35% visible
  (the orbit, on a laptop, once its whole stage and the top of its text columns show),
  pause off screen and in a hidden tab, and build nothing under reduced motion, where each shows
  its end state with all its text. A scene's Pause and Replay appear when its story starts: until
  then nothing moves, and they wait unseen in their place. Typed text is real DOM text with an `aria-hidden` typing twin.
  In the e2e build only, `window.__motion` seeks any scene to any moment, so the tests check
  animation frame by frame.
- **Motion controls are glyphs** (rulings 22 and 35): white pause, play and replay icons, no text
  and no outline, 44 px targets, each named for what it does ("Pause the background video",
  "Pause the orbit animation", "Replay the pipeline animation").
- **No scare labels** (rulings 15, 37, 39 and 59): no "Not yet validated", no "Schematic" and no
  limitation disclaimers anywhere, the Atlas app and Docs included. Figures that draw modeled or
  sample data carry a short note and a link to the live Atlas. The honest science stays in the
  figures themselves: ranges, hatching where there is no estimate, provenance.
- **US English** on every marketing page (harmonize, hemoglobin, programs, behavior, color).
