## Scientific and product claim

Atlas users can inspect reviewed gnomAD evidence without conflating aggregate genetic-ancestry frequencies, regional constraint, or ClinVar assertions with the geographic inferred surface. Missing or unknown upstream fields remain explicit refusals.

## Implementation

- extends the versioned gnomAD cache contract to schema v2
- filters sex and total frequency strata, validates the ten documented v4 genetic-ancestry groups, and rejects unknown groups
- adds focused ancestry, 1 kb genomic-constraint, and ClinVar views inside the existing More info panel
- deduplicates ClinVar conditions while preserving classifications and submission counts
- keeps complete JSON download and provenance available
- adds a deterministic capture script and desktop/mobile interaction coverage

Implements Atlas design §11. Expert review should check the scientific wording around ancestry aggregation, non-coding regional constraint, and ClinVar assertions.
